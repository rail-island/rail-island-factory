# DR VT 18.16 "Delfin"

**Covers** - the Deutsche Reichsbahn's VT 18.16 diesel-hydraulic express unit (`veh-dr-vt1816`, in Czech "Delfin"): its references, dimensions, the `unit` parameter (the power car, the VMc, VMd and VMe intermediate cars, the full articulated train with its `cars`), how the bodies are lofted, the nose and the cockpit, the deviations and the tests.

**See also** - [foreign-units.md](foreign-units.md), [vehicles.md](vehicles.md), [parameters.md](parameters.md), [vehicle-rig.md](vehicle-rig.md), [lod.md](lod.md), [the source notes](../../assets/dr-vt1816/SOURCES.md)

## The design

| id | title | parameter | values (default first) | triangles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `veh-dr-vt1816` | DR VT 18.16 Delfin | `unit` | `powerCar`, `dining`, `firstClass`, `trailer`, `train` | about 9,600 a power car, 5,400 to 5,700 an intermediate car |
| | | `cars` (with `train` only) | `4` (default), `2` .. `8` | the train's cars added up: 19,200 (2) to 52,200 (8) |

The VT 18.16 (from 1970 class 175, the "SVT Gorlitz") was built 1963-68 by
VEB Waggonbau Gorlitz for the international express trains Berlin - Prague -
Vienna ("Vindobona") and Berlin - Copenhagen. A four-car train is power car,
VMd, VMc and the second power car turned: 98.06 m over the couplers.

- `powerCar` - the end car: the long rounded nose with the cream hood and the red
  sweep, the raised red cockpit, the engine room with four portholes and the roof
  louvres, the service compartment's double door, the entrance and the second
  class saloon's six windows. The nose is at +X; the inner end carries the
  gangway and a centre coupler.
- `dining` - the VMc dining car (the drawing's upper middle car): three second
  class compartments, the dining room's seven windows at 1.74 m, the kitchen's
  small window (right side only) and a ventilation box on its roof.
- `firstClass` - the VMd first and second class compartment car (the drawing's
  lower middle car): nine compartment windows at about 2.02 m.
- `trailer` - the VMe second class "unit middle car" added for five- and six-car
  trains: ten windows at 1.95 m. (The first version's only `trailer` keeps its
  value.)
- `train` - the whole train as one articulated vehicle of `cars` cars: a power
  car at each end (the rear one turned), the VMd behind the leading power car,
  the VMc ahead of the trailing one and VMe cars between them; a three-car train
  has the VMc alone, a two-car train is the two power cars.

The VMc and the VMd have an entrance with doors on both sides behind one
gangway and a single door on one side at the other end; the VMe's two doors are
diagonally opposite (at each coupling one entrance a side). Which side carries the
single door was not documented; the model puts it on the left (-Z), which the
drawing's lower car shows. The real sets ran with four to six cars; seven and
eight are the game's.

Each car is centred between its coupling planes (the nose coupler's face and
the planes halfway between two bodies), so the game can couple them in any order.
The catalog fields are `DMU_CATALOG` (rail vehicles, group `unit`, diesel).

## Construction

The bodies are not built by the coach builder: `drVt1816.ts` lofts **vertical
slices** along the car (`halfSlice`, `loftHalves`). Each slice is the side's
bottom with a small tuck, the vertical side painted in the stripes (grey skirt,
black band, white, blue and white lines, red, cream) and a quarter ellipse from
the shoulder S to the crown C. Every quantity is a function of d, the distance
back from the +X end: the half width (the nose's elliptical plan, a flat front
0.5 m wide rounding into the sides over 1.4 m), the bottom (the nose's chin, the
skirts, the openings over the bogies, the trailer's apron), S and C (the hood
rising from 2.3 m at the nose over the windscreen's foot to the 4.20 m roof
11.1 m back), and the top of the red, which rises from 1.95 m on the sides to
2.20 m at the nose. Because the paint edges are vertices of every slice, they
stay exact on the curved nose.

- **Nose** - the lamps (`noseDisc`), the winged DR emblem and the running number
  (`noseDecal`) are laid on the lofted surface itself (`nosePoint` finds the
  slice segment of a lateral position). The nose coupler hangs at 1.03 m.
- **Cockpit** - a second loft of trapezoid slices, sunk 0.1 m into the hood
  (`hoodY`): the leaning windscreen between its first two slices, the corner
  panes, side windows, the roof lamp, and a red cap tapering into the roof
  11.1 m back.
- **Sides** - doors with their leaves painted in the body's bands, windows with
  the top light's bar, the portholes, grab handrails and steps; louvres laid on
  the roof's shoulder over the engine room; the running number `VT 18.16.07`
  on the power car's red, "DR" on the trailer.
- **Running gear** - `unitBogie` (unitParts.ts) for all four bogies; the power
  car's motor bogie adds the engine and the transmission inside its node.

## The full train

`train(n)` lays the cars out front to back (`vt1816Train`), the train centred on
x = 0, and draws each car into one builder as a section (`articulate` from the
tram kit): `section<k>` front first, hinged at the coupling plane ahead of it at
the coupler's height (1.03 m), one bellows joint over the 0.70 m between two
bodies, a 12 degree bend. Unlike the TGV's Jacobs bogies, every car keeps its own
two bogies, so the rig has two supports per section and the game places each car
on the track by its own bogies; the joint only skins the gap. The trailing power
car is drawn facing +X about its centre, then turned (`MeshCore.turned`). Inside
the train the cars leave out their own gangway frames (the joint is the bellows)
and keep their end doors and couplers.

## Distant model

`vt1816Lod` builds a `UnitBody` for `fitUnit` (lodUnits.ts): the section, the
paint as skirt, one dark band for the stripes, red and cream; the power car's
nose as the end's centre line rising into the hood, the cockpit as a red roof
box with the windscreen in LOD glass; the window band; the bogies. The train
keeps the full model's sections and joints, its inner ends hidden under the
bellows, within 100 triangles a car and 300 at most (`lodTriangles: 300`); in a
seven- or eight-car train the intermediate cars' bogies are given up first.

## Game properties

Power car 66 t, 736 kW; VMc 42.4 t, VMd and VMe 40 t, no power; 160 km/h. The
set's published 214.4 t empty is split by estimate. The train adds its cars up
(132 t for two power cars, 214.4 t for four), 1,472 kW, 160 km/h up to five cars
and 140 km/h from six (the published limit of the six-car trains).

## Deviations

- The bogies are the shared modern two-axle bogie (coil and air springs), not
  the Gorlitz bogies of the 1960s; the transmission's cardan shafts are left out.
- The gangways are plain bellows frames; the end doors, the air hoses and the
  wipers are simplified or left out. The trailers' interior variants (the
  restaurant and first-class cars) share one layout.
- The intermediate cars carry no running number and no class numerals (see
  SOURCES.md); their windows on the corridor side repeat the drawn side's.
