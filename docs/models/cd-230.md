# CD 230 / 240 / 340 (laminatka)

**Covers** - the parametric `veh-cd-230` design: the Skoda 47E glass-fibre locomotives CD 230, 240, 240 047 and 340 and their steel sister CD 242 as one `livery` parameter, how the rounded nose is built and how the face is laid on it, the livery model, the native comparison, deviations and tests.

**See also** - [vehicles.md](vehicles.md), [loco-liveries.md](loco-liveries.md), [parameters.md](parameters.md), [source notes](../../assets/cd-230/SOURCES.md), [measured comparison](../../assets/cd-230/COMPARISON.md), [the vlakosimdb-transfer skill](../../.agents/skills/vlakosimdb-transfer/SKILL.md)

## The design

| `livery` | vlakosimdb model | number | paint |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cd230` (default) | `cd_230` | 230 025-9 | red, yellow bands, silver lip |
| `cd240` | `cd_240` | 240 048-9 | cream over red, yellow and orange lip band |
| `cd240No047` | `cd_240_047` | 240 047-1 | red, cream bands, red and white stripes |
| `cd340` | `cd_340` | 340 062-9 | blue, cream bands, red and white stripes |
| `cd242` | `cd_242` | 242 214-5 | cream over red, yellow nose band |

One rigid Bo'Bo' locomotive, 16.44 m over the buffers, 15.20 m body, 3.0 m wide,
7.80 m between the pivots, 2.80 m bogie wheelbase, 1.25 m wheels; 4.55 m with
both collectors down, 5.87 m with the rear (-X) diamond pantograph raised and
the front one folded. About 15,600 to 15,900 triangles. The four laminatka vlakosimdb
models share every clip profile; the 240 family adds a roof box and moves the
main breaker (`roofBox`). The CD 242 (`cd_242`, a plain box in vlakosimdb) is the
240's steel-bodied sister of the same outline: its livery takes the paint from
its BMPs (the side one 157 x 57 px on the 17 x 6 m canvas) and keeps the 240
family's roof.

## The body

`shellRing(y)` gives the body's plan at height y: two noses joined by straight
sides. The noses follow the XML plan (0.1, 0.2 and 0.4 m back at |z| = 1.0,
1.3, 1.5) as a superelliptic quarter that meets the sides tangentially; the
tip and half width at each height come from the `TIP` and `HALF` tables (the
lower face leaning out, the 0.1 m lip, the raked windscreen, the hood rolling
into the roof, the tumblehome). The shell is lofted between consecutive heights
of those tables and every band edge, and painted per edge: `face` bands on the
noses, `cab` bands on the side walls from `CAB_X` = 6.3 m to the noses, `side`
bands in between. Each painted strip is one leaf, `body/shell/<region>/band-<i>`.

### The face

The face is the reason this model exists, so its details are laid on the curved
skin rather than on a flat plane. `onNose(e, s, y, d)` returns the point `d` off
the skin at arc length `s` from the nose's centre line, at height y; `nosePanel`
builds a panel from an (s, y) outline, its face cut into column strips and its
walls round the edge. Two rules keep panels on the skin:

- Positions around the nose are arc lengths at 3.0 m scaled by the nose's arc
  at every other height, and the shell samples the same fractions of the curve,
  so the panel's columns and the shell's ruled lines join the same points.
- Panel faces split at the profile's kinks (`PROFILE_KINKS`), so a 1 m tall pane
  does not cut through the skin where it leans in at 3.4 m.

Without them the grey skin showed through the wrap-round glass near the
corners; `test/cd230.test.ts` guards it with rays from ahead, the three-quarter
view and the side.

Laid on the face: the wrap-round windscreen (two panes running 0.1 m past the
nose onto the side, rubber, glass, parked wipers, the painted centre pillar),
the round headlight housing on the hood with its ridge tapering into the roof
(twin lenses on the 230, one lens on the others), two vents with rims over the
glass, the oval grilles on the lip's corners, two lamp housings with steps under
them, the number plate, buffers with rectangular heads, the coupling and the
perforated plough.

## Liveries

`Cd230Livery` holds `side`, `cab` and `face` bands (the `face` helper adds the
grey windscreen surround from 2.5 to 3.62 m and the hood in the upper body
colour up to the roof at 3.8 m), the frame and plough colours, an optional
hazard strip on the plough, the pillar and vent-rim colours, the headlamp kind
and `roofBox`. Bands were read row by row from each model's `x_both` and
`z_both` BMPs (row r spans 6.0 - (r + 1) / 10 to 6.0 - r / 10 m), as in
[loco-liveries.md](loco-liveries.md).

## Comparison

`cd230Comparison()` projects the actual GLB vertices of named scopes onto the
native canvases: both noses against `x_both`, both sides against `z_both`, 77
boxes in `assets/cd-230/native/landmarks.json`. `pnpm exec tsx
generator/cd230Report.ts` (after `pnpm build:models`) writes
`assets/cd-230/COMPARISON.md` and annotated views under `export/review/cd-230/`.
Mean absolute error went from 0.576 pp (the initial model, `baseline.json`) to
0.391 pp; the remaining larger rows are the deliberate departures below.

## Deviations

- Hood colour and headlight housing from the photographs (the BMP paints the
  hood grey and the housing as a 0.4 m square).
- The standard 1.75 m buffer spacing; the plough and the breaker after the XML
  clips rather than the paint.
- Running gear from the published dimensions; diamond pantographs with one arm
  length in both poses (the source stretches its folded one to 4.4 m).
- Not modelled: the small dark mark under the 240 family's pillar, the 230's
  door transom strip, emblems (the native textures carry none).

## Tests

`test/cd230.test.ts`: every landmark within its stated pixel tolerance; length
over buffers, axle positions, wheel contact and gauge; the 3.1 m by 4.7 m
envelope and 6.5 m operating height in every livery; one raised and one folded
pantograph with level collectors and equal arms; glass, not skin, across both
windscreens at both ends; valid running numbers; the triangle budget and the
roof box per livery. `test/params.test.ts` and `test/models.test.ts` cover the
design with the other parametric models (determinism, flicker, envelope, the JS
model).
