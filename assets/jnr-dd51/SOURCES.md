## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "JNR Class DD51" and ja.wikipedia "国鉄DD51形ディーゼル機関車": B-2-B (Bo-2-Bo), 18,000 mm over couplers, 2,950.6 mm wide, 3,800 mm roof (3,956 mm over the signal flare tube), 860 mm wheels, DT113 powered bogies of 2,200 mm wheelbase, the idle TR106 of 1,600 mm, 84 t, 1,640 kW (2,200 ps), 95 km/h, 649 built 1962 - 1978; the Sorachi depot units repainted for the Hokutosei in blue with a gold band ("青地に金帯").
- **Not published, estimated from the photographs** - the hood top 3.0 m (1801 and 849 disagree: the cab stands about half the hood height above it, which puts it near 3.0 m under the 3.8 m roof, while the end face's aspect alone would say 3.3 m; the model takes 3.0 m and a slightly narrower hood, 2.16 m, to keep the face's proportion), the cab 3.0 m long, the hood ends 0.65 m inside the end beams, the radiator sections 1.55 m long.
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
