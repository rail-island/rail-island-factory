## Model photograph (not stored)

A side view of the LGB garden-railway model of FO 101 (a maker's catalogue picture, supplied by the user in chat)
gave the proportions; its rights lie with the maker, so the picture is not kept here. Measured on it at
46.5 px/m (14.776 m over the couplers): the bogie pivots 7.26 m apart, the bogie wheelbase 2.96 m
(Wikipedia: 2,980 mm), the body side 0.99 - 3.10 m above the rail, the cab front leaning back about
0.35 m from 1.6 m to the roof, the cab side window 5.2 - 6.2 m from the centre (2.15 - 2.9 m high),
the door 3.8 - 4.5 m, the FO logo 0.1 - 3.4 m on the half away from the door, and seven logo bars
between the side ribs.
