## Published figures

Redoutey, *Le materiel moteur de la SNCF*, 2007): 28.90 m over the couplers, 2.90 m wide, 3.70 m high, bogie centres 17.50 m, bogie wheelbase 2.10 m, wheels 840 mm, 50 t in service, 2 x 257 kW (514 kW) MAN engines under the cabs, 140 km/h, 61 seats and 21 folding seats, two single-leaf 1.30 m doors a side, a low-floor middle section.
