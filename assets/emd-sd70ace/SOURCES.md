## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - The Diesel Shop data sheet "EMD SD70ACe" and Progress Rail's SD70ACe/LCi sheet: 74 ft 3 in (22.63 m), 15 ft 11 in, cab 10 ft 3 in wide, truck centres 47 ft 10 in, truck wheelbase 13 ft 7 in, 42 in wheels, 408,000 lb, 4,300 hp, 70 mph.
- **Running numbers** - illustrative numbers in each railroad's SD70ACe series (UP 8500, NS 1000, BNSF 9200).
- **Photo against the published figures** - fitting the NS 1143 photo with both end sills as extra
  points gives truck centres of only 13.0 - 13.6 m; the published 47 ft 10 in is kept (the data sheet
  rules) and the photo is used for positions between the trucks only.
- **Paint** - the railroads' schemes as colour fields and stripes measured on the photos: UP's red
  pinstripe at 3.76 m with grey above and a simplified winged shield (no lettering in it), NS's nose
  stripes and NS speed lines (no horse), BNSF Heritage III's pinstripes at 3.5 m swooping down behind
  the cab to the walkway and BNSF across the nose; the railroad's name in plain letters.
