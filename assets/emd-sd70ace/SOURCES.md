## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - The Diesel Shop data sheet "EMD SD70ACe" and Progress Rail's SD70ACe/LCi sheet: 74 ft 3 in (22.63 m), 15 ft 11 in, cab 10 ft 3 in wide, truck centres 47 ft 10 in, truck wheelbase 13 ft 7 in, 42 in wheels, 408,000 lb, 4,300 hp, 70 mph.
- **Running numbers** - illustrative numbers in each railroad's SD70ACe series (UP 8500, NS 1000, BNSF 9200).
- **Paint** - the railroads' schemes simplified to colour fields, stripes and the
  railroad's name in plain letters; no heralds or logos are drawn.
