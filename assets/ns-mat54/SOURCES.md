## Published figures

- **Lengths, masses, power** - de.wikipedia, "NS-Baureihe Mat '54": 51.12 m over the couplers (ElD-2), 99.26 m (ElD-4), giving 25.56 m end cars and 24.07 m middle cars; 110 t / 210 t; 680 kW / 1360 kW; Bo'2' + 2'Bo' and Bo'Bo' + 2'2' + 2'2' + Bo'Bo'; Scharfenberg couplers; 140 km/h.
- **Liveries and numbers** - nl.wikipedia, "Mat '54": the original green with the red line, later the yellow moustache; yellow from 1968 (359 first, the last green unit repainted in 1980); the museum units 386 (ElD-2) and 766 (ElD-4) in green. The model's numbers: 766 (green), 786 (yellow).
