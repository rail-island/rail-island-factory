## Published figures

de.wikipedia "OBB 1042": 16.22 m over the buffers, 83.5 t (1142), 4,000 kW one-hour rating, 150 km/h,
Bo'Bo', 15 kV 16.7 Hz, 258 built 1963-1977 by SGP (1042.531 - 707 rebuilt as 1142). The 2.98 m width,
the 7.7 m bogie centres, 2.95 m wheelbase and 1.25 m wheels are estimates from the photographs.
