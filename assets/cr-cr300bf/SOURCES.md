## Published figures

Wikipedia (en, zh "CR300BF"): 8 cars 4M4T (motor cars 2, 4, 5, 7), about 209 m, 250 km/h (300 km/h design),
613 seats (48 first, 565 second class), pantographs on cars 3 and 6, in service since 24 December 2020.
Car lengths, width, bogies and wheels as the CR400BF's shell (assets/cr-cr400bf). Weight (48 / 46 t a car) and
power (5,440 kW, 16 YJ-305A motors) are estimates: no published figure was found.
