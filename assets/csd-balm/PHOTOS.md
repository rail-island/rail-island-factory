# CSD Balm - reference photos

Prototype: CSD Balm (later Bix, CD class 020; 68 modernised in the 1990s as class 021 Btx), trailer car to the M 240.0 (CD 820) railcars, built by Vagonka Tatra Studenka from the early 1960s; inward-folding doors and open end gangways with scissor gates. Source: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%8CSD-Baureihe_M_240.0 . Exact build years of the trailers are not in that source (uncertain).

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and, for BY-SA, share-alike.
