## Figures

English Wikipedia, "China Railways HXN5" (infobox): 22,295 mm long, 3,119 mm wide, 4,775 mm high, 150 t,
Co-Co, GEVO-16 of 6,250 hp (4,660 kW), 120 km/h. Truck centres (50 ft 4 in) and axle spacing (6 ft 7 in)
are the GE Evolution's (the ES44AC, the same 22.3 m frame); the side photograph's fit agrees with them.
