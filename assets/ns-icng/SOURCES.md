## Published figures

- **nl.wikipedia, [Intercity Nieuwe Generatie](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercity_Nieuwe_Generatie)** (infobox, ERATV): 110 m over five cars, 164.9 m over eight; Bo'(2)'(2)'Bo'+2'(2)'Bo' and Bo'(2)'(2)'(2)'Bo'+Bo'(2)'(2)'(2)'Bo' (Jacobs bogies, one coupled joint with two bogies); 198.7 / 285.7 t empty; 2,545 / 3,390 kW; 200 km/h; single swing-plug doors 1,600 mm wide, 6 / 9 a side; floor 810 mm at the doors; the domestic scheme (yellow, the blue window band rising and fading to black over the cab windows, yellow doors).
- **Car lengths** - a Dutch Coradia Stream overview (mjs.home.xs4all.nl, "Alstom Coradia Stream, Max"): cars between Jacobs bogies 18.3 m, a car with its own bogie at one end 21.4 m; with the unit lengths above the end cars come to 24.45 m.
