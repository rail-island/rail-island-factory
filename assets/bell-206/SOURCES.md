## Published figures

- Bell 206 JetRanger (Wikipedia "Bell 206"): in service from 1967; fuselage length 9.5 m, main rotor diameter 10.16 m (2 blades), height 2.91 m to the rotor head; about 4 seats (typical layout, approx.); maximum take-off mass about 1.45 t; about 313 kW shaft power; cruising speed about 216 km/h.
- Positions of the wing, engines, tail, gear legs, doors and windows are read off the photographs and published three-view drawings (approx.); the livery colours are approximations of the carriers' schemes.
- Rate of climb about 6.9 m/s (1,350 ft/min, 206B-2; GlobalAir JetRanger 206B-2 specifications, https://www.globalair.com/aircraft-specifications/bell/jetranger-206b-2-specifications/265); the game's transit height (150 m) is a game choice, not a published figure.
