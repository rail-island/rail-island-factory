# Faccs hopper wagon - reference photos

Prototype: the CD Faccs (ex CSD Sas) self-discharging ballast hopper, 12.8 m over buffers, end platforms and discharge chutes under the hopper. Confidence: high.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (thumbnail of 1280 px, the longer side at most 1600 px), saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and, for BY-SA, share-alike.
