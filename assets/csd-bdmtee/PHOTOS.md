# CSD Bdmtee - reference photos

Prototype: CD Bdmtee281 (in the 1990s Bymee), 2nd class open coach, UIC-Z body 26.4 m, GP 200 S disc-brake bogies, 160 km/h, entrance doors at about 1/4 and 3/4 of the length, built by VEB Waggonbau Bautzen 1989-1990 for CSD (Bdmtee275 is a partial rebuild with a staff compartment). Source: https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C5%AFz_Bdmtee281,_275_%C4%8CD . Confidence: high. Not Halberstadt-built. All free photos found show the later CD green/white livery, none the all-green delivery livery.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and, for BY-SA, share-alike.
