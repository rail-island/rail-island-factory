## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - de.wikipedia "Stadler Regio-Shuttle RS 1": 25.5 m over buffers, body 24.46 m (drawn 24.2 m from tip to tip, the kit's buffers 0.65 m out), 2.9 m wide, 3.7 m high, low floor 0.6 m (65 %), raised floor 1.0 m over the bogies.
- **Running gear** - B'B', bogie centres 17.1 m, bogie wheelbase 1.8 m (drawn 2.2 m: the series kit's bogie needs at least 2.05 m, and 2.05 m puts its springs on the frame), wheels 770 mm new.
- **Performance** - 2 x 257 kW (MAN), 120 km/h, 40 t empty; screw coupling with side buffers on DB's and ODEG's units (Scharfenberg on some others).
- **Gauge** - standard gauge, UIC loading gauge.
