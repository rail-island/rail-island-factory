## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "500 Series Shinkansen": end car 27.0 m, intermediate 25.0 m, 3.38 m wide, 3.69 m high, 300 km/h, 1997 - 2010 on the Nozomi, then the 8-car Kodama sets.
- **Measured** (`photo-500kei-himeji.jpg`, d behind the tip) - tip 0.79 m high; top 1.29 m at 1 m, 1.64 m at 2 m; canopy foot 2.75 m (1.92 m high), glass to about 6.7 m, hood to 8.6 m; roof 3.43 m at 9 m, 3.52 m at 10 m, full 3.69 m about 14 m back; door 9.8 - 10.45 m, sill 1.31 m, head 3.0 m; window band from 10.86 m, windows 0.69 m wide, 1.01 m apart, 2.2 - 2.73 m; dark band 2.14 - 2.77 m, blue band 1.83 - 2.14 m; leading bogie's centre 5.4 m; fairings 3.25 - 7.5 m, 0.33 - 0.65 m high; skirt down to 0.3 m.
- **Width** - narrowed to 3.24 - 3.28 m (the Shinkansen gauge's 3.3 m; the E6 keeps its own).
