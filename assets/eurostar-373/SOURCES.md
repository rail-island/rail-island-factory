# eurostar-373 references

**Covers** - provenance of the `eurostar-373` model's references: photographs and published figures.
**Code** - see the design that names `references: "eurostar-373"`
**See also** - [docs/models/world-fleet.md](../../docs/models/world-fleet.md)

## Published figures

Power car 22.15 m, end trailers 21.845 m, intermediate trailers 18.70 m, 2.81 m wide; 920 mm motor and 910 mm carrying wheels; two pantographs a power car (Faiveley GPU for 25 kV, Brecknell Willis for DC); the end trailers' outer bogie motored (fr.wikipedia "TGV TMST"). Bogie centres 14.0 m (TGV power car), its front bogie about 5.1 m behind the nose (photographs). The model shows 3104 (original) and 3211 (blue).
