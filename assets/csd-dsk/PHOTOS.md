# CSD Dsk - reference photos

Prototype: not identified with certainty. The model is a two-axle wooden luggage van (CSD D-series) with a lookout and an end platform, probably a pre-war (possibly ex-kkStB/Austrian) design. No Commons category or article for a CSD 'Dsk' was found. The photo below is a similar preserved two-axle wooden CSD luggage van at the Luzna u Rakovnika museum; its class lettering is not legible at this size (uncertain). The CSD Ds/Da categories on Commons are steel brake vans and the later bogie Da, i.e. different types.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and, for BY-SA, share-alike.
