Published figures (Wikipedia, "TGV Duplex"): a train of two power cars and eight
trailers, 200.19 m, 2.904 m wide (trailers 2.896 m), 4.32 m high; 380 t; 8,800
kW under 25 kV; 320 km/h. The power car's 22.15 m over the couplers and 14 m
bogie centres, the end trailer's 21.845 m and the intermediate trailers' 18.70 m
are those of the TGV Réseau family (as `assets/tgv/`); they add up to the
published 200.19 m. The power car's 68 t is the published figure of the TGV
Réseau power car; the trailers' split of the remaining 244 t (33 t an end
trailer, 30.5 t the bar car, 29.5 t an intermediate trailer) is an estimate.

The trainset number `249` is the photographed set's. The original 1996 livery
(silver with dark blue window bands and the DUPLEX lettering) and the Ouigo
trailers are drawn from memory of photographs; no free image of either was
downloaded (Wikimedia Commons rate-limited the downloads), so their colours and
paint lines are approximate.
