# MAV MDmot - reference photos

One parametric model, `veh-mav-mdmot`: `unit` motorCar (vlakosimdb mav_mdmot, the default), trailerAx (mav_ax), trailerBx (mav_bx), drivingTrailer (mav_btx).

Prototype: Ganz-MAVAG diesel-hydraulic multiple unit of the Hungarian State Railways, delivered 1970-1976 as class MDa, later MDmot: a twin-cab baggage motor car (15.52 m over buffers, 2.85 m wide, 8.05 m bogie pivots, 2.4 m wheelbase, 920 mm wheels), first- and second-class trailers and a driving trailer; a four-car set is 82.38 m, a six-car set 127.18 m (de.wikipedia "MAV-Baureihe MDmot").

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (rendered by `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title.
