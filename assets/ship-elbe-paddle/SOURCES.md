## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "Sächsische Dampfschiffahrt" and the photographs: an Elbe paddle steamer of the Saxon fleet, built 1926 (approx.): 68.7 m long, 12.9 m over the paddle boxes (6.6 m hull), 0.9 m draught, 600 passengers, 300 kW, 20 km/h.
- From German Wikipedia "Dresden (Schiff, 1926)": 68.70 m long, 6.90 m beam (12.90 m over the paddle boxes), 2.23 m depth, 1.13 m draught (0.80 m light), 300 PS compound engine, two side wheels of 3.20 m diameter with nine feathering floats, 628 passengers. The model takes 6.9 m beam, 2.3 m depth, 1.0 m draught, 12.9 m over the casings.
- Measured off `photo-dresden-1926.jpg` (side-on, bow left, about 16 px/m along the hull, approx.): paddle casing 10 m long, its shaft at 0.67 L from the stern (u 46 m); funnel 3.9 m aft of the shaft; folding mast just forward of the casing (u 51.5 m); forward saloon from the casing to about 6 m from the bow; sun deck over the after saloon from the casing to about 5 m from the stern, its awning from the funnel back to u 16 m; sun deck about 3.4 m and funnel top about 9.6 m above the water.
- From `photo-schiff-dresden-in-pillnitz-2010-09-15.jpg`: the casing is a flat closed face with round lights each side of the arched name board, its foot at main-deck height on a green strake, the sponson's underside sweeping down aft of the wheel; the varnished wheelhouse stands on the sun deck ahead of the funnel; the funnel is white with a black top and two green bands.
