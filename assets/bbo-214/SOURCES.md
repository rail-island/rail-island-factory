## Published figures

From de.wikipedia "BBÖ 214" (and en.wikipedia "BBÖ 214"): 1'D2' h2, 22,580 mm over the buffers
with the tender, 3,150 mm wide, 4,650 mm high, drivers 1,940 mm, carrying wheels 1,034 mm,
cylinders 650 x 720 mm, 15 bar, 118 / 123.5 t in service (engine), tender 60 t with 29.5 m3 of
water and 8 t of coal, 2,162 kW indicated, 110 / 120 km/h (155 km/h on a test run), main rods
4.25 m long. The wheelbase and the engine/tender split are measured on the 2007 side photograph.
