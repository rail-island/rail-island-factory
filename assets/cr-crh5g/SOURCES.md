The modelled sets are the twelve enhanced CRH5G (5218 - 5229, 2017) with the new nose and the dark
window band; the earlier CRH5G (5141 - 5217) kept the CRH5A's nose, plain white with the blue stripe
(photo-crh5g-5160). No true side-on photograph of an enhanced set was available: the side lengths come
from the projective fit on photo-crh5g-5227 and the published car lengths, the heights from the blue
stripe and the band on photo-crh5g-5228.

## Published figures

From the Chinese Wikipedia article "和谐号CRH5型电力动车组" (infobox and formation tables): CRH5G built
2014 - 2016 (the enhanced sets 2017), 84 sets; eight cars Mc M Tp M T Tp M Mc, the pantographs on the two
Tp trailers; end car 27,600 mm, intermediate cars 25,000 mm, width 3,200 mm, height 4,270 mm; 613 seats
(48 / 90 / 90 / 77 / 63 / 90 / 90 / 65); 550 kW motors, two in each of the five motor cars (5,500 kW);
250 km/h; CA250 bogies. The weight is taken as the CRH5A's 451 t plus the cold and sand equipment
(about 460 t; no published figure found).
