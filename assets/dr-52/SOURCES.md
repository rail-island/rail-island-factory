# DR 52 source references

**Covers** - provenance of the DR 52 model (`proc-vehicle-dr-52`): photographs and published figures.
**Code** - `generator/deSteam52.ts`, `generator/worldSteamLocos.ts`
**See also** - [the reference](../../docs/models/germany-trains.md), [world-steam.md](../../docs/models/world-steam.md)

The model is a DR **52.80** (the Reko rebuild: 50E boiler, IfS/DR mixing preheater, plate frames,
oval cab front windows, curved smoke deflectors on the smokebox) with the 2'2' T 30 tub tender, as
52 8154 runs today. 52 1360 (an unrebuilt DR engine with a box tender) and the tender of 52 4867 (DB
museum engine) are sister vehicles, used only for what they share.

## Published figures

- de.wikipedia "DR-Baureihe 52.80" and "DR-Baureihe 52": 1'E h2, drivers 1,400 mm, pony truck 850 mm,
  22,975 mm over buffers, 19,000 mm wheelbase with the tender, cylinders 600 mm, boiler type 50E, tender
  2'2' T 30 (30 m3 water, 10 t coal), only plate-frame engines rebuilt.
- Coupled axles 1,650 mm apart; the pony truck 2.5 m ahead of the first driver and the tender trucks
  (1.9 m wheelbase, 4.0 m apart) measured from 52 8154's side-on photograph.
- Power, top speed and first year as the design states them (`generator/worldSteamLocos.ts`,
  `generator/openttdTable.ts`).
