## Published figures

- Trieste Opicina Tram (Wikipedia "Opicina Tramway" and the operators' published data): in service from 1935; 11 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.2 m wide, 3.3 m to the roof (approx.); 90 passengers with standing room (approx.); 40 km/h, 160 kW, 16 t (approx.); track gauge 1000 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
