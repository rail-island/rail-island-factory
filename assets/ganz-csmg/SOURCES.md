## Published figures

- Ganz CSMG (Wikipedia "Ganz CSMG" and the operators' published data): in service from 1967; 26.9 m long over the bodies in 2 sections, 2.35 m wide, 3.2 m to the roof (approx.); 170 passengers with standing room (approx.); 60 km/h, 260 kW, 29 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
