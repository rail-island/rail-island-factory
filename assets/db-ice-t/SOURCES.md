## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - de.wikipedia "DB-Baureihe 411" (after ETR 1999): end car 27,450 mm, middle car 25,900 mm, up to
  2,850 mm wide and 3,910 mm high, floor 1,250 mm, bogie centres 19,000 mm, bogie wheelbase 2,700 mm; seven cars
  184.4 m; tilting up to 8 degrees, 230 km/h, from 1999.
- **Formation** - 2'2' + (1A)(A1) + (1A)(A1) + 2'2' + (1A)(A1) + (1A)(A1) + 2'2': the end cars are unpowered
  "transformer cars" carrying the pantograph at their inner ("short coupling") end over the rear bogie; one
  pantograph raised feeds the train through a roof line. Doors about a third along each car ("Dritteleinstieg").
- **Running numbers** - 411 001 (DB), 4011 090 (OBB, photographed).
- **Gauge** - standard gauge, UIC loading gauge.
