## Notes

- Running number shown: CRH2A-2377 (photo-crh2a-2377-at-motan-20190420133135.jpg).
- Published figures (they override the photos): end car 25.7 m and intermediate car 25.0 m over the couplers,
  body 3.38 m wide (drawn 3.27 m in the Shinkansen gauge), 3.7 m high, bogie centres 17.5 m, wheelbase 2.5 m,
  wheels 860 mm; the E2-1000 nose about 9 m; 250 km/h; pantographs on the fourth and sixth of eight cars.
- Every photograph is oblique: lengths along the side come from the projective fit through the car joints
  (about +-0.5 m near the nose), heights from the head-on views scaled on the oval cover.
