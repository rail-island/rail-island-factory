## Published figures

- **Line** - Roosevelt Island Tramway, New York (Wikipedia "Roosevelt Island Tramway"): opened in 1976 over the East River, rebuilt by Poma in 2010 with two new cabins of 110 passengers each, about 8 m/s (17.9 mph).
- **Cabin** - red with a grey roof band and the line's name on its side, under an A-frame hanger; 6.8 x 3.4 m in plan and 2.95 m to the roof (approx., from the photographs).
