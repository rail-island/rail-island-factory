## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "E235 series" (en, ja): 20.0 m over the coupling planes, bodies 19.57 m (end cars) and 19.5 m (middle cars), 2.95 m wide, 3.62 m high, floor 1.13 m, four double doors a side, single-arm pantographs, 120 km/h, from 2015. The ja article: the sides rise upright from the waist (the gutter built into them), no inward lean.
- **Door pitch** - JR East's 20 m four-door standard: door centres 4.82 m apart, the outer ones 2.52 m from the body's ends, 1.3 m doors; the cab car's first door 3.6 m ahead of its second (measured on photo-jr-east-kuhae235-1.jpg).
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
