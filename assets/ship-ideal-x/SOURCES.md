## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "SS Ideal X" and the photographs: the converted T2 tanker that carried the first 58 containers from Newark to Houston in 1956: 159.6 m long, 20.7 m beam, 9.2 m draught, 4,500 kW turbo-electric drive (approx.), 14.5 knots (27 km/h). The trailer boxes stood on a spar deck built over the tank deck.
