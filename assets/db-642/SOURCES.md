## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - de.wikipedia "DB-Baureihe 642": B'2'B', 41.7 m over the Scharfenberg couplers, 2.83 m wide, floor 575 mm at the doors (60 % low floor), 2 x 275 kW (MTU 6R 183 TD13H), 120 km/h, 68 t, built 1998 - 2008.
- **Height** - 3.85 m (Siemens Desiro Classic data); the bogie centres (15.8 m) and the motor bogie 4.7 m behind the face measured on the Scharfenstein photograph.
- **Gauge** - standard gauge, UIC loading gauge.
