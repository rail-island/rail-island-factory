Published figures (Wikipedia, "0 Series Shinkansen"): end cars 25,150 mm and
intermediate cars 25,000 mm long, 3,383 mm wide, 3,975 mm high; DT200 bogies,
2,500 mm wheelbase, 910 mm wheels, bogie centres 17,500 mm; every car motored,
four 185 kW MT200 motors (740 kW a car); 210 km/h at the opening in 1964, 220 km/h
later. The model is 3.06 m wide, inside the repository's 3.1 m envelope.

Car weights are not published per car in these sources: the model's 56 to 60 t
are estimates. The running numbers `21-1`, `25-1`, `26-1`, `15-1` and `35-1`
follow the preserved first-production cars (21-1 and 35-1 are at the Kyoto
Railway Museum); the 25-1, 26-1 and 15-1 are illustrative numbers of the same
scheme (type, hyphen, serial), which has no check digit.

The JR West "fresh green" scheme (the last Kodama R sets, 2002 to 2008: light
grey, a dark grey window band and a green line) is drawn from memory of
photographs; no free image of it was downloaded (Wikimedia Commons rate-limited
the downloads), so its colours are approximate.
