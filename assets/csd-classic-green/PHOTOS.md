# CSD Classic Green Coach - reference photos

One parametric model, `variant` classA (vlakosimdb csd_a) or classB (csd_b), on the same UIC-Y body.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and, for BY-SA, share-alike.

## Variant classA (CSD A)

Prototype: CSD class A (1960s-80s Ba/Am, later CD A149/A150/A151 etc.), 1st class 24.5 m UIC-Y compartment coach with 9 compartments, built for CSD by VEB Waggonbau Bautzen (GDR), Goerlitz V/Va bogies. Source: https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C5%AFz_A150,_151,_149_%C4%8CD (article title found, not read in detail - builder and dates follow the sister B-coach article https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C5%AFz_B256,_250,_249,_244_%C4%8CD). Confidence: high for the type. No free photo of a 1st class Y coach in the original green CSD livery was found; the green AB below shows that livery on the same body.

## Variant classB (CSD B)

Prototype: CSD 2nd class UIC-Y coach (Ba in the 1960s/70s, Bm in the 1980s, CD B256/B250/B249/B244), 24.5 m, 10 compartments, built by VEB Waggonbau Bautzen 1965-1985, Goerlitz V (to 1981) or Va bogies, 140 km/h. Source: https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C5%AFz_B256,_250,_249,_244_%C4%8CD . Confidence: high.
