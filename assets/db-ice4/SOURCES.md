## Drawing (not stored: DB copyright)

DB Fernverkehr, "Einsatzmerkblatt für Eisenbahnfahrzeuge, ICE 4 / Baureihe 412, 7-Teiler", 02/2024
(https://www1.deutschebahn.com/resource/blob/12769326/3c6acc0dd3fab0030768e133a6133e13/DB-412_7T_01-2024-data.pdf):
side drawings of every car type. Measured at 53.4 px/m (end car) and 66.5 px/m (Mittelwagen 3812):
the nose profile (bumper to 1.4 m, windscreen's foot 1.4 m behind the tip at 2.4 m, roof reached 5.9 m
behind it), the end car's windows (pointed band from 2.5 m, first window 6.0 - 7.5 m, door 7.9 - 9.0 m,
six windows at 2.48 m pitch, rear door at its end), the intermediate car's doors at both ends and nine
1.75 m windows at 2.53 m pitch in a band from 2.0 to 24.8 m, the bogies 19.5 m apart (4.9 m behind the tip
on the end car), the pantograph over the rear bogie of the unpowered Mittelwagen 3812 and the end car's
roof rising over its rear third. Also its text: the end cars and the Mittelwagen are unpowered (class
812), the three Powercars (412) carry the traction; pantographs on the Mittelwagen and Traktionswagen 7412.

## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - de.wikipedia "DB-Baureihe 412" and en.wikipedia "ICE 4": 29.106 m end car and 28.75 m
  intermediate car over the couplers, 2.852 m wide, 4.11 m high (end car 3.87 m), bogie centres 19.5 m,
  trailer bogies 2.3 m wheelbase with 825 mm wheels (powered: 2.6 m, 920 mm), 250 km/h (265 km/h for the
  12- and 13-car sets), from 2017.
- **Gauge** - standard gauge, UIC loading gauge.
