## Published figures

- fr.wikipedia "Talgo Avril" (after Transportrail, 29 June 2021): power cars 19.37 m, trailers 13.21 m (14.94 m for the two beside the power cars), 8,000 kW, 329 t empty.
- en.wikipedia "Talgo AVRIL": 12 passenger cars and 2 power cars, 201.8 m, 3.2 m wide, 330 km/h; es.wikipedia "Serie 106 de Renfe": 507 (AVE) / 581 (Avlo) seats, the luggage room in the power cars, units 106.001-015 and 106.051-065.
