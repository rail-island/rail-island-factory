## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (`pnpm refs:fetch`). The model is the **low-cab Kuha 103 with the
twin sealed-beam headlight and the AU75 air conditioner** (1970s - 2000s), as on Kuha 103-1, -149 and the
Yamatoji car; Kuha 103-6 shows the 1963 single round headlight the twin beams replaced. None of them has a
front gangway door: the 103's front is a non-gangway (hikantsu) end.

## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "103 series": 20.0 m cars, 2.832 m wide, 100 km/h, 3,447 built 1963 - 1984; the line colours vermilion (Chūō), uguisu green (Yamanote).
- **Bogies** - 13.8 m bogie centres, 860 mm wheels (DT33 / TR64), centred on the 19.5 m body.
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
