## Measurements

Source x from the front coupler face, which stands 0.17 m ahead of the pilot's foot where the
drawing's "length over all" starts; heights over the rail.

- **Drawing** (`drawing-k4s.jpg`, side elevation, 92.3 px/m from the 36 ft 2 in total wheelbase over
  1017 px): lead axle 3 ft 7 1/8 in behind the pilot, lead truck wheelbase 6 ft 10 in, its centre
  8 ft 5 in ahead of the first driver, drivers 6 ft 11 in + 6 ft 11 in (13 ft 10 in), trailing axle
  10 ft 6 in behind the last driver, 7 ft 1 1/4 in + 16 1/4 in to the drawbar, 48 ft 2 5/8 in over all:
  axles at 1.27, 3.35, 4.87, 6.98, 9.09 and 12.29 m, the drawbar end at 14.86 m. Boiler centre
  9 ft 9 11/16 in (2.99 m), stack top 15 ft 0 in (4.57 m), coupler 2 ft 10 1/2 in (0.88 m), cab roof
  14 ft 6 in (4.42 m over the ventilator), cab 10 ft 3 in wide. Measured on the grid: smokebox front
  1.43 m, smokebox 1.0 m in radius to 3.09 m, the conical course to about 7.4 m (top 4.0 to 4.17 m),
  the Belpaire firebox from 9.17 m to the cab at 12.97 m (top 4.2 m), stack centre 3 ft 3 1/2 in behind
  the smokebox front (2.48 m), bell 5 ft 2 in further (4.06 m), sand dome 6 ft 3 in further (5.96 m),
  steam dome 6 ft 11 in further (8.07 m), cylinders 27 by 28 in from 1.92 to 3.06 m.
- **5400** (`photo-k4s-5400.jpg`, 65.2 px/m from the drivers' 6 ft 11 in pitch): running board at
  2.02 m, smokebox top 4.05 m, boiler top 4.2 m over the firebox; cab side 1.6 m long, its sash window
  0.9 m wide from 2.74 to 3.42 m, the number plate 1.15 m wide from 2.26 to 2.52 m under it; the roof
  from 1.2 m ahead of the cab side (a hood over the firebox's back) to 1.0 m behind it, crown 4.3 m.
  Its front overhang reads 0.45 m longer than the drawing's (perspective at the ends of a broadside):
  the drawing's lengths are used.
- **1361 and 3750**: the electric headlight on top of the smokebox front with the number boards on
  its sides, the keystone (red field, gold rim and number) on the door, the platform ahead of the
  smokebox at the running board's height, the cast steel pilot, spoked drivers on both.

## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "Pennsylvania Railroad class K4": 4-6-2, 80 in drivers, 36 in leading
  and 50 in trailing wheels, 27 by 28 in cylinders, about 140 t engine, 100 mph; from 1914.
- **Tender** - Wikipedia "Pennsylvania Railroad 1361": since the 1950s 1361 has carried a 130-P-75
  tender, 13,475 US gallons and 22 tons of coal (originally a 70-P-75, then 90-P-75 and 110-P-75).
- **Running number** - 1361, a preserved engine (Railroaders Memorial Museum, Altoona).
