## Published figures

- **Figures** - Wikipedia, [SNCF CC 40100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNCF_CC_40100) and [CC 40100](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/CC_40100) (fr): 22.03 m over the buffers, 107 t, 4,480 kW, 160 km/h in service, 1.10 m wheels, built 1964 - 1970 (`generator/frCc40100.ts`, `generator/openttdTable.ts`, [france-trains.md](../../docs/models/france-trains.md)). The bogie centres (about 14 m) and wheelbase (3.2 m) are measured on 40110.
