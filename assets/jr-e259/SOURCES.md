## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - ja.wikipedia "JR東日本E259系電車": 21,430 mm end car and 20,500 mm intermediate car over the couplers (124.86 m in six cars), body 2,946 mm wide, roof 3,655 mm and floor 1,140 mm above the rail; single-leaf doors 935 mm wide (two a side, one on the Green car), window glass 745 mm tall; 4M2T, 16 x 140 kW (560 kW a motor car); PS33D single-arm pantographs, one on the Moha E259 0, two on the Moha E259 500 (one a spare); 130 km/h, from 2009.
- **Liveries** - the same article: white body, black round the windows up to the roof's shoulder, the whole roof carmine (#e30304); on the front only the gangway door black with the N'EX. From 2023 the door is silver (#c5c5c5) with "SERIES E259", the side's rear N'EX also "SERIES E259".
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
