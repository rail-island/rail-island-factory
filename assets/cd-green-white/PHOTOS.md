# CD Green-White Coach - reference photos

One parametric model, `variant` a, ab, b, bee, beel, bds, ds, bdmeer or bdmtee (vlakosimdb cd_a ... cd_bdmtee).

Prototypes: the UIC-Y compartment coaches built for CSD by VEB Waggonbau Bautzen (CD A149/AB349/B249 and their modernised Bee and air-conditioned Beel rebuilds), the Y-type BDs (with a luggage compartment) and Ds (luggage van), and the 26.4 m Bdmtee (UIC-Z, built 1989-1990) and BDmeer, all in the CD green and white livery of the 1990s and 2000s. Sources: https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C5%AFz_B256,_250,_249,_244_%C4%8CD and https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C5%AFz_Bdmtee281,_275_%C4%8CD .

New photographs could not be downloaded for this transfer: the build environment's network policy blocks commons.wikimedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org. The four files below were downloaded earlier for the CSD Bdmtee and the CSD classic green coach (same files, same licences) and are kept here because they show this livery and running gear. Still wanted: a side view of a green and white Y coach (A, B, Bee or Beel), a BDs or Ds with its lookout bay, and a BDmeer.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and, for BY-SA, share-alike.
