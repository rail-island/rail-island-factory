## Published figures

From the English and Chinese Wikipedia articles "CRH5" (China Railway CRH5, 和谐号CRH5型电力动车组; the
manufacturer's data sheet cited there): end car 27,600 mm, intermediate cars 25,000 mm, width 3,200 mm,
height 4,270 mm, train 211.5 m and 451 t (eight cars, 5M3T: Mc M Tp M T Tp M Mc, the pantographs on the
two Tp trailers), 5,500 kW, 250 km/h, wheels 890 / 810 mm, bogie wheelbase 2,700 mm, CA250 bogies.
The 19.0 m bogie centres are the ETR 600 family's (end overhang 3.0 m over the couplers, as measured).
