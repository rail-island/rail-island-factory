# CD 460 - reference photos

One parametric model, `veh-cd-460`: `unit` motorCar (vlakosimdb cd 460/cd_460 and its liveries, the default) or trailer (cd 460/cd_060, csd 460/csd_060.045); `livery` cd (the default), cd003, cd007, csd.

Prototype: Vagonka Tatra Studenka EM 488.0, CD/ZSSK class 460 + 063, 43 units built 1971 and 1974 to 1978 for 3 kV DC service in the Ostrava region and in Slovakia, in service until 2024; five-car sets of two motor cars and up to three trailers, 122.5 m over the buffers, Bo'Bo' + 3 x 2'2' + Bo'Bo', high floor, side buffers and screw couplings at the cabs, two pantographs per motor car (cs.wikipedia "Elektricka jednotka 460").

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (rendered by `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title.
