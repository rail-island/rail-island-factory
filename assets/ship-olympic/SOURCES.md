## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "RMS Olympic" and the photographs: RMS Olympic (1911), the Titanic's sister: 269 m long, 28.2 m beam, 10.5 m draught, 52,067 t displacement, 2,435 passengers, steam engines and turbine of 38,000 kW, 23 knots (43 km/h), four funnels (the aft one a dummy).
- Measured off the class profile plan (`photo-titanic-side-plan-annotated-english.jpg`, 4.46 px/m over the 269 m length, the keel as datum; the funnel tops land on the published 175 ft) - metres from the stern: poop 0-33, aft well 33-46, the house (B and A decks) 46-214 with the boat deck's houses from 60, forward well 214-229, forecastle 229-269; funnels 85, 122, 156, 192 (about 7.5 m long, raked about 3.6 m over their height, the black top about 6.7 m); mainmast 45, foremast 230 (crow's nest about 37 m over the keel, mast tops about 69-70 m); bridge front 211; boats 66-106 and 164-203 (four a side in each group, 1911); the counter overhangs about 9 m aft of the sternpost, its underside about 17-19 m over the keel (approx.).
- Heights over the keel (approx., from the plan and the published 60 ft of the boat deck over the waterline): C (shelter) deck and the well decks about 20.4 m, B deck (poop, top of the black) 22.8 m, A deck 26 m, boat deck 29 m, the deckhouses' roofs 31.6 m.
- From the photographs (`photo-olympic-in-new-york-cropped.jpg`, `photo-olympic-sjohistoriska-museet-fo192178-cropped.jpg`): black hull up to the B deck following the sheer, the white B-deck strake with its windows flush above it, the A-deck promenade open along its whole length under the boat deck, the forecastle and poop black, the buff funnels with black tops, the masts raked like the funnels.
