## Published figures

zh.wikipedia "和谐号CRH1型电力动车组" (2026): CRH1E 1061 - 1080 built on Bombardier's Zefiro 250
platform, 16 cars 10M6T, 250 km/h, train 428.9 m (CRH1B 426.3 m with the CRH1A's 26.95 m end cars,
so 28.25 m end cars), intermediate cars 26.6 m, width 3,328 mm, height 4,040 mm, wheelbase 2,700 mm;
cars 1 and 16 second-class seat cars (61 seats), soft sleepers of 40 berths, the deluxe sleeper (16)
and dining car in the middle; the pantographs on the Tp cars 2, 7, 10 and 15; 1076 - 1080 were built
as CRH1B with the CRH1E nose. Power 11,000 kW (CRH1E-NG figure, same 10M6T traction).
