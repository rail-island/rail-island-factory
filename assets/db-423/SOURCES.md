## Works drawing (figures only, not stored)

The side, plan and end elevation "Zeichnung Baureihe 423" (https://www.nahverkehr-franken.de/sbahn/img_techdat/423_zeichnung.jpg,
cited by de.wikipedia; no free licence, so only measured, at 15.04 px/m): end cars 18.24 m coupler to
joint, intermediate cars 15.46 m, end bogie centres 15.14 m, wheelbase 2.2 m (end bogies) and 2.7 m
(Jacobs bogies), 3.02 m wide; crown 3.79 m, window band 1.8 - 2.88 m, doors 0.97 - 3.14 m (1.3 m opening
in a 1.5 m portal); every door and window position (`layout` in generator/deBr423.ts); the face's profile
and plan; the pantograph on car 433.0 next to the middle joint; an air-conditioning unit on every car.

## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - de.wikipedia "DB-Baureihe 423": four cars, Bo'(Bo')(2')(Bo')Bo', 67.4 m over the
