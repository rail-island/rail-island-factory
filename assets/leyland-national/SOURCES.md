## Published figures

- Leyland National (Wikipedia "Leyland National"): the integral single-decker built from 1972 to 1985 at Workington, 10.3 or 11.3 m long, 2.5 m wide, about 3.05 m high, its heating unit in a pod on the rear of the roof; modelled at 11.3 m in National Bus Company poppy red with the white band, about 80 passengers (approx.).
