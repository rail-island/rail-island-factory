# Remaining freight source evidence

Native XML and original BMP textures from [vlakosimdb](https://github.com/JanProch/vlakosimdb), revision `b170acfe6dbd1e8e6f17dd3bfd19bafa3d2fc94f`.
The source files retain their original author/credit metadata; no `_4x` images are used.
The database source is a shape/paint reference, not photographic evidence of an individual wagon.

Source X runs along the wagon, Y is height above rail, Z is lateral. Factory X is translated by half the nominal length; Y and Z keep metre units. Shared Wagon running gear uses the existing factory reference evidence under `assets/wagon-nas/` and the documented 1.435 m gauge / 1.75 m buffer spacing.

`n_z`, `n_lp`, `n_zaz`, `n_vte`, `n_uaaikk` supply body profiles. `n_pao` and `n_wap` document empty variants of existing families. Transported tram and bus loads are intentionally excluded.

The new numbers `21 54 1500 001-1` and `31 54 3999 001-3` are illustrative UIC numbers with valid check digits, not photographed vehicle identities. Body detail is simplified; no claim of pixel-exact texture reproduction is made. See [COMPARISON.md](COMPARISON.md) for geometric checks and limitations.
