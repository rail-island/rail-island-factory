Uaaikk whole-model height includes the end shelter and stakes; its dropped deck follows the source 0.20–1.30 m section, so the whole-model height difference is not deck error. Covered wagon widths include protruding door handles; the structural body is 2.80 m wide. Lp uses a simplified common body profile and should not be treated as a literal copy of its wider source shell.

This envelope comparison does not measure individual lettering, ribs or doors. Those details remain approximate, especially for archived replacements. Ground-vehicle scales are deliberately left to the game.
