## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - The Diesel Shop data sheet "EMD GP9" (thedieselshop.us): 56 ft 2 in over the couplers, hood 13 ft 11 in and cab 14 ft 6 in over the rail, cab 10 ft 3 in wide, 9 ft truck wheelbase, 40 in wheels, 249,000 lb, 1,750 hp, 65 mph. Truck centres 31 ft 0 in (the published EMD figure; the data sheet's 40 ft would put the trucks at the pilots).
- **Measured on UP 211** (scale from the published truck centres, about 51 px/m, cross-checked against
  the 9 ft wheelbase and the published hood and cab heights): long hood end 7.70 m behind the
  middle, cab 2.96 - 4.82 m, short hood face 6.65 m, end sills at 8.0 m with 0.72 m step wells cut
  into the side sills; deck 1.58 m, sill foot 1.15 m dropping 0.22 m between the trucks; UP's red
  stripe 3.49 - 3.56 m; cab side window 3.53 - 4.51 m by 3.08 - 3.82 m; radiator grilles
  -6.33 .. -4.06 m; louvred doors -1.17 .. 0.59 m; generator louvres 0.97 - 1.92 m with the air
  intake above them; battery vent -6.59 .. -6.18 m.
- **Running numbers** - illustrative numbers in each railroad's GP9 series (UP 150, PRR 7006, ATSF 2894);
  the photographed units are UP 211, PRR 7048 and 7000, ATSF 2879.
- **Paint** - the railroads' schemes simplified to colour fields, stripes and the
  railroad's name in plain letters; no heralds or logos are drawn.
- **Per scheme** - UP as on 211 (no dynamic brake, the small hood number at the rear corner);
  PRR as on 7048 and 7000 (dynamic brake, snowplow, no sill stripe, safety-yellow end rails);
  Santa Fe as on 2879 (dynamic brake, yellow hood ends, stripe and sill).
