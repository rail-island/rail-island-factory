## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (the first two at 1920 px, scaled to 1600 px on the long side).
The modelled car is a stand-alone 40 ft well car of TTX's DTTX 45xxxx series (Gunderson's
"Husky-Stack" type, many rebuilt from 48 ft cars); its running number and lettering are those of
DTTX 455501. The articulated cars (DTTX 72xxxx, 76xxxx) show the shared details only: the end
platforms, walkways, ladders, guides, brake wheel and the well floor.

## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - a stand-alone 40 ft well car about 63 ft 6 in over the couplers (Greenbrier's 53 ft
  Husky-Stack is 76 ft 8.75 in; the 40 ft car is 8 ft shorter in the well and 13 ft shorter overall,
  confirmed by the photograph: about 17.6 m over the end sills); truck centres 52 ft (photo: 15.9 m);
  ISO 40 ft high-cube containers 12.19 by 2.44 by 2.9 m; double-stack clearance about 20 ft 3 in (AAR Plate H).
- **Containers** - the boxes are a believable unbranded leasing livery (blue, oxide red) with the
  illustrative owner code RIXU and valid ISO 6346 check digits (RIXU 401235 0, RIXU 512870 3).
