## Published figures

From the English and Chinese Wikipedia articles "China Railways HXD1" and "HXD1型电力机车" (citing the Siemens and CSR Zhuzhou data sheets and Railway Gazette, 2007): 35.222 m over the couplers, two identical single-cab Bo'Bo' sections (17.611 m each) joined by a gangway; 3.247 m wide (drawn 3.1 m, the UIC gauge); bogie centres 9.0 m, bogie wheelbase 2.8 m, wheels 1.25 m; 9,600 kW, 120 km/h, 184 t (200 t ballasted); one TSG15 single-arm pantograph on each section's roof at its inner end.

The plan gave the design a red livery, but every photograph shows the HXD1 blue: the model paints the original dark blue and silver livery (the later light blue overhaul livery is not drawn). The running number HXD1 0016 is the side-on photograph's unit.
