# CD 451 - reference photos

One parametric model, `veh-cd-451`: `unit` motorCar (vlakosimdb cd 451/cd_451, the default) or trailer (cd 451/cd_051).

Prototype: Vagonka Tatra Studenka EM 475.1, CD class 451 + 051, 51 two-car technical units built 1964 to 1968 for 3 kV DC suburban service around Prague (and for a time Ostrava, Usti and Kosice), run as four-car sets of two motor cars and two trailers until 2018; 95.29 m over the couplers for the four-car set, Bo'Bo' + 2'2' + 2'2' + Bo'Bo', low floor at about 550 mm between the bogies, Scharfenberg couplers. Nicknamed "zabotlam" (frog mouth) for the nose, also "pantak" and "Emilka" (cs.wikipedia "Elektricke jednotky 451 a 452").

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (rendered by `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title.
