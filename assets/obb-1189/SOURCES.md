## Published figures

From the German Wikipedia article "BBÖ 1100" (the 1100.1 series, later OBB 1189): built 1926/27 by the
Austrian Brown Boveri works (electrical part) and Floridsdorf (mechanical part), nine locomotives;
(1'C)(C1'); 20,400 mm over the buffers; total wheelbase 17,700 mm; drivers 1,350 mm, carrying wheels
870 mm; 116 t in service; 1,900 kW one-hour and 1,740 kW continuous rating; 75 km/h. Liveries: fir green
RAL 6009 from 1955, brown on 1189.05 and 1189.08 from 1953 to 1955, blood orange on 1189.02 from 1971.

## Measured (photo-1100102petrzalka1998a.jpg, 60.3 px/m)

Axles from the centre: pony 8.85 m, drivers 6.86 / 3.01 / 1.30 m, jackshaft about 4.6 m at 1.1 m height.
Cab body 6.84 m long with 0.45 m corner chamfers, roof 3.8 m; hoods to 8.75 m, top about 2.7 m.

## Running numbers

`1189.05` (green; photographed at Wien Heiligenstadt 1985 and preserved at Strasshof), `1189.08` (brown,
1953-55, after the article; no photograph of the brown livery was found), `1189.02` (blood orange, photographed).
