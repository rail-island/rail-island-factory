## Published figures

zh.wikipedia "和谐号CRH1型电力动车组" and en.wikipedia "China Railway CRH1": end car 26,950 mm, intermediate car
26,600 mm, 213.5 m for eight; 3,328 mm wide, 4,040 mm high; 2,700 mm bogie wheelbase; 420.4 t; 265 kW x 20 =
5,300 kW; formation Mc Tp M M T M Tp Mc (5M3T); first sets ZY 72 and ZE 101 seats; 200 km/h in service. The
modelled unit is CRH1A-1003 (the former CRH1-003A). The pantograph's car follows the photographs (the end car near its
inner end), not the table's Tp; bogie centres (19.0 m) and the door and window positions are photo estimates.
