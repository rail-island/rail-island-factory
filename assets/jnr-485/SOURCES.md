## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "485 series": 21 m end (KuHa 481) and 20.5 m middle cars, 2.9 m wide, 120 km/h, from 1964.
- **Heights** - ja.wikipedia "国鉄485系電車": floor 1,230 mm, the step at 970 mm, the cab's top 3,880 mm (as on the 151 series); the bodywork in Cream No. 4 and Red No. 2; KuHa 481-1 - 8 had red skirts, the later cars cream ones.
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
