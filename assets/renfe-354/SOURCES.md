The model carries 354 001 "Virgen de Covadonga" (number `354-001-0` on the face), photographed in the
Talgo livery as restored at the Museu del Ferrocarril de Catalunya.

## Published figures

Wikipedia (es: "Serie 354 de Renfe"; en: "Renfe Class 354"): Krauss-Maffei 1982-84, eight units, B'B'
diesel-hydraulic, 19.92 m long, 3.04 m wide, 3.45 m high, 80 t, 2 x MTU 396 TD 13, 4,171 CV (3,070 kW),
180 km/h as delivered, 200 km/h from 1986.
