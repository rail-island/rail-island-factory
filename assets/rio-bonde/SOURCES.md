## Published figures

- Santa Teresa Bonde (Wikipedia "Santa Teresa Tram" and the operators' published data): in service from 1896; 9.2 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.3 m wide, 3.1 m to the roof (approx.); 32 passengers with standing room (approx.); 25 km/h, 60 kW, 9 t (approx.); track gauge 1100 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
