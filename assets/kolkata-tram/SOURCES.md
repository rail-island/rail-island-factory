## Published figures

- Kolkata Tram (Wikipedia "Trams in Kolkata" and the operators' published data): in service from 1940; 18.5 m long over the bodies in 2 sections, 2.2 m wide, 3.3 m to the roof (approx.); 200 passengers with standing room (approx.); 40 km/h, 120 kW, 18 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
