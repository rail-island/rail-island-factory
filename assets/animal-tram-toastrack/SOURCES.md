## Published figures

- Douglas Bay Horse Tramway, Isle of Man (Wikipedia "Douglas Bay Horse Tramway"): opened 1876, track gauge 3 ft (914 mm); open summer "toastrack" cars built 1876-1907 by Milnes, Starbuck and others, about 7.5-8 m over the dashes, about 2.0 m wide over the bench ends and 2.6 m over the running boards, about 2.9 m to the canopy's clerestory (approx.); 8 reversible cross benches seating 40 (approx.); about 13 km/h (8 mph) behind one horse, the car about 2 t empty (approx.).
- Read off the photographs (approx.): the canopy about 2.6 m up with a raised clerestory and a scalloped white valance (car 45), the posts at every bench, red solid bench ends with varnished arms, the solid red dash with gold-lined panels and the brass rail, the low red valance hiding the truck, the grey running board along each side.
- Estimates (approx.): bench pitch (0.81 m), wheel diameter (0.72 m) and wheelbase (2.0 m), colours. The real gauge is 914 mm; like every tram the model rides on the factory's standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/world-trams.md, docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
