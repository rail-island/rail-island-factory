## Figures

English and Chinese Wikipedia, "China Railways SS8" / "韶山8型电力机车" (infobox): 17,516 mm over the coupler
centres, a 16,300 mm body, 3,100 mm wide, 4,040 mm to the pantograph seat, 88 t (22 t axle load), Bo-Bo,
3,600 kW, 170 km/h in service (239.6 km/h record by SS8 0001 in 1998), wheels 1,250 mm new (1,200 mm half
worn), two single-arm pantographs (TSG3, later DSA-200), one behind each cab. Bogie centres and the bogie
wheelbase are not published there: measured from the side photograph (about 8.2 m and 2.8 m).

## Livery

Every photograph shows the class's blue livery (light blue, cream, royal blue, a navy stripe); no SS8 in red and
white was found, so the model is in the blue livery (its single livery is not a parameter).
