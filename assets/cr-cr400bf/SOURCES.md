The modelled units: `gold` CR400BF-5001 (the 2017 production livery, white with a charcoal window
band and two gold lines). The `red` livery is the same scheme with red lines; no photograph here shows
a CR400BF in it (5033's car 5 wears the gold lines), so it is illustrative and its number kept from
the earlier model.

## Published figures

English and Chinese Wikipedia ("China Railway CR400BF", "复兴号CR400BF型电力动车组"): 8 cars 209.06 m
(4M4T: Tc M Tp M M Tp M Tc, the pantographs on cars 3 and 6, one raised in single-set service), end car
27.089 m, intermediate cars 25.65 m, width 3.36 m, height 4.05 m, floor 1.25 m, 920 mm wheels,
10.14 MW, 350 km/h. The model: end car 27.35 m and intermediate cars 25.7 m over the couplers (8 cars
208.9 m), 17.5 m bogie centres, 2.5 m wheelbase, narrowed to 3.24 m for the Shinkansen gauge.
