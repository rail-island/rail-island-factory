## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "100 Series Shinkansen": end car 26.05 m, intermediate 25.0 m, 3.38 m wide, 4.0 m high, 230 km/h, 1985 - 2012; the double-deck dining and Green cars are not drawn.
- **Bogies** - 17.5 m centres, 2.5 m wheelbase, 910 mm wheels; the leading one 4.73 m behind the tip.
- **Pantograph** - PS202 diamond, at the car's end (the K58 photograph).
- **Width** - narrowed to 3.24 - 3.28 m (the Shinkansen gauge's 3.3 m; the E6 keeps its own).
