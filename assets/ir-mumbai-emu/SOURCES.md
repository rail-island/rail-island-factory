## Figures

- Car about 20.7 m over the body, 3.66 m wide (narrowed to 3.24 m for the 3.3 m gauge), ICF bogies 14.63 m apart, 2.9 m wheelbase, 952 mm wheels; the 3.85 m roof height is the photographs' body side (2.73 m) over a 1.1 m sole bar.
