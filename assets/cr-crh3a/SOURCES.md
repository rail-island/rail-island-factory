## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons by `pnpm refs:fetch` as standard thumbnails, at most 1280 px on the long side.
The model is the 250 km/h CRH3A of CRRC Changchun's production sets CRH3A-5230 - 5257 (2017; Chengdu
Railway Bureau), the version with by far the most Commons photographs; the Tangshan sets 3081 - 3111 share the
livery. The CRH3A-A (the 4-car intercity version, its own nose) is not modelled. Its running number
CRH3A-5253 is the unit of `photo-china-railways-crh3a-5253-20180427.jpg` and
`photo-crh3a-5253-at-chengjiayao-20250108120103.jpg`.

## Published figures

- zh.wikipedia, the article "CRH3A" (Hexie CRH3A EMU): CRRC Changchun (sets 5230 - 5257) and CRRC Tangshan (3081 - 3111),
  type approval 2016, in service from 6 December 2017; 8 cars (one first class, six second class, one second
  class and buffet car), 4M4T; 250 km/h; 5,120 kW at the wheels; bogie centres and car-end spacing as the
  China standard EMU (Fuxing), so the CR300BF's 17.5 m bogie centres and car lengths are used.
- Not found: the cars' published lengths and weights - taken from the China standard 250 km/h shell
  (27.35 m end car, 25.7 m intermediate car) and estimated at 52 t and 50 t.
