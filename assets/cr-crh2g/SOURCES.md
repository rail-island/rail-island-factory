## Published figures

- 201.4 m over the 8 cars (painted on the cars' sides), so 25.7 m end cars and 25 m intermediate cars as the CRH2A; 3.38 m wide, 3.7 m high (en.wikipedia, China Railway CRH2).
- 6M2T, Tc M M Mp M Mp M Tc, the pantographs on cars 4 and 6; seats 48/90/90/77/63/90/90/65 = 613; 250 km/h; certified 10 November 2015 (zh.wikipedia, CRH2G section).
- The power is not given in the sources read: 7,200 kW is assumed from six motor cars of four 300 kW CRH2 motors (zh.wikipedia calls the type underpowered for the Lanzhou-Xinjiang line's long gradients).
