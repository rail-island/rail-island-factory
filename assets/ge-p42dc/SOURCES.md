## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "GE Genesis" and The Diesel Shop "P40/P42DC": 69 ft 0 in, 10 ft 0 in wide, 14 ft 4 in high, truck centres 43 ft 2.5 in, 9 ft truck wheelbase, 40 in wheels, 268,240 lb, 4,250 hp, 110 mph, from 1996.
- **Measured from the photographs** (docs/models/us-carbodies.md): 167's near-orthographic side
  at 51 px/m (truck centres 677 px = 43 ft 2.5 in) for every opening along the body, the face's
  rake (58.6 degrees, the knee at 2.15 m) and the band's heights (3.30 - 4.02 m); 27 head-on at
  136 px/m for the face; 95 (New Haven) for the swoosh (lower edge about 3.75 m at the rear).
- **Running numbers** - units photographed in each scheme: Amtrak 78 (Phase V), 145 (the 2011
  Phase III heritage unit), VIA 915.
- **Paint** - the schemes as colour fields, stripes and plain letters; the Amtrak travel mark is
  drawn as three tapered stripes, the VIA logo and flags are not drawn.
