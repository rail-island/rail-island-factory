## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "GE Genesis" and The Diesel Shop "P40/P42DC": 69 ft 0 in, 10 ft 0 in wide, 14 ft 4 in high, truck centres 43 ft 2.5 in, 9 ft truck wheelbase, 40 in wheels, 268,240 lb, 4,250 hp, 110 mph, from 1996.
- **Running numbers** - illustrative (Amtrak 100, 42, VIA 901).
- **Paint** - the schemes simplified to colour fields, stripes and plain letters; no
  heralds or logos are drawn.
