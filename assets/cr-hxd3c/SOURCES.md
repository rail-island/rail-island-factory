## Published figures

Wikipedia (en "China Railways HXD3C", zh "HXD3C" article): 20,846 mm between the coupler centres,
3,100 mm wide, 4,100 mm high, 1,250 mm wheels, bogie wheelbase 2,250 + 2,000 mm, 138 t (150 t
ballasted for a 25 t axle load), 7,200 kW, 120 km/h, built from 2010 by CNR Dalian (also CNR Beijing
Feb. 7th and CRRC Datong); the carbody is the HXD3B's. The bogie centres (9.9 m) are a photo estimate
scaled to the published length: no published figure was found.

## Livery and running number

Every photograph found shows the blue "Hexie" livery (blue, grey skirt, white trapezoid, dark grey cab
cap, silver face panel); no red and maroon HXD3C was found. The model carries HXD3C 0736 as
photographed at Beijing West in 2018. The 0679 photograph's named-train slogan is not drawn.
