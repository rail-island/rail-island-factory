## Published figures

Rebuilt 333.3 / 333.4 (Wikipedia "Renfe Class 333" after the Vossloh data sheet; es.wikipedia "Serie 333 de Renfe"): 22.33 m over the buffers, 3.16 m wide, 4.307 m high, 120 t, GM 16-645-E3 engine of 3,300 hp (2,460 kW), Co'Co' on flexicoil bogies, 333.3 120 km/h, 333.4 160 km/h. Wheels 1,016 mm (EMD 40 in). The original 333.0 was 20.7 m long; both modelled liveries are photographed on rebuilt engines.
