## Published figures

es.wikipedia "Serie 120 de Renfe": MCT-MIT-MIP-MCP, 106.96 m over four cars, 2.92 m wide, 4.23 m high, 225 t empty, 4,000 kW (8 x 512 kW), 250 km/h, 237 + 1 seats (120.0). The split into 27.35 m end cars and 26.13 m intermediate cars is an estimate that sums to the published length. Running numbers shown: 120 061 (Alvia, photo-alvia-120-861-madrid-hendaya.jpg) and 121 020 (Media Distancia, illustrative).
