## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "Union Pacific Big Boy": 4-8-8-4, 36 in leading, 68 in driving and 42 in
  trailing wheels; locomotive 85 ft 3.4 in (25.99 m), with the tender 132 ft 9.25 in (40.47 m: the tender
  14.47 m); engine wheelbase 72 ft 5.5 in (22.08 m), with the tender 117 ft 7 in (35.84 m); 16 ft 2.5 in
  high, 11 ft 2 in wide (drawn 3.29 m, the US gauge's width); boiler 107 in outside at its largest; engine
  762,000 lb; 135,375 lbf tractive effort, about 6,290 hp, 80 mph; built 1941 - 1944.
- **Running number** - 4014, the restored engine (its number boards read X4014).

## Measurements

Photographs in perspective: positions along the engine come from a 1-D projective fit of the image x of
the axles (leading 2.05 m, drivers 5.45 - 11.0 m at their 1.85 m pitch; residuals under 8 px), heights
from the scale of a driver near the point measured (Cajon: 168 px/m in a 2.5x crop at the fourth driver).
