## Published figures

de.wikipedia "Nightjet" (and "Railjet 1. Generation" for the shared Viaggio Comfort body): seven-car sets
Bfmpz (control car, 70 seats), ABbmpvz (multi-purpose car, 26 seats, 2 barrier-free berths and 2 companions'
places, 6 bicycles), 3 x Bcmz (couchette cars, 40 places: 28 mini cabins and 3 four-berth compartments),
2 x WLAmz (sleeping cars, 10 compartments, 20 beds); 260 places a set; 230 km/h; Siemens Mobility, in service
from December 2023, 24 sets. Coaches 26.5 m over buffers, 2.825 m wide, 4.05 m high, bogie centres 19.0 m,
2.5 m wheelbase (the Railjet's). Not found: the control car's length (taken as the Railjet driving
trailer's 26.85 m) and the cars' order in the set (the sleeping cars at the locomotive's end, as photographed).
