## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "SS Great Britain" and the photographs: Brunel's SS Great Britain (1845): 98 m long, 15.4 m beam, 5.5 m draught, 3,675 t displacement, the first iron screw ship on the Atlantic, a 750 kW (1,000 hp) steam engine and six masts, 11 knots (20 km/h).
- Read off the 1843 elevation (approx., 11.4 px/m over the 98 m hull): masts 1-6 at 81.4, 68.3, 51.1, 39.2, 25.4 and 12.6 m from the stern, the funnel's centre at 57 m, its diameter about 2.6-2.8 m and its top about 12 m above the deck; the museum ship's rig (photographs): mast 2 square-rigged with its yards, the others fore-and-aft.
