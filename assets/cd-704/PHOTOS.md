# CD 704 / CSD T 212.1 - reference photos

Two liveries share one body in the model: `cd704` (704 014-0) and `t2380` (704 518, the industrial T 238.0); the vlakosimdb paint (blue with a yellow band) is drawn from its BMPs, the photographs give the body, the cab roof, the louvres and the running gear.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (1280 px thumbnails, or the original where it is smaller, saved as JPEG quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title.
