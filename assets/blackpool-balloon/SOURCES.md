## Published figures

- Blackpool Balloon (Wikipedia "Blackpool Balloon tram" and the operators' published data): in service from 1934; 12.8 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.29 m wide, 4.75 m to the roof (approx.); 94 passengers with standing room (approx.); 55 km/h, 160 kW, 19 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
