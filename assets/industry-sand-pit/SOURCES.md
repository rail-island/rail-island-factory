## Judged dimensions

No drawing of a sand pit was used; the rest is judged from the photographs and common practice.

| figure | value | use |
|---|---|---|
| pit face | a 3.2 m sand face sloping about 1:1 under 0.6 m of soil and grass | the shallow excavation at the back of the 46 x 32 m site |
| washing plant | a 6.4 x 5 m stage at 5.6 m carrying the vibrating screen and two hydrocyclones, a sump with a sand screw, a settling pond | the plant fed by a field conveyor from the hopper at the face |
| loading bin | a 3.6 x 3.6 m bin from 4.6 to 7.2 m on four legs over a concrete road bay, a three-axle tipper under it | the road loadout |
| sand | pale ochre (#ead3a0) heaps, the faces #ddc189 | the conical heaps under the stackers and the pit face |
