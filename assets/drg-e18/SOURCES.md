## Drawing and photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons by `pnpm refs:fetch` as standard thumbnails, at most 1280 px on the long side.
The modelled unit is **E 18 31** in DR green (photographed in Weimar): green body, black sill band, window
ledge and gutter line, grey roof, red wheels and pantographs. The preserved E 18 047 (Koblenz-Luetzel) is
blue today and E 18 08 was photographed in grey: both are used for the shared body only. The DR drawing
shows E 218 019, an E 18 the DR rebuilt; its body, running gear and dimensions are the E 18's.
