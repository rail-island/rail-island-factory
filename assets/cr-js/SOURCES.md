## Published figures

- Wikipedia, [China Railways JS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Railways_JS) (after Gibbons 2015): 23,370 mm over
  the couplers with the tender; wheelbase 4.419 m fixed, 10.192 m engine, 20.487 m engine and tender; wheels 840 mm
  leading, 1,370 mm coupled, 1,120 mm trailing, 1,000 mm tender; 3,150 mm wide, 4,711 mm high; cylinders 580 x 710 mm;
  engine 104 t, tender 82 - 85.7 t; 2,270 hp (1,690 kW); 85 km/h; knuckle couplers; built 1957 - 1988.
- The axle positions not published (pony truck to first driver 2.70 m, the tender's bogies 1.85 m wheelbase 4.75 m
  apart) come from the side-on photograph's fit, scaled so the total wheelbase and the length are the published ones.
