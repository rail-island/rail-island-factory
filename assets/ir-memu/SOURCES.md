## Running numbers and measurements

- Running numbers: the blue livery carries **13029**, the Southern Railway (Kollam) driving motor coach
  of `photo-memu-kollam-jn-2.jpg` and `photo-ernakulam-quilon-memu-at-kottayam.jpg`; the maroon livery
  carries **377**, the number painted on the front of the Northern Railway unit in `photo-agra-memu-01.jpg`.
- Face (`photo-delhi-memu-02.jpg`, head-on, about 200 px/m on the face from its 3.66 m width): windscreens
  2.48 - 3.28 m, destination boxes over them 3.37 - 3.57 m, the front's cream down to 2.42 m, the red V's
  upper edge from the corners at 2.42 m to 1.66 m at the centre, its lower edge from 2.0 m to 1.24 m;
  the twin headlights' round housing about 0.55 m across on the dome, centred at 3.9 m.
- Side (`photo-ernakulam-quilon-memu-at-kottayam.jpg`, about 220 px/m up the motor coach's side near the
  cab): window band 1.9 - 2.81 m, barred windows 2.06 - 2.68 m; from the front: the cab door, the
  louvred equipment hatch (louvres 1.88 - 2.98 m, a framed panel under them), a passenger doorway, the
  windows in fours. The folded pantograph sits about 4 m from the motor coach's inner end.
- Northern Railway face (`photo-agra-memu-01.jpg`): the lower colour rises at the corners to about
  3.05 m, the cream V reaching down to about 1.75 m over the hood box.
- Not photographed: a maroon MEMU. The maroon livery is the photographed scheme with maroon in place of
  blue, on the Northern Railway face. The trailer coaches' door positions (a quarter of the car in from
  each end) and the motor coach's second passenger door are not measured: no side-on photograph of a
  whole car was found.
- Published figures: ICF bogie 2,896 mm wheelbase, 952 mm wheels, bogie centres 14.63 m; body about
  21.3 m long and 3.66 m wide (drawn 3.24 m, narrowed to the 3.3 m gauge).
