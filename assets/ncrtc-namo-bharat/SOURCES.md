## Published figures

Wikipedia ("Namo Bharat", "Delhi-Meerut RRTS"): Alstom (ex-Bombardier) trainsets built at Savli, six
cars on the Delhi - Meerut line (three on the Meerut Metro variant, which has its own blue and yellow
livery and a blunter nose), standard gauge 1,435 mm, 25 kV AC, 180 km/h design speed, cars about 22 m
long and 3.2 m wide.
