## Published figures

- **German Wikipedia (DB-Baureihe V 200.0)** - length over buffers 18,530 mm, height 4,160 mm, bogie centres 11,500 mm, wheels 950 mm, 1,618 kW, 140 km/h, about 80 t. The body is drawn 3.0 m wide (the cab doors' handrails within the 3.1 m gauge); the bogie wheelbase (3.2 m) is estimated.
