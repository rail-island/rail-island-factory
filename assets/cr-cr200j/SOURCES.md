## Published figures

From the English and Chinese Wikipedia articles "CR200J" (infobox): power car 20 m, intermediate car 25.5 m,
control car 27.955 m over the couplers; 3,105 mm wide, 4,433 mm high; 2.8 m wheelbase (the power car's);
formations Mc + 7T + Tc (short sets) and Mc + 16T + Mc (long sets); 5,600 kW; 160 km/h; 445 t for the
short set. The power car's 1.25 m wheels and its bogie centres (7.9 m) are measured on the side-on photograph.
