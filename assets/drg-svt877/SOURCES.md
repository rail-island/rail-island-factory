## Published figures

- **en.wikipedia "DRG Class SVT 877"** - 41,920 mm over the buffers; 2,830 mm wide; 4,080 mm high (over the
  stacks); 37,250 mm between the outer axles (so the leading axle 2.335 m behind the coupling plane);
  driving wheels 1,000 mm, carrying wheels 900 mm; 77.4 t empty; 2 x 302 kW diesel-electric; 160 km/h;
  98 seats and a four-seat buffet; cream and violet like the Rheingold's coaches; later DB VT 04 000.
- **Gauge** - standard gauge, UIC loading gauge.
- The engine bogie's 3.5 m wheelbase is an estimate from the side view.
