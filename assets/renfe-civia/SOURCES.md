## Photographs and drawing

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons by `pnpm refs:fetch` as standard thumbnails, at most 1280 px on the long side.

shield) except 465-243, which wears the Rodalies de Catalunya orange and is used for the layout and the
roof only. The model's front shows 465-076 ("076 M" in the cap); the closed coupler cover is 226's and 048's.

## Published figures

es.wikipedia "Civia" (Renfe's fleet sheet cited there): series 462 - 465 of two to five cars
(A1 + A5 + A3 + A4 + A2), 44.8 m (two cars) and 98.05 m (five cars) long - the article's 65.55 m and
80.30 m for three and four cars do not fit those; the model takes the drawing's 17.75 m for every
intermediate car - 2.94 m wide, 4.265 m high, 80 t (two cars) to 157.3 t (five cars), 1,270 kW (two
cars) to 2,200 kW (five cars), 3 kV DC, 120 km/h; 124 seats in two cars, 277 in five; trailer bogies (BR)
under the cabs and motor bogies (BM) shared between the cars; 1.3 m clear doors; the A3 car low-floored.
