Published figures (Wikipedia, "MTR Metro Cammell EMU (DC)"): A cars 22.85 m and
B, C and D cars 22.0 m long, 3.11 m wide, 3.7 m high without the pantograph, floor
1.1 m above the rail; five double-leaf sliding doors a side, 1.4 m wide; Duewag
SF2100 bogies of 2.5 m wheelbase on 850 mm wheels; 85 kW DC traction motors, four a
motor car (340 kW); 1,500 V DC overhead; 80 km/h in service (90 km/h design);
1.0 m/s2. A and B cars are motor cars, the A car with the cab; C cars are motor
cars with the pantograph; D cars are trailers. The 8-car urban formation is
A-C+D+B-C+D+C-A (the caption of the A164 photograph).

Estimates and deviations:

- The model is 3.06 m wide, inside the repository's 3.1 m envelope (3.11 m real).
- Bogie centres (15.6 m) are not published; they are scaled from the photographs.
- Car weights are not published in these sources: the model's 37 t (A), 36 t (B),
  37 t (C) and 27 t (D) are estimates for aluminium metro cars of this size.
- The coupling planes stand 0.25 m beyond the inner end walls (0.5 m between two
  bodies, the bellows between them); the A car's coupler face 0.35 m ahead of its front.
- The running numbers are the cars of the A164 set in the photograph (A164, B456,
  C169, D602; the train's cars A169, C169, D602, B456, C456, D619, C164, A164); the
  retro livery's A car is A161 (the anniversary train's number). The Disneyland
  train is P503-Q503+Q504-P504 (the photographed set). MTR numbers have no check digit.
- The Disneyland Resort Line's cars (Wikipedia: P cars 22.85 m with the cab, Q cars
  22.0 m with the pantograph, three doors a side) keep the urban cars' bogies,
  height and roof; their door spacing and the number of Mickey-head windows (as many
  as fit at 1.45 m) are scaled from the photographs.
