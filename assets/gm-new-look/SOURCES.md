## Published figures

- GM New Look: in service from 1959; 12.2 m long, 2.59 m wide, 3.05 m high; 2 axles, at 2.1, 9.34 m from the nose (approx., read off the photographs where not published); 2 doors on the modelled side; about 70 passengers (approx.); 10.4 t empty, 175 kW, 90 km/h (approx.); liveries or colours: TTC, NYCTA. Source: Wikipedia "GM New Look bus" and the photographs.
