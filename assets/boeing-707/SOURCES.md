## Published figures

- Boeing 707-320 (Wikipedia "Boeing 707"): in service from 1959; length 46.61 m, wingspan 44.42 m, height 12.93 m; fuselage about 3.76 m wide (approx.); about 189 seats (typical layout, approx.); maximum take-off mass about 151 t; total thrust about 320 kN; cruising speed about 960 km/h.
- Positions of the wing, engines, tail, gear legs, doors and windows are read off the photographs and published three-view drawings (approx.); the livery colours are approximations of the carriers' schemes.
