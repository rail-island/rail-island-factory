## Published figures

- Transtech Artic (Wikipedia "Škoda Artic" and the operators' published data): in service from 2013; 27.6 m long over the bodies in 3 sections, 2.4 m wide, 3.6 m to the roof (approx.); 160 passengers with standing room (approx.); 80 km/h, 520 kW, 41 t (approx.); track gauge 1000 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
