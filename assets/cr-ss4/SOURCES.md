## Published figures

- Wikipedia (en "China Railways SS4", zh "SS4 article"): 2 x 16,416 mm over the coupler centres (32,832 mm),
  15,200 mm underframe, 3,100 mm wide, 4,040 mm to the pantograph seat, 2 x 92 t, 6,400 kW, 100 km/h, wheels 1,250 mm
  new (1,200 mm half worn), Bo-Bo+Bo-Bo; a single-arm pantograph behind each cab, the main breaker on the
  transformer room's roof. Bogie centres (8.7 m) and wheelbase (2.9 m) measured, not published.
- Running number: SS4 1072 (Zhuzhou-built SS4G), sections 1072A (front) and 1072B (rear), from the three-quarter photograph.
