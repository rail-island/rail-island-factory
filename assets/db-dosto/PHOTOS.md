# DB Regio double-deck coach - reference photos

One parametric model, `variant` firstClass, secondClass, drivingTrailer (vlakosimdb db_ds_re1, db_ds_re2, db_ds_re2_s).

Prototype: the Goerlitz double-deck coaches of DB Regio (built by VEB Waggonbau Goerlitz, later DWA and Bombardier) in the verkehrsrot DB Regio livery, and the driving trailer with the angular head of classes 761/762. Sources: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppelstockwagen and https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardier-Doppelstockwagen .

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (through `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85).
