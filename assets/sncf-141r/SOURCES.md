# SNCF 141 R source references

**Code** - `generator/fr141r.ts`, `generator/worldSteamLocos.ts`
**See also** - [the reference](../../docs/models/france-trains.md)

## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons at 1280 px wide. The model is **141 R 1199** as preserved
(Baldwin 73048 of 1947, oil-fired, of the 1101 - 1340 batch: Boxpok drivers, cast frame, Kylchap
chimney, "Nord" smokebox door; green with red lining). Photographs of other engines inform only
what the class shares (568 and 840 are in the service black; 568 is of the first batch, with
spoked drivers and a coal tender; 840 and 1244 carry the same door and chimney).

## Published figures

- **Figures** - French Wikipedia, [141 R](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/141_R) (infobox and the characteristics table) and English Wikipedia, [SNCF Class 141R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNCF_Class_141R): 14.639 m over the engine, 24.13 m with the tender (30 R tender 9.49 m); drivers 1,651 mm, pony wheels 914 mm, tender wheels 1,067 mm; cylinders 597 x 711 mm; 116 t in service (80 t adhesive), tender 75 t; 2,150 kW at 80 km/h; 100 km/h; Boxpok wheels, roller bearings and cast frames on 1101 - 1340; 141 R 1199 Baldwin 73048 (1947), oil-fired.
- The coupled wheelbase (3 x 1.676 m) and the pony and trailing axles' places are measured from photo-141-r-568-a.jpg (no published figure found).
