# Texture library

The photo sets every surface draws with (docs/models/surfaces.md): albedo (sRGB), OpenGL normal
and roughness maps at 512 x 512, fetched and resized by `pnpm exec tsx generator/fetchTextures.ts`.
All are CC0 (public domain); no attribution is required, the slugs are recorded here.
