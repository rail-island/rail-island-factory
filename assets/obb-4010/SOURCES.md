## Published figures

de.wikipedia "OBB 4010" (https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%96BB_4010): 149.12 m over the buffers in six
cars (power car 16.82 m, intermediate cars 26.4 m, driving trailer 26.7 m), 283 t, Bo'Bo' power car,
2,500 kW (hourly), 150 km/h, 29 sets built 1965 - 1978 by SGP; the formation (4010 power car with
luggage and guard's compartments and no seats, 7010 open second, 7110 compartment cars, 7310 dining or
buffet car, 6010 driving trailer with luggage compartment and first-class saloon); seats 66 first,
174 second, 34 dining; the liveries: sapphire blue and ivory with red-framed lamps (1965), ultramarine
and ivory with the Pflatsch (1970s), traffic red, umbra grey and grey-white (Valousek design, 1990s).
No blood orange scheme is recorded for the class. Height and width are not published there: the 2.83 m
width and 3.85 m crown are estimates from the head-on photographs. Running numbers shown: 4010 027-3 and
6010 012-0 (blueIvory), 4010 008-3 and 6010 008-8 (trafficRed).
