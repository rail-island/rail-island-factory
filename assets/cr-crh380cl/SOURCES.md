## Published figures

CRH380CL (Wikipedia "China Railway CRH3", zh "CRH380C"): CNR Changchun, 2012, 16 cars (8 motor cars),
403 m long against the CRH380BL's 399.27 m - the 3.73 m drawn as 1.85 m more nose at each end (end car
27.525 m), the intermediate cars the CRH3C's 24.775 m; 3.265 m wide (drawn 3.1 m, the UIC gauge),
3.89 m high; 19,200 kW; 380 km/h design, 350 km/h in service; 1,061 seats. The model offers 2 - 8 cars
(the series kit's longest train), with the CRH3C's pantograph cars.
