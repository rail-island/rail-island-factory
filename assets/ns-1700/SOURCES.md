## Published figures

- **German Wikipedia**, [NS-Baureihe 1700](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS-Baureihe_1700) and [NS-Baureihe 1600](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS-Baureihe_1600): 17,640 mm over the buffers (both classes), 3,047 mm wide, bogie centres 9,694 mm, wheelbase 2,800 mm, wheels 1,250 mm, B'B' with two monomotor bogies, 4,540 kW, 83 t (1600) / 86 t (1700), 180 km/h by design (in service 160 km/h, 140 for the 1600s in freight); the nine DB Cargo Nederland 1600s repainted traffic red with the DB emblem from 2012.
- **English Wikipedia**, [NS Class 1700](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS_Class_1700): power, top speed and first year as the design states them (`generator/nlLocos.ts`, `generator/openttdTable.ts`, [netherlands-trains.md](../../docs/models/netherlands-trains.md)).
- The model's body is 3.0 m wide, 47 mm under the published width, so the handrails stay inside the 3.1 m loading gauge.
