## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "Budd Rail Diesel Car": 85 ft long, 10 ft wide, two 275 hp diesels, 85 mph, 90 seats (RDC-1), built 1949 - 1962.
- **CB drawing** - 25,908 mm over the coupling points, 18,136 mm truck centres, 838 mm wheels, 3,067 mm over the fluted panels, roof 3,980 mm, 4,445 mm over the canopy and exhausts; window glass 1,397 x 628 mm.
- **Side layout** - the engineer sits on the right at each end, so each side has his window, a fluted pier and a door at one end and a door at the corner at the other (B&M 6211, AEAX 6202, Reading 9163). The Danbury car seems to show a door at the corner on the engineer's side; taken as an exception.
