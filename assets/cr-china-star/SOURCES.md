## Published figures

- Power car 21,700 mm long, Bo-Bo, 4,800 kW continuous: Jean-Marc Allenbach's sheet (documents.epfl.ch/users/a/al/allenbac/www/DJ0.htm).
- Power car 78 t (axle load 19.5 t), the 5.5 m head, one Stemmann DSA380 single-arm pantograph a power car, nine trailers (two first class, six second class, a bar car), the 25DT trailers 3.3 m wide: Wikipedia (zh) DJJ2.
- 726 passengers, 270 km/h design, 321.5 km/h record on 27 November 2002: Wikipedia (en, zh).
- The running number DJJ2 0001B: the power car at the China Railway Museum (photographs).
