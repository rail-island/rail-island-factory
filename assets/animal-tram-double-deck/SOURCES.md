## Published figures

- Horse Tram (Wikipedia "Horsecar" and the operators' published data): in service from 1880; 6.4 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2 m wide, 2.6 m to the roof (approx.); 40 passengers with standing room (approx.); 10 km/h, 1 kW, 2.8 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
