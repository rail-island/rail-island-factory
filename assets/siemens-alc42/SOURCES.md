## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "Siemens Charger": 71.5 ft (21.79 m), 10 ft wide, 14.4 ft high, truck centres 40.8 ft, 9.8 ft truck wheelbase, 46 - 49 in wheels, 260,000 lb, 4,200 hp (ALC-42), 125 mph; first revenue service 8 February 2022 (the SC-44 in 2017).
- **Running numbers** - Phase VII as 363 (photographed), Midwest as 4610 (photographed).
- **Measured** - 363 at the cab (70 px/m): door 1.71 - 2.45 m behind the tip, 1.42 - 3.48 m high; the red and white foot stripe 1.17 - 1.38 m; the side's crease 3.68 m. 304 (59 px/m): cab side window 0.93 - 1.40 m, intakes 4.82 - 5.43, 6.14 - 7.36 and 11.75 - 15.15 m, radiator fans over 11.8 - 15.2 m, exhaust 7.5 - 8.8 m. The right side (363) has no louvred intake and no filter panel.
- **Paint** - the schemes simplified to colour fields, stripes and plain letters; no
  heralds or logos are drawn.
