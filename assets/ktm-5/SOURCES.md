## Published figures

- KTM-5 (Wikipedia "71-605" and the operators' published data): in service from 1969; 15 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.6 m wide, 3.05 m to the roof (approx.); 129 passengers with standing room (approx.); 75 km/h, 180 kW, 20 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
