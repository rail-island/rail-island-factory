## Published figures

- Montpellier Citadis 302 (Wikipedia "Montpellier tramway" and the operators' published data): in service from 2000; 32.6 m long over the bodies in 5 sections, 2.65 m wide, 3.3 m to the roof (approx.); 220 passengers with standing room (approx.); 70 km/h, 520 kW, 40 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
