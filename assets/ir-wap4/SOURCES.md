## Measurements

- **Published figures** (Wikipedia, "Indian locomotive class WAP-4"): length over buffers 18.794 m,
  width 3.18 m, height 4.165 m, Co-Co, wheels 1,092 mm, 140 km/h; the design keeps its game figures.
- **Side photo** (Secunderabad): the body spans 1,170 px for about 17.7 m (66 px/m along the body;
  the image is squeezed vertically, so heights use the 0.25 m stripe, about 60 px/m). Eleven bays,
  pitch 78 px (1.18 m), windows and louvres about 0.8 m wide, the first bay's centre 6.1 m behind
  the centre; the cab doors 6.8 - 7.4 m and 7.1 - 7.8 m from the centre, a small window ahead of
  the door at one end only; the pantographs' centres at 0.27 and 0.74 of the body (about 4.2 m
  either side of the centre); the face's top corner 0.6 - 0.75 m behind its foot.
- **Face** (AJJ 22829, about 141 px/m across the face): windscreens 3.02 - 3.80 m high, each about
  1.15 m wide either side of a 0.15 m pillar; stripe 2.06 - 2.31 m; marker housings at 0.8 - 1.05 m
  from the centre line, 2.1 - 2.6 m high; red to 1.72 m, dark below; plough from 0.15 to 0.86 m.
- **Bogies** are hidden in the side photo: bogie centres 11.0 m and axles 1.9 m apart are estimates.
