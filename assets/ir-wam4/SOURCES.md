## Published figures

Indian locomotive class WAM-4 (Wikipedia): CLW, from 1971; 25 kV 50 Hz AC; Co-Co; length over buffers
18.794 m; width about 3.18 m; wheels 1,092 mm; cast trimount bogies; 113 t; 2,700 kW (3,640 hp); 120 km/h.
Bogie centres (10.0 m) and the trimount's axle gaps (2.15 m inner, 1.88 m outer, as the WDM-2's) are
estimates from the side photographs, not published figures.
