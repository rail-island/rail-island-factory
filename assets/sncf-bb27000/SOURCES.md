## Published figures

- **fr.wikipedia, [BB 27000](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/BB_27000)** (infobox after D. Redoutey, *Le matériel moteur de la SNCF*, La Vie du Rail 2007): 2.857 m wide, 4.31 m high, 90 t, 4,200 kW, 140 km/h, pivots 10.06 m, bogie wheelbase 2.6 m, wheels 1,150 mm, one pantograph for 25 kV and one for 1.5 kV. Its length (19.72 m) is not used: the frame of 427020 is painted 19.52 m, the length the model keeps.
- **en.wikipedia, [SNCF Class BB 27000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNCF_Class_BB_27000)**: first year (2001).
