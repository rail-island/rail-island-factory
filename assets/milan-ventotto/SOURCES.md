## Published figures

- Milan Ventotto (Wikipedia "ATM Class 1500" and the operators' published data): in service from 1928; 13.6 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.35 m wide, 3.35 m to the roof (approx.); 130 passengers with standing room (approx.); 50 km/h, 160 kW, 16.5 t (approx.); track gauge 1445 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
