## Dimensions

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 14 590 mm |
| Width | 2 550 mm |
| Height | 2 850 mm (without air conditioning) |
| Wheelbases | 6 800 mm (front axle to driven second axle) + 1 690 mm (second axle to the trailing third axle) |
| Front / rear overhang | 2 700 / 3 400 mm (2 700 + 6 800 + 1 690 + 3 400 = 14 590, adds up) |
| Doors | 3 doors in the right side, usually 2-2-2 (also offered 1-2-0, 2-2-0, 2-2-1) |
| Floor | low floor over the whole length, 320 mm at doors 1 and 2, 340 mm at door 3; kneeling about 70 mm |
| Engine | at the rear, standing in a tower (low-floor version); DAF or MAN engines |
| Built | 1999-2018: Mk1 1999-2002, Mk2 2002-2005, Mk3 2005-2018 |
| Notes | a later table column gives 14 890 mm with 7 000 + 1 690 mm wheelbases and 2 750 / 3 450 mm overhangs (variant not labelled); tyre size not given |

The third axle is a trailing axle (rigid or actively steered, depending on the variant).

## Native vlakosimdb files

`native/solaris_urbino_15/` - vlakosimdb `db/motocar` at commit `b170acf` (`#` in a folder name becomes `_`):
`index.xml`, the model XML as `model.xml` and the original BMPs (10 pixels a metre;
`x_inc` the front, `x_dec` the rear, `z_inc` / `z_dec` the right and left sides,
`y_dec` the underside). The XML gives the shell's outlines; the BMPs the doors,
windows and paint; the photographs and dimensions above everything the textures
cannot resolve (docs/models/buses.md).
