## Published figures

es.wikipedia "Serie 102 de Renfe": 200 m train of 2 power cars and 12 Talgo 350 coaches (M-12R-M),
width 2.942 m, height 3.365 m (power cars 4.0 m), 8,000 kW (8 x 1,020 kW three-phase motors),
330 km/h in service, 322 t, 334 seats. Power car 20.0 m, coach 13.14 m (en.wikipedia, the line
scans agree). Bogie centres (10.8 m), wheelbase (2.65 m) and the power car's wheel (1.0 m) are
measured on the line scan, not published.
