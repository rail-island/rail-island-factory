- Sukhoi Superjet 100 (Wikipedia "Yakovlev SJ-100 (Sukhoi Superjet 100)"): in service from 2011; length 29.94 m, wingspan 27.8 m, height 10.28 m; fuselage about 3.46 m wide (approx.); about 98 seats (typical layout, approx.); maximum take-off mass about 49.45 t; total thrust about 154 kN; cruising speed about 830 km/h.
