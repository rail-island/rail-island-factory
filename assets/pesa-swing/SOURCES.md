## Published figures

- Pesa Swing (Wikipedia "Pesa Swing" and the operators' published data): in service from 2011; 33.4 m long over the bodies in 5 sections, 2.4 m wide, 3.5 m to the roof (approx.); 220 passengers with standing room (approx.); 70 km/h, 400 kW, 41 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
