## Published figures

- **Figures** - Wikipedia, [DHR B Class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DHR_B_Class): 0-4-0ST, 2 ft gauge, 26 in drivers, two outside 11 x 14 in cylinders, 14 long tons, built 1889 - 1925. The 5 ft 6 in wheelbase and the 5.75 m length over the couplers are measured on 19B's photograph (about 205 px/m along the engine) and the three-quarter views, scaled to the drivers.
