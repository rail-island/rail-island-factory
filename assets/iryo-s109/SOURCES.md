## Figures

FS ETR 1000 (it.wikipedia "Frecciarossa 1000", de.wikipedia "FS ETR 1000"; en.wikipedia "Frecciarossa 1000"):
202.02 m in eight cars, 2,924 mm wide, 4,080 mm high, bogie centres 17,400 mm, wheelbase 2,850 mm,
wheels 920 mm, 461 t ready to run (501 t in service), 9,800 kW under 25 kV, 360 km/h, 457 + 2 seats,
28 doors. Cars DM1 - TT2 - M3 - T4 - T5 - M6 - TT7 - DM8: the 25 kV pantographs on T4 and T5, the
3 kV ones on TT2 and TT7. Car lengths are not published in these sources: 27.6 m for an end car and
24.47 m for an intermediate car follow from the photographs (the projective fit that gives the leading
bogie the published 2.85 m wheelbase) and the 202 m total.
