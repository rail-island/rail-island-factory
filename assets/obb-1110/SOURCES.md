## Published figures

German Wikipedia, "ÖBB 1010" and "ÖBB 1110" (infoboxes): 17.86 m over buffers, 3.09 m wide, 4.26 m high,
bogie centres 8.6 m, bogie wheelbase 4.1 m, wheels 1,300 mm, Co'Co', 4,000 kW, 280 kN; the 1010 106-109.8 t
and 130 km/h, the 1110 106 t (the 1110.500 114 t) and 110 km/h; two scissor pantographs as built, the ten
1110.500 (1974-75) a rheostatic brake with resistors over the cabs and single-arm pantographs.
