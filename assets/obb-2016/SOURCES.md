## Figures

de.wikipedia "OBB 2016" (infobox): 19,275 mm over the buffers, 2,870 mm wide, bogie centres
10,362 mm, wheelbase 2,700 mm, wheels 1,100 mm (new), 80 t, 2,000 kW (MTU 16V 4000 R41),
140 km/h, 100 built 2002 - 2004 (2016 001 - 100). These override the photo estimates. The
running number 2016 038-8 is legible on `photo-austria-2016-01.jpg` (its check digit verified).
The ER20 photographs (RTS, GKB, Beacon Rail, ER 20-006) inform the body only; the paint is
from the three OBB photographs.
