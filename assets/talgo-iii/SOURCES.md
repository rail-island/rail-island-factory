## Published figures

- Javier Rosello Iglesias, "Adios al Talgo III, el final de una epoca", Via Libre (https://vialibre-ffe.com/pdf/ADIOS_AL_TALGO_III[1].pdf): boxes of 11.1 m (Talgo II 6 m); riveted aluminium skin ribbed lengthwise on roof and sides except the window band; each car its own vestibule and doors, the saloon between the vestibule and the toilet / air-conditioning block; separate wheel units ("rodales") at the joints; the set framed by a one-axle van and a two-axle second-class tail car; 140 km/h at first, later 160 km/h.
- Not found: the cars' height and width and the wheel diameter; the height (3.4 m) is the photographs' over the 11.1 m box, the width kept at the Talgo family's 2.94 m.
