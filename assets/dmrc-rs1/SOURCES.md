## Published figures

- en.wikipedia "Delhi Metro" (November 2026): Phase I stock (Red, Yellow, Blue Lines) by Hyundai
  Rotem, Mitsubishi Corporation and MELCO, later BEML; **broad gauge 1,676 mm**, 25 kV 50 Hz
  overhead, 3.2 m wide stainless-steel cars, four sliding doors a side, four-car trains extended to
  six and eight cars. The car lengths (21.84 m driving trailer, 21.34 m intermediate car over the
  couplers) are the commonly quoted figures; the side photograph's door pitch agrees with them.
- en.wikipedia "MTR Rotem EMU" (the K-stock sister design): 860 mm wheels, 2.5 m bogie wheelbase,
  3.698 m roof height without air conditioners.

## Notes

- The front photographs are of Blue and Yellow Line units; the RS1 front is the same on all three
  lines, only the stripe's colour and the line number differ. The model shows the Red Line: a red
  stripe and the number 1 on the door.
- Which cars carry the pantograph is not visible in these photographs: the model raises it on the
  motor cars next to the driving trailers.
