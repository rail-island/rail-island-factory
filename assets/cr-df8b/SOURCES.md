## Published figures

From the Chinese Wikipedia article on the DF8B (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/DF8B, technical section, citing the Qishuyan works'
book and operating manual): 22,000 mm over the coupler centres, frame 20,900 mm, body 3,304 mm wide
and 4,736 mm high; Co-Co, bogie wheelbase 2 x 1,800 mm, 1,050 mm wheels; 138 t (150 t with the
ballast plates on the side sills); 3,100 kW rated (16V280ZJA, 3,680 kW installed); 100 km/h; the
windscreens leaning back 15 degrees, the lower face 20 degrees, a 300 mm lamp band standing forward;
the rooms in order cab 1, electrical, power, cooling (two 1,600 mm fans), auxiliary, cab 2.
