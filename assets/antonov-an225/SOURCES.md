## Published figures

- Antonov An-225 Mriya (Wikipedia "Antonov An-225 Mriya"): in service from 1988; length 84 m, wingspan 88.4 m, height 18.1 m; fuselage about 7.3 m wide (approx.); about 250 t payload; maximum take-off mass about 640 t; total thrust about 1380 kN; cruising speed about 800 km/h.
