## Published figures

- Stadler Tango (Wikipedia "Stadler Tango" and the operators' published data): in service from 2009; 43.75 m long over the bodies in 4 sections, 2.3 m wide, 3.5 m to the roof (approx.); 280 passengers with standing room (approx.); 70 km/h, 1000 kW, 60 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
