## Published figures

ja.wikipedia "CRH380A" (with en/zh): end car 26,500 mm, intermediate car 25,000 mm, body 3,380 mm wide
(drawn 3.26 m, the Shinkansen gauge's 3.3 m), 3,700 mm high; the nose 12.0 m long (the CRH2A's 9.4 m);
860 mm wheels, 2,500 mm bogie wheelbase; DSA350 single-arm pantographs on cars 4 and 6 of eight, normally
only the rear one raised, with large side shields on both sides; 6M2T, 9,600 kW.
