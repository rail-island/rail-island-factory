## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - ja.wikipedia "小田急70000形電車" (after Nippon Sharyo and SUBWAY 2018/5): 7-car sets of
  bogie cars (not articulated: Odakyu dropped the articulated Romancecar for platform doors), end cars
  21,300 mm, intermediate cars 20,000 mm (142.6 m a set), width 2,878 mm, roof 3,700 mm, 4,057 mm over the
  raised cab, floor 1,150 mm, side windows 1,000 mm tall, wheels 860 mm (motor) and 810 mm, wheelbase
  2,100 mm, single-arm pantographs on cars 2, 4 and 6; 268.7 t a set, 4 motor cars of 4 x 190 kW; 110 km/h.
- **Colours** - the same article: body "rose vermilion" (#d04b26), roof "rouge bordeaux" (#9f3042), a
  "vermilion orange" line under the windows (#ef2300), "moonlight silver" underframe covers (#d8d8da).
  The model's body and line follow the sunlit photographs, where the published swatch reads brown.
- **LSE colours** - ja.wikipedia "小田急7000形電車": vermilion orange (#f30a03), white and grey (#6a6a6a),
  two white lines low on the body. The GSE never carried them; the livery is the model's alternative.
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
