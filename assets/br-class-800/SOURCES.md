## Published figures

Driving cars 25.85 m and intermediate cars 26.0 m over the couplers, 2.7 m wide, five-car set 129.7 m,
formation DPTS-MS-MS-MC-DPTF (pantographs on the driving cars, generator units under the motor cars):
en.wikipedia "British Rail Class 800", after Hitachi Review 63 (2014) 646. The crown (3.65 m) and the
heights along the side are measured on the photographs.
