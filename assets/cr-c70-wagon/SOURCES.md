## Figures

- C70 (CRRC, 2006): 13,976 mm over the couplers, 13,000 x 2,892 x 2,050 mm inside, 3,242 mm wide, 3,143 mm high,
  bogie centres 9,210 mm, K6 (or K5) bogies of 1,830 mm wheelbase and 840 mm wheels, 23.6 t tare, 70 t load,
  77 m3 (the tare, load, volume and length factor confirmed by the load marks on the photographed wagons).
- P70: about 17 m over the couplers (length factor 1.6 in its load marks; the model keeps 17.0 m), 16.1 x 2.8 x 2.8 m
  inside, 145 m3, 24.6 t tare, 70 t load (load marks). Its bogie centres (12.0 m), eaves (4.0 m) and roof rise (0.6 m)
  are estimates from the photographs and the inside measures: no published drawing was found.
