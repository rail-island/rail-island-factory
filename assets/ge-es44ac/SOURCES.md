## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "GE Evolution Series" and American-Rails "ES44AC": 73 ft 2 in (22.30 m), 15 ft 5 in over the cab, 10 ft 3 in wide, 42 in wheels, 416,000 - 432,000 lb, 4,400 hp, 75 mph. Truck centres 50 ft 4 in between bolster centres and a 13 ft 2 in truck wheelbase from the CIT Rail AC4400CW specification sheet (same frame and length; https://www.firstcitizens.com/content/dam/firstcitizens/pdfs/commercial/rail/ac4400cw-locomotive.pdf); The Diesel Shop's ES44DC sheet gives 53 ft 0 in, which would put the leading wheels under the pilot steps.
- **Running numbers** - illustrative numbers in each railroad's ES44AC series (BNSF 5800, UP 5348, CSX 850).
- **Paint** - the railroads' schemes as colour fields, stripes and the railroad's
  name in plain letters, with their emblems in simple shapes (the UP wings, shield
  and flag, the BNSF swoosh, the CSX flash); no heralds or logos are traced.
