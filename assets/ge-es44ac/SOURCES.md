## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "GE Evolution Series" and American-Rails "ES44AC": 73 ft 2 in (22.30 m), 15 ft 5 in over the cab, 10 ft 3 in wide, 42 in wheels, 416,000 - 432,000 lb, 4,400 hp, 75 mph. Truck centres 53 ft 0 in from The Diesel Shop's ES44DC sheet (same frame).
- **Running numbers** - illustrative numbers in each railroad's ES44AC series (BNSF 5800, UP 5348, CSX 850).
- **Paint** - the railroads' schemes simplified to colour fields, stripes and the
  railroad's name in plain letters; no heralds or logos are drawn.
