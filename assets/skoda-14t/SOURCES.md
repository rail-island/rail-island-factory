## Published figures

- Skoda 14T (Wikipedia "Škoda 14 T" and the operators' published data): in service from 2005; 31.2 m long over the bodies in 5 sections, 2.46 m wide, 3.46 m to the roof (approx.); 202 passengers with standing room (approx.); 60 km/h, 540 kW, 39 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
