# CSD Bi20 - reference photos

Prototype: uncertain. A short two-axle compartment coach with a side door to every compartment and open end platforms: a CSD Ci (later Bi) of former kkStB/Austrian compartment design, as stated in the brief; not verified against a source.

No photo saved. The CSD Ci coaches on Commons (Ci 4-2262, Ci 3-9425, the Luzna museum Ci) are open-saloon coaches with end-platform doors only, not compartment coaches, so they are the wrong type. No free photo of a CSD/kkStB two-axle compartment coach with per-compartment doors was found.

## Type reference (added later)
