## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "Pennsylvania Railroad class GG1": 79 ft 6 in over the coupler faces, 10 ft 4 in wide, 15 ft 0 in over the locked-down pantographs, 57 in drivers, 36 in guiding wheels, rigid wheelbase 13 ft 8 in (two 6 ft 10 in spaces), total wheelbase 69 ft 0 in, 475,000 lb, 4,620 hp continuous, 100 mph, from 1935.
- **Running numbers** - 4935 (Brunswick green, the preserved unit), 4913 (Tuscan red, photographed), 4879 (Penn Central, photographed).

## Measured

Scales from the driver spacing (6 ft 10 in) on the prototype's side photograph (about 53 px/m in the original) and
the coupler-to-coupler length of the N scale model (about 64 px/m); heights cross-checked on 4935's close-up.

- **Axles from the centre** - drivers 1.48, 3.56, 5.64 m; guiding truck 8.05 and 10.52 m (the inner drivers 2.9 m
  apart across the frames' hinge; the outer guiding axle fixed by the 69 ft wheelbase).
- **Heights** - the body's foot 1.22 m, the shelf 2.85 m, the hoods' crown 3.86 m, the cab's roof 4.15 m; the five
  stripes 2.24 - 2.56 m, 0.08 m apart, 0.045 m wide; the lettering's foot 1.62 m, 0.3 m high.
- **Along the body** - the end's face 0.75 m behind the coupler face (the deck and pilot ahead of it), the cab's front
  wall 5.9 m from each end; the stripes leave the level 1.55 - 2.35 m from the end and meet at its lower corner; the
  pantographs' centres 3.4 m from each end.

## Paint

The schemes as colour fields, stripes and plain stroke letters; the keystone and the Penn Central logo are drawn as
simple shapes.
