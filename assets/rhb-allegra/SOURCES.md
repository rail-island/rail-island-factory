## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons at 1280 px wide (the files the class's de.wikipedia article shows).
The model carries the running number of the Pontresina photograph, **3502** "Friedrich Hennings".
Positions along the cars were read with a projective fit along each car's side (equal window pitch).
