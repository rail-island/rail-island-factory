## Published figures

- Budweis-Linz horse railway (Pferdeeisenbahn Budweis-Linz-Gmunden): opened between Ceske Budejovice and Linz on 1 August 1832, the first public railway on the European continent; track gauge 1106 mm; horse-drawn throughout, about 10-12 km/h in service.
- The coach "Hannibal" (preserved, Linz / Ceske Budejovice museums, the second-class "II" coach of the photographs): a stagecoach body on a four-wheel railway frame, one closed compartment with a door each side, an outside seat under a leather hood at each end (the coachman's at the front, the conductor's at the rear), luggage on the roof inside a railing.
- Measured from the side view of the model photograph at about 150 px/m (the horse's withers about 1.6 m): wheel diameter 0.86 m, wheelbase 1.5 m, body 2.5 m long, the lower body's bottom 0.95 m and the waist 1.78 m above the rail, roof 2.95-3.05 m, railing to 3.25 m, luggage to 3.35 m; 4.25 m over the hood mouths and the draw hooks, 4.4 m over the scrolled end irons (approx.).
- Width 1.6 m over the body, 2.0 m over the hood mouths (approx.); about 8 passengers inside and 4 outside (approx.); 1.8 t empty (approx.); its own limit 15 km/h (approx.).
- Estimates (approx.): the frame, springs, scrolled end irons, the hood's shape and the colours are read off the photographs; the frame is drawn outside the wheels.
