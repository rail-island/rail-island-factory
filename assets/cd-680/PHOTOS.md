# CD 680 Pendolino - reference photos

One parametric model, `veh-cd-680`: `unit` endCar (vlakosimdb cd_681), car683, car685, car686 (cd_683, cd_685, cd_686).

Prototype: Alstom (Fiat Ferroviaria) Pendolino, CD class 680, seven tilting seven-car units built 2003 to 2005 (681, 081, 683, 084, 684, 082, 682); 185.3 m over the couplers, end cars 27.7 m and intermediate cars 25.9 m, 2.8 m wide, up to 8 degrees of tilt (cs.wikipedia "Elektricka jednotka 680", de.wikipedia "CD-Baureihe 680", en.wikipedia "CD Class 680").

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (rendered by `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and share-alike (SA).
