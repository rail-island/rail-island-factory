## Published figures

- Tatra T2 (Wikipedia "Tatra T2" and the operators' published data): in service from 1955; 15.2 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.5 m wide, 3.06 m to the roof (approx.); 100 passengers with standing room (approx.); 55 km/h, 160 kW, 16.5 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
