## Published figures

- London knifeboard omnibus (London General Omnibus Company, Thomas Tilling and others; Wikipedia "Horsebus", "Knifeboard"): the knifeboard roof seat from 1847, in service until the garden-seat buses replaced it in the 1880s; 12 inside and 14 on the knifeboard, 2 beside the driver (approx. 26-28 seats); a pair of horses; about 1.4 t empty (approx.), 10-12 km/h in service.
- Read off the Holborn Viaduct photograph (1869), scaled on the horse (about 1.6 m at the withers): rear wheels about 1.45 m, front wheels about 1.0 m (ratio 0.7), wheelbase about 2 m, saloon about 3.1 m long with six small windows a side, roof about 2.3 m, knifeboard back about 3.0 m, the box seat at roof height over the front wheels (approx.).
- Estimates (approx.): the model is 4.4 m long over the platform and the dash, 1.9 m wide, 3.05 m high; the cut-under of the lower saloon for the front wheels, the curved ladder and the colours (maroon body, cream advertising boards, red wheels) follow the photographs and period drawings.
