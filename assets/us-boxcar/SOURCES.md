## Measurements

- **RBOX 33646** (side-on): body ends at 27.5 and 932.5 px, taken as 15.8 m: 57.3 px/m. Posts 0.87 m apart, 7 each
  side of the door from 1.81 m off the middle; door 3.23 m wide (10 ft opening), 1.0 to 4.27 m over the rail; eave
  4.44 m; side sill 0.87 to 1.04 m; side ladder rungs about 1.1, 1.57, 2.04, 2.53 m. Lettering: "rail" x-height 0.23 m
  at 3.63 m, "box" 0.19 m at 3.25 m, "RB OX" 0.31 m and "33 646" 0.26 m centred 5.24 m left of the middle; the X logo
  0.58 by 0.47 m centred 4.92 m right of the middle at 3.57 m, "next load" and "any road" either side.
- **SP 245516** (from above, slight perspective): body 997 px for 15.8 m, 63 px/m along the side; heights by fraction
  of the side (sill to eave). Two door leaves 162 px (2.57 m) each, a 16 ft opening; 7 posts each side of the door
  about 0.775 m apart from 2.66 m off the middle; "SOUTHERN" 3.2 to 3.8 m and "PACIFIC" 2.45 to 3.03 m centred
  5.1 m right; "HYDRA-CUSHION" 3.36 to 3.92 m, 4.45 m long, centred 5.37 m left; roof in 10 panels.

## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - a 50 ft (inside) 70-ton boxcar, about 55 ft 4 in over the couplers, truck centres 40 ft 6 in,
  Plate C extreme height 15 ft 1 in; 33 in wheels on a 5 ft 8 in truck wheelbase.
