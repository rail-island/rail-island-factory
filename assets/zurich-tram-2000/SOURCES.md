## Published figures

- Zurich Tram 2000 (Wikipedia "Tram 2000" and the operators' published data): in service from 1976; 21.65 m long over the bodies in 2 sections, 2.2 m wide, 3.3 m to the roof (approx.); 150 passengers with standing room (approx.); 65 km/h, 300 kW, 26 t (approx.); track gauge 1000 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
