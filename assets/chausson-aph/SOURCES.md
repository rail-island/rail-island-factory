## Published figures

- Chausson APH: in service from 1950; 9.65 m long, 2.5 m wide, 2.95 m high; 2 axles, at 1.1, 6 m from the nose (approx., read off the photographs where not published); 2 doors on the modelled side; about 60 passengers (approx.); 7 t empty, 77 kW, 70 km/h (approx.). Source: Wikipedia "Chausson (coachbuilder)" and the photographs.
