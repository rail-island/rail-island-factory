# CSD Bam - reference photos

Prototype: CSD Bam, bogie trailer coach in red/ivory with rounded ends and end gangway, procured as trailers for the M 284.0 / M 284.1 diesel railcars and also usable in locomotive-hauled trains (Commons description of File:I19_878_Bf_Eger,_Bam_5112.jpg by Falk2, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:I19_878_Bf_Eger,_Bam_5112.jpg). Builder and build years not confirmed (uncertain; the Commons category is https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:%C4%8CSD_Class_Bam). Not CD class 021 (that is the Btx 763 trailer). The lettering on Bam 5159 reads: 88 seats, brake weight 25 t, home depot DHV Luzna u Rakovnika.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and, for BY-SA, share-alike.
