## Published figures

Siemens Velaro E (Renfe class 103): eight cars, 200.32 m over the couplers, 2.95 m wide, 3.89 m high;
425 t empty, 8,800 kW, 350 km/h in service. Car lengths and bogie centres taken as the ICE 3's
(Velaro family), the end car 0.16 m longer to make up 200.32 m.

The running number is 103 019 (unit 19 in photo-s3274-bf-madrid-atocha-103-xxx.jpg).
