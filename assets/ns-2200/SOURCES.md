## Published figures

- **Figures** - Wikipedia, [NS 2200](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS_2200): 14.01 m long, 72 t, 662 kW (887 hp), 106 (later 100) km/h, 950 mm wheels, Bo'Bo', 1955 - 1958 (`generator/nl2200.ts`, `generator/nlLocos.ts`, `generator/openttdTable.ts`, [netherlands-trains.md](../../docs/models/netherlands-trains.md)). Heights and widths not published there are measured on the photographs (cab 2.9 m wide and 3.98 m high, hood 1.8 m wide and 3.6 m high, walkway 1.5 m).
