# TGV - reference photos

One parametric model, `veh-tgv`: `unit` powerCar (the default), endCoach, coach; `livery` pse (vlakosimdb tgv_loco, tgv_vag#1, tgv_vag#2, the default) and atlantique (tgv_at_loco, tgv_at_ch1, tgv_at_ch2).

Prototype: SNCF TGV Sud-Est (1978-1988) and TGV Atlantique (1988-1992): power cars 22.15 m over couplers, 2.814 m wide, 4.10 m high, 14.0 m bogie pivots, 3.0 m bogie wheelbase; articulated trailers 2.904 m wide, 3.42 m high, the end trailers 21.845 m and the intermediate ones 18.7 m between the Jacobs bogies (the PSE's 200.19 m and the Atlantique's 237.59 m sets; en.wikipedia "TGV Sud-Est", "TGV Atlantique", fr.wikipedia "TGV Sud-Est").

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (rendered by `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85).
