## Notes

- The horse is the former horse tram's bay Clydesdale, measured from the Victor Harbor photograph in
  `assets/animal-tram-double-deck/`; the donkey and the mule are drawn on its stations, scaled
  (0.64 and 0.86) and narrowed, with their own ears, manes and tails.
- The cow's yoke follows the Toledo ox team; the camel's breast band and pad the Pushkar camel cart;
  the elephant's proportions the Bandipur photograph (Asian elephant, shoulder about 2.6-2.9 m).
- Estimates (approx.): every measure is read off the photographs at a known withers height; the
  game figures (pace, power, weight) are reasoned, not measured (docs/models/draft-animals.md).
