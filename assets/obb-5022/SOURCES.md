## Published figures

de.wikipedia "ÖBB 5022": 41,700 mm over the couplers, 2,830 mm wide, B'2'B', 117 seats (10 tip-up),
2 x 315 kW (5022 001 - 020; 021 - 060 2 x 335 kW), 70.3 t, 120 km/h, built 2003 - 2008, in service from 2005,
Cityjet repaint from 2017. The body, face shape and window layout are the DB 642's (assets/db-642/).
