## Published figures

es.wikipedia "Serie 440 de Renfe" and de.wikipedia "Renfe-Baureihe 440": three cars M-R-Rc, 80.164 m over the
buffers (53.494 m for M + Rc), 2.90 m wide, 4.26 m high, 150.4 t empty (110 t for M + Rc), Bo'Bo' + 2'2' + 2'2',
4 x 290 kW = 1,160 kW on the motor car, 3 kV DC, 140 km/h, 260 seats (160 in M + Rc). Two single-arm pantographs on
the motor car, the knees outward on units 001 - 079 and inward on 080 - 253; every cab originally with a gangway door;
the windscreens lowered on all units after the first rebuild. Schemes of the unrebuilt 440: blue with a yellow band
(original), Cercanias (white, red, grey), Delta (Puigcerda), Regionales R1 (white with an orange band) and R2 (white
and orange, the 470's too).
