## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "Hankyu 9000 series": 19 m cars, 2.75 m wide, 115 km/h, from 2006. The roof at 3.65 m, floor and door sill 1.15 m, doors 1.3 x 1.85 m and the coupler at 0.88 m are the usual Japanese commuter-car figures the photographs agree with.
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
