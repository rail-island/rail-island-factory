## Published figures

German Wikipedia, "OBB 2043" (series 2043.05 - 77): length over buffers 15,760 mm, bogie centres
8,000 mm, bogie wheelbase 2,500 mm, wheels 950 mm, 70.0 t, 1,104 kW, 110 km/h; Jenbacher Werke,
1964 - 1977. The four pre-series locomotives were 14,760 mm long; the model is the series.
