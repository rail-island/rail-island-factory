## Published figures

Wikipedia "Indian locomotive class WAP-5" (infobox and the IRFCA specification table): length over buffers
18,162 mm, over headstocks 16,880 mm, body width 3,142 mm, height over the folded pantographs 4,237 mm,
bogie centres 10,200 mm, bogie wheelbase 2,800 mm, wheels 1,092 mm new, cab length 2,434 mm, Bo-Bo
Henschel Flexifloat bogies, fully suspended drive, two pantographs, 78 t.

## Choices

- The model is the later CLW unit as photographed (30164, its running number shown): the aerodynamic cab 1
  at +x, the flat cab 2 at -x. Most free photographs show the flat end (30022, 30026, 30035, 30080, 30099);
  the ABB and early CLW units have flat faces at both ends.
- The side photographs are all strongly foreshortened; side positions (doors, cab windows, the
  machine-room window about 3.2 m from the centre, diagonally opposite on the two sides) follow the
  2.434 m cab length and the photos' order of features, not a measurement.
- Gatimaan (`gatimaan`, unit 30086): no free photograph of the locomotive in that scheme was found; it keeps
  the white-red layout in blue with a yellow line.
