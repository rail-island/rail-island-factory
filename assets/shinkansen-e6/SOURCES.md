## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "E6 Series": end car 23.075 m, intermediate 20.5 m, 2.945 m wide, 3.65 m high, 13 m nose, 320 km/h, from 2013 on the Komachi.
- **Width** - the E6 keeps its own 2.945 m inside the Shinkansen gauge's 3.3 m.
- **Livery** - "akane red" upper part, "hiun white" lower part, "arrow silver" line between them.
