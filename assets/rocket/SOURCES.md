## Published figures

- **Figures** - Wikipedia, [Stephenson's Rocket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephenson%27s_Rocket): length, power, top speed and first year as the design states them (`generator/worldSteamLocos.ts`, `generator/openttdTable.ts`, [uk-trains.md](../../docs/models/uk-trains.md)).

Driving wheels 4 ft 8.5 in (1.435 m), trailing wheels 2 ft 6 in (0.76 m), wheelbase 7 ft 2 in
(2.18 m); boiler 6 ft (1.83 m) long, 3 ft 4 in (1.02 m) across; cylinders 8 in bore, 16.5 in
stroke, inclined at about 35 degrees as built in 1829 (Wikipedia, "Stephenson's Rocket").
The tender's 0.9 m wheels and 1.7 m wheelbase are read from the drawing.
