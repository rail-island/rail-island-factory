## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "Product tanker" and the photographs: a typical medium-range product tanker of about 50,000 dwt (approx.): 183 m long, 32.2 m beam, 12 m draught, 9,500 kW, 14.5 knots (27 km/h).
- Read off the photographs (approx.): moulded depth about 19 m; house aft from 0.07 to 0.235 of the length, five decks and the wheelhouse (about 3 m each), the wheelhouse narrower than the house (about 16 m of 25 m) with closed wings to the ship's sides; funnel top about 23 m over the deck; one hose crane each side by the manifold amidships; MP MR Tanker 2 and Iver Exact are both MR product tankers of 45,000-50,000 dwt.
