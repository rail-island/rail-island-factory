## Published figures

From the Chinese and English Wikipedia articles "东风11型柴油机车" and "China Railways DF11": 21.25 m over the couplers (20.15 m over the body), 3.304 m wide (drawn 3.0 m, inside the UIC gauge's 3.1 m with its handrails), 4.736 m high, Co-Co, wheels 1.05 m, bogie wheelbase 2 x 2.0 m, 138 t, engine 3,610 kW (16V280ZJA) and 3,040 kW at the rail, 170 km/h, built 1992 - 2005 by Qishuyan. Bogie centres are not published: 11.8 m is measured from DF11 0177's traction brackets.

Livery: every photograph of the class found (Beijing, Shanghai, Jinan and Nanchang depots, 2008 - 2017) shows the cream and royal blue scheme; the plan's "red and white" was not found, so the model paints blue and cream (the plan's `redWhite` livery key was never exported: the design has one livery and no parameter). The running number DF11 0010 is the unit of the Wikipedia articles' lead photograph. Both sides are drawn with the same layout (every side photograph shows the side with the three louvres toward cab 2).
