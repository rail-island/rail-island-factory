## Published figures

From the Wikipedia article "British Rail Class 378" (its infobox: Platform 5's British Railways
Locomotives & Coaching Stock 2012 and 2025, and TfL's Class 378 data sheet, FOI-0416-2223):
five cars, 101.35 m; driving cars 19.995 m, the others 19.660 m (bodies; the model's lengths
over the couplers add the Dellner couplers' gap); width 2.80 m, height 3.774 m, floor 1.151 m;
two 1.5 m double-leaf pocket doors a side a car; wheels 840 mm, bogie wheelbase 2.6 m, bogie
centres 14.173 m; 121 km/h (75 mph); 2,400 kW; weights 45.5 t (driving motor), 41.1 t (motor),
39.9 t (pantograph trailer), 212.8 t the unit; 186 fixed and 86 tip-up seats. The running number
378 204 is the unit in `photo-class-378-204-brondesbury-park.jpg`. The band heights are estimates
from the photographs.
