## Figures

zh.wikipedia "东风4型内燃机车" and en.wikipedia "China Railways DF4": 21,100 mm over the coupler centres, 3,309 mm wide, 4,755 mm high, 138 t, 1,050 mm wheels, 2 x 1,800 mm bogie wheelbase, engine 16V240ZJ(B) 2,430 kW (3,300 hp), 100 km/h freight and 120 km/h passenger gearing. The model is the DF4B (1984 on); both photographed sides are the same side, the other is drawn alike.
