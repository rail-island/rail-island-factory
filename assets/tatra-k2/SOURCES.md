## Published figures

- Tatra K2 (Wikipedia "Tatra K2" and the operators' published data): in service from 1966; 20.4 m long over the bodies in 2 sections, 2.5 m wide, 3.15 m to the roof (approx.); 157 passengers with standing room (approx.); 65 km/h, 160 kW, 22 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
