## Published figures

- **German Wikipedia, "DB-Baureihe 101"**: length over buffers 19,100 mm, bogie wheelbase 2,650 mm, wheel
  diameter 1,250 mm (new), Bo'Bo', 6,400 kW, 220 km/h, 84 t.
- **Measured** on the side-on photograph: bogie centres about 10.8 m apart (drawn 10.8 m), roof crown
  3.89 m, the side walls upright to 3.2 m, the grey skirt 0.58 - 1.32 m, the windscreen 2.54 - 3.2 m.
