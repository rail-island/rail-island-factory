placed within about half a metre.

## Figures

China EMU database (china-emu.cn, CR300AF parameters) and Wikipedia (en, zh): eight cars 208.95 m
(end cars 27.525 m from the tip, 27.2 m between the couplers; intermediate cars 25.65 m between the
couplers, 25.0 m bodies), 3.36 m wide, 4.05 m high, 17.8 m bogie centres, 2.5 m wheelbase, 920 mm
wheels, coupler 1.0 m up; 4M4T, 16 x 350 kW = 5,600 kW, 17 t maximum axle load, 250 km/h in service
(300 km/h design); pantographs (DSA250) on cars 3 and 6; 613 seats (car 1 48 first class, car 8 65,
the middle cars 90, 90, 77, 63, 90, 90). No published weight: the model's 48 t end cars and 50 t
middle cars are estimates from the axle load and the CR400AF's.
