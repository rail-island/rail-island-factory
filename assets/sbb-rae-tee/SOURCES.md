## Figures and running numbers

- de.wikipedia "SBB RAe TEE II": 149.76 m over couplers in six cars, 125.33 m in five (so the
  intermediate cars 24.43 m, the driving cars 26.0 m); 2.84 m wide; 4.21 m high; bogie centres 18.3 m,
  the machine car's 17.0 m on two A1A bogies (1110 mm driving, 940 mm carrying wheels); 2310 kW;
  160 km/h; 296 t in six cars, 259 t in five; formation driving car - (intermediate car from 1966) -
  machine car - dining car - intermediate car - driving car; four diamond pantographs, interlaced in
  pairs over the machine car's bogies.
- Running numbers: the TEE livery shows 1053 (the train SBB Historic runs in TEE colours, the
  Satigny, Silenen, Airolo and Como photographs); the grey livery 1051 (a RABe EC of 1988 - 1999).
