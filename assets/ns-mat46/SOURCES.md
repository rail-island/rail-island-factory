## Published figures

nl.wikipedia "Mat '46" (https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mat_%2746): the two-car unit (ELD2) 44.7 m long, Bo'2'Bo' with a Jacobs bogie under the joint, 89 t, 796 kW (four Heemaf TM 711 of 199 kW), 140 km/h allowed (125 km/h in service), Scharfenberg couplers, ABDk + Bk after 1956, 24 first and 80 second class seats and 21 folding seats; units 279, 281, 295 and 299 painted yellow from about 1970.
