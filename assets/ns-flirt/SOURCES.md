## Published figures

- **Figures** - nl.wikipedia, [NS FLIRT](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS_FLIRT) (citing Stadler's data sheet "Electric low-floor multiple-unit FLIRT for NS the Netherlands"): 63.2 m (three cars) and 80.7 m (four cars), end cars 22.85 m and middle cars 17.5 m, 2.82 m wide, 4.12 m high, Bo'(2)'(2)'Bo' and Bo'(2)'(2)'(2)'Bo', wheels 920 mm (motor) and 760 mm (Jacobs), floor 780 mm, doors 1.3 m (6 a side on three cars, 8 on four), 160 km/h, 3,000 kW (1,500 kW continuous), Scharfenberg couplers; the yellow fronts from February 2023 (2206 first, all three-car units by 2024).
