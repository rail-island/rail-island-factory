## Measurements

- **Side (EF66 27)** - a 1-D projective fit over the seven window starts (residuals under 1 px) puts the
  body at 9.83 window pitches; with the 17.1 m body that is a pitch of 1.74 m. Behind the face's tip: cab
  side window 0.98 - 1.36 m, door 1.70 - 2.42 m (its window 1.85 - 2.11 m), windows 2.53 m on at 1.74 m
  pitch and 1.575 m wide, the louvres under them; the raised cab roof ends at 1.65 m, just ahead of the door.
- **Heights (EF66 27 front, 201 px/m)** - body bottom 1.34 m, the blue band to 1.69 m, the grille 1.82 -
  2.06 m, the cream to the brow at 2.74 m, the shelf to the windscreen's foot at 3.04 m, the windscreen to
  3.63 m, the visor's lip 3.68 - 3.71 m, the cab roof 3.87 m; lamp housings 1.64 - 2.26 m, 0.35 m wide;
  number plate 2.44 - 2.64 m, 0.72 m wide, on a gold vee 1.26 m wide reaching down to 2.34 m. Side
  (local 183 px/m): windows 2.98 - 3.32 m, louvres 2.41 - 2.88 m, the cream stripe 1.92 - 2.02 m.

## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - ja.wikipedia "国鉄EF66形電気機関車": length 18,200 mm over couplers, body 17,100 mm, 2,800 mm
  wide, roof 3,670 mm, front (cab roof) 3,872.3 mm, 4,200 mm with the pantographs folded; bogie centres
  5,300 mm, wheelbase 2,800 mm, wheels 1,120 mm; Bo-Bo-Bo, 100.8 t, 3,900 kW, 110 km/h, from 1968.
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
