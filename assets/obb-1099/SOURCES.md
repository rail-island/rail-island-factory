## Published figures

German Wikipedia "NÖLB E" (infobox): 16 locomotives, built 1909-1914 (Krauss Linz, Austrian Siemens-Schuckert),
C'C', 760 mm gauge, 11,020 mm over the couplers, height 3,400 mm, width 2,480 mm, bogie wheelbase 2,400 mm,
pivots 6,100 mm apart, total wheelbase 7,900 mm (the model uses 8.5 m, pivots at the bogie centres:
the side photograph puts the outer axle about 0.8 m inside the end wall), 49.8 t, 420 kW one-hour and
320 kW continuous, 50 km/h, wheels 800 mm, 6.5 kV 25 Hz, Bosna centre coupler. The body was renewed
in 1959-62 (the one modelled). E 10 and E 7 were painted gold and brown in 2021 and 2022.

## Running numbers

`1099.011-7` (blood orange, photographed in 2004 with the Puchenstuben arms), `E 10` (gold and brown, photographed in 2021).
