## Published figures

- zh Wikipedia (DJJ1 article): formation 1M6T (power car, coaches, driving trailer), power car 20,500 mm, coach 25,500 mm, control car 25,325 mm over the couplers; width 3,104 mm, height 3,950 mm; 4,800 kW, 200 km/h in service, 235.6 km/h on test; power car wheels 1,050 mm, trailers on CW-200 bogies; 421 - 423 seats (per car 35, 78, 78, 66, 78, 78); eight sets 0001 - 0008, built 2000 - 2001.
- en Wikipedia (China Railway DJJ1) and loco-info.com: power car 78 t (19.5 t axle load), Bo'Bo', 4,800 kW; set length 173.33 m.
- The trailers' weights are not published: 45 t for a coach and 47 t for the driving trailer are estimates (axle load at most 15.5 t). The power car's bogie centres (10.5 m) and wheelbase (2.8 m) are estimated from the photographs.
