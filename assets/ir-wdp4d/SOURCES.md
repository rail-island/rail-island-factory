## Published figures

- Wikipedia, Indian locomotive class WDP-4 (WDP-4D section): EMD JT46PACe, dual cab, 4,500 hp, Co-Co on HTSC
  bogies, 1,092 mm wheels, 123 t, 135 km/h, built by DLW / BLW 2010 - 2017, numbers 40001 - 40608.
- Indian Railways (CRIS) diesel locomotive parameter table, WDP4: 19,964 mm over the buffer beams, 21,244 mm
  overall; the 4,500 hp WDP-4B/4D listing: 3,127 mm wide, 4,201 mm high.

The model carries 40086's number (the side-on photograph, fresh paint, yellow-band scheme).
