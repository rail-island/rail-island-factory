## Published figures

- **Line** - Vanoise Express, Les Arcs - La Plagne, Savoie (Wikipedia "Vanoise Express"): a double-deck aerial tramway opened in December 2003 over the Ponturin gorge, 200 passengers a cabin, 12 m/s.
- **Cabin** - two glazed decks; 9.2 x 4.6 m in plan and 4.95 m to the roof (approx.: the photographs show the cabins only from far).
