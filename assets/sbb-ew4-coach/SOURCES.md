## Figures

- 26.40 m over the buffers and 18.60 m between the bogie centres: painted on the B 50 85 21-95 149-3 (photo above).
- Ten windows between the doors in first class, eleven in second; the doors set in from the ends; the green and
  stone-grey delivery livery, the white-red-black livery of the 2000s modernisation and the 2021 repaint (red doors,
  black ends and skirt, a red line under the roof): de.wikipedia, "Einheitswagen (Schweiz, Normalspur)", section
  "Einheitswagen IV".
- The two side views of models (`photo-sbb-a-50-85-10.jpg`, `photo-sbb-b-50-85-21-75.jpg`) are orthographic and set
  the positions along the car; heights are taken from the photographs of the real cars.

## Running numbers

The A carries A 50 85 10-95 031-6 and the B carries B 50 85 21-95 149-3, both photographed (check digits verified).
The cars are numbered as the 200 km/h push-pull cars they are today in every livery; in 1981 they were 10-73 and 21-73.
