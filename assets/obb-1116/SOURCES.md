## Published figures

German Wikipedia, "OBB 1016/1116": 19,280 mm over buffers, 3,000 mm wide, bogie centres 9,900 mm, wheelbase
3,000 mm, wheels 1,150 mm, 86 t (without ETCS), 6.4 MW, 230 km/h. Running numbers: 1116 038-9 (traffic red)
and 1116 233-6 (Railjet), check digits by the Luhn rule the photographed 1116 005-8 and 1116 061-1 follow.
