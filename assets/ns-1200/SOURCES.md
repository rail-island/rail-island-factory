## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons by `pnpm refs:fetch` as standard thumbnails, at most 1280 px on the long side.
The `blue` livery is matched to the turquoise as delivered in 1951 (the museum's 1201, restored; the running
number drawn); `yellowGrey` to NS's yellow and grey of the 1970s - 1990s (1220 drawn; 1208, 1211). 1251 - 1255
are ACTS numbers (1999 on), so the yellow and grey loco carries an NS number.

## Published figures

From the Dutch, German and English Wikipedia articles ("NS 1200 (elektrische locomotief)", "NS-Baureihe 1200",
"NS Class 1200"; the height from Somda RailWiki via the Dutch article): Co'Co', 18.086 m over the buffers, 108 t
(18 t axle load), 150 km/h (130 - 135 in service), 2,400 kW one-hour / 2,220 kW continuous, 1,500 V DC, 4.064 m
from rail to roof. No published bogie centres or wheelbase were found: the pivots 9.2 m apart, the axles 1.75 m
apart and the 1,100 mm wheels are estimates from the museum 1201 photo (the middle axle under the cab door, the
leading axle at the nose-to-cab joint).
