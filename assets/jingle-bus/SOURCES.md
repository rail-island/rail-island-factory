## Published figures

- Pakistani Jingle Bus: in service from 1970; 9.5 m long, 2.45 m wide, 3.2 m high; 2 axles, at 1.3, 6.3 m from the nose (approx., read off the photographs where not published); 2 doors on the modelled side; about 60 passengers (approx.); 8 t empty, 100 kW, 80 km/h (approx.); liveries or colours: Red, Green, Blue. Source: Wikipedia "Truck art in South Asia" and the photographs.
