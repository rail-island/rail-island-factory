## Published figures

- de Havilland Comet 4 (Wikipedia "de Havilland Comet"): in service from 1958; length 33.99 m, wingspan 35 m, height 8.99 m; fuselage about 3 m wide (approx.); about 81 seats (typical layout, approx.); maximum take-off mass about 73.5 t; total thrust about 184 kN; cruising speed about 800 km/h.
- Positions of the wing, engines, tail, gear legs, doors and windows are read off the photographs and published three-view drawings (approx.); the livery colours are approximations of the carriers' schemes.
