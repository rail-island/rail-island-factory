Running numbers modelled: RW25T 553745 and YZ25T 351219 (white and blue), RW25T 553849 and YZ25T 350662 (green).
The green 25T as photographed stands for the `green25b` livery: the 25B's dark green with two yellow lines, which
the 25T cars carry today.

## Published figures

China Railway 25T (zh.wikipedia "25T型客车", "25型客车"): body 25.5 m long, 3.105 m wide, 4.433 m over the rail
(over the air-conditioning units), bogie centres 18 m, 160 km/h; 26.6 m over the couplers, 54.5 t and 36 berths
(RW25T, painted on the car's end); 118 seats (YZ25T).
