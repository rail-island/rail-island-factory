## Published figures and numbers

- de.wikipedia "Railjet 1. Generation" (technical table): Afmpz 26.85 m, Ampz / ARbmpz / Bmpz 26.50 m, Bmpvz 26.45 m
  (185.80 m the set), 2825 mm wide, 4050 mm high, bogie centres 19.0 m, wheelbase 2500 mm; seats 27 / 55 / 10 / 80 / 72
  (404 the set), 417.2 t empty, 230 km/h; the formation of sets 01-51 (cars 21-27).
- Running numbers: the OBB driving trailer carries 80-90 728 (photographed at Wien Hbf), the CD one 80-91 005
  (photographed at Graz Hbf), as on the cab cap.
- The restaurant car ARbmpz was seen only far off (Guntramsdorf, Pfarrwerfen): its window layout is approximate.
