## Published figures

- **DEWAG drawing** - axles 650 + 1000 + 2200 + 1800 + 2300 + 2300 + 3800 mm from the front buffers, engine
  wheelbase 12,400 mm, 2220 mm trailing axle to tender, tender axles 3 x 1900 mm, boiler centre 3100 mm,
  chimney 4550 mm, buffers 1025 mm, tender 3970 mm high. The drawing's 23,750 mm over buffers is the early
  tender; the 2'2' T 32 makes 23,940 mm, the extra 0.19 m given to the tender's tank and rear overhang.
- **Wheels** - leading 850 mm, coupled 2000 mm, trailing 1250 mm; two cylinders 650 x 660 mm.

- **Figures** - the class's Wikipedia article and the table in [germany-trains.md](../../docs/models/germany-trains.md): length, power, top speed and first year as the design states them (`generator/worldSteamLocos.ts`, `generator/openttdTable.ts`).
