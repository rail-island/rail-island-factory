## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "JNR Class C62": 4-6-4, 1,750 mm drivers, 21.475 m with the tender, 3.98 m high, 88.8 t engine, 100 km/h (129 km/h record), 49 built 1948 - 1949; 10-22 tender (10 t coal, 22 t water, 56.34 t). Japanese Wikipedia: C62 2's stainless swallow on the deflectors (fitted 1951), the white running-board line painted in 1970 and kept.
- **Gauge** - the prototype's track is 1,067 mm; the model keeps its real body on the game's standard-gauge wheelsets.
