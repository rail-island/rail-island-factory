The model's running numbers: 1302 (turquoise, as photographed at Stroe) and 1312 (yellow, the museum unit).
The turquoise colour itself and the lining's colour are not in a colour photograph here: the lining is drawn
pale yellow (light strips in the 1952 photograph), the band between the strips dark blue.

## Published figures

- **Figures** - Wikipedia, [NS 1300 (nl)](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS_1300_(elektrische_locomotief)), [NS-Baureihe 1300 (de)](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS-Baureihe_1300), [NS Class 1300 (en)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS_Class_1300): 18.95 m over the buffers, 111 t, 130 km/h, 3,159 kW (de, en; nl gives 3,600 kW), turquoise until 1955, Berlin blue, the 1970s grey and yellow, yellow with the big logo after the 1980s life extension (lamp bar). [SNCF CC 7100 (fr)](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/CC_7100): 1,250 mm wheels, type G pantographs (`generator/nl1300.ts`, `generator/openttdTable.ts`).
