# CD Y Coach - reference photos

One parametric model, `variant` bcc, beer, bnb, wrrm (vlakosimdb cd_bcc, cd_beer, cd_bnb, cd_wrrm). See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and, for BY-SA, share-alike. No photograph of the cream and red Bnb was found.
