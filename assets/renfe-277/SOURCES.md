## Published figures

Renfe 7700 / 277 (English Electric, Vulcan Foundry and CAF, 1952 - 1959): Co'Co', 3,000 V DC,
20.567 m over the buffers, 2.953 m wide, 4.28 m high with the pantographs folded, 1.22 m wheels,
120 t, 2,208 kW continuous (six EE 514/B motors), 110 km/h (Spanish Wikipedia, "Serie 7700 de Renfe").

## Liveries

`green` is the dark Renfe green with the yellow line (277 026, 277 047); the museum engines
(7702, 277 002) wear a lighter green. No photograph of a 277 in blue was found among the Commons
files: `blue` (a kept parameter value) is drawn as the blue-with-yellow-line scheme of Renfe's
older electrics and is not verified.
