# CSD Be31 - reference photos

Prototype: uncertain. Most likely a CSD two-axle Ce coach with enclosed end vestibules (Ce became Be after the 1956 class renaming; '31' may be a build year or a type code - unverified). A Commons description of a Ringhoffer-built Ce series of 1938-1949 exists ('File:Ce 3 - 3274 - Nadrazi Branik - 3-4 detail napis CSD a cislo vozu.JPG'), but that is a lettering detail only.

No photo saved. Checked: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:%C4%8CSD_Class_Ce (Ce 3-2252 photo set: only underframe/roof details downloaded and checked; Ce 3-4415 is a rebuilt service vehicle) and Category:CSD Class Ci (open-platform coaches, wrong type). Leads worth a manual look: the full views in Category:Ce 3-2252 (files 14 and 19 show the whole coach next to Ci 4-2262) and the Ce 3-3274 file at Praha-Branik.

## Detail references (added later)
