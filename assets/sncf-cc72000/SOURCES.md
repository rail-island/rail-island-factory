## Published figures

- **Figures** - French Wikipedia, [CC 72000](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/CC_72000) (after Defrance 1978 and Redoutey 2007): 20.19 m long, 2.952 m wide, 4.29 m high, bogie centres 11.99 m, bogie wheelbase 3.216 m, 1.14 m wheels, Y 218 bogies, 114 t, 2,650 kW, 140 km/h (72001 - 72020 but 72017) or 160 km/h; which units wore the Corail+ / Multiservices scheme.
