## Published figures

de.wikipedia "OBB Nahverkehrs-Doppelstockwagen": driving trailer 27.1 m and coach 26.8 m over the buffers,
4.632 m high, bogie centres 20 m, 140 km/h, empty 49 t (driving trailer) and 48 t (coach), 86 and 114 seats,
built 1996 - 2009 (253 coaches, 72 driving trailers), the cab face after the OBB 1014's. Width 2.8 m and the
920 mm wheels / 2.5 m wheelbase are the usual figures for these bogies (not stated in the article).

Running numbers: 50 81 86-33 102-3 (CityShuttle driving trailer, photographed), 50 81 86-33 113-0 (Cityjet,
photographed; check digit computed), 50 81 26-33 114-1 (coach, Commons file at Wien Floridsdorf; check digit valid).
