## Published figures

- Lisbon Remodelado (Wikipedia "Trams in Lisbon" and the operators' published data): in service from 1936; 8.4 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.3 m wide, 3.25 m to the roof (approx.); 58 passengers with standing room (approx.); 50 km/h, 70 kW, 10.5 t (approx.); track gauge 900 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
