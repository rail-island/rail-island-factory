## Published figures

- Turin Series 2800 (Wikipedia "Trams in Turin" and the operators' published data): in service from 1958; 20.45 m long over the bodies in 2 sections, 2.3 m wide, 3.3 m to the roof (approx.); 150 passengers with standing room (approx.); 60 km/h, 200 kW, 25 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
