## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons at 1280 px wide. Running numbers drawn: Pullman 4141 and sleeping car 3552 (both photographed).
The 1920s steel cars share one body (23.45 m), so the dining car 3348 informs the sleeping car's heights, lining, ends and running gear.
