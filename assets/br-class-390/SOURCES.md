## Figures

- en.wikipedia "British Rail Class 390": end cars 24.80 m, intermediate cars 23.90 m, 2.73 m wide, 3.56 m high;
  9- and 11-car sets, the pantographs on the pantograph trailers (the third car and the third from last of a
  nine-car set), Fiat SIG tilting bogies.
- Running numbers: 390 013 (Virgin, Stoke-on-Trent photograph), 390 130 (Avanti, Rugeley photograph).
