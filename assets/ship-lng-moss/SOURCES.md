## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "LNG carrier" and the photographs: a Moss-type LNG carrier with five spherical tanks of about 125,000 m3 (approx.): 290 m long, 48 m beam, 11.5 m draught, 29,000 kW steam turbines, 19 knots (35 km/h); the tanks are about 40 m across.
- Measured on the Gulf Energy photograph at 3.74 px/m (the hull over all, 1086 px, taken as 290 m; approx.):
  freeboard to the main deck 15.5 m; the tank covers 39.5-41 m across, nearly touching, their centres at
  0.27, 0.42, 0.55, 0.70, 0.82 of the length (a 40 m pitch from 57 m to 257 m off the stern); each cover a
  skirt 4-4.5 m from the deck to the walkway round the equator, then a dome about 12 m high (16 m over the
  deck); the pipe rack over the covers' crowns about 16-17 m over the deck, on braced trestles in the gaps
  with vent masts to about 23 m; the goosenecks on the tank domes about 6 m over the rack; the low
  deckhouse aft 20-39 m (2 decks) with the funnel at 21.5-29 m, its top about 20 m over the deck; the
  accommodation tower 39-56 m, its wheelhouse roof about 26 m over the deck, the mast to about 35 m; the
  forward mast at 269 m, about 17 m over the deck; a cargo machinery house on the starboard side between
  the third and fourth covers.
