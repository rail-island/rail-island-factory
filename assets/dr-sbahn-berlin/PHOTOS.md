# DR double-deck coach (Berlin livery) - reference photos

One parametric model, `variant` coach and drivingTrailer (vlakosimdb dr_sds_b, dr_sds_s).

Prototype: the Deutsche Reichsbahn's Goerlitz double-deck single coaches and their DBmq driving trailer, in the cream and dark red "Hauptstadtanstrich" of 1987. Sources: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppelstockwagen and the photographs' descriptions.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (through `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85).
