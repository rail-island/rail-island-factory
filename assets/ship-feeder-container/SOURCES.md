## Published figures

- Vega Scorpio (IMO 9491599), Zhejiang Yangfan Ship Group, Zhoushan, 2010, Zhejiang 950 type (Contships
  Management, 2024; sister Vega Carina on naviecapitani.it): 139.10 m long, 22.60 m beam, 11.80 m depth,
  8.80 m draught (max), 966-997 TEU (220-252 reefer plugs), 11,768 dwt, 9,981 GT, two 45 t cranes, one
  MAN B&W 8L48/60B of 9,600 kW, 18.8 knots.
- Read off the photographs (approx.): seven 40 ft bays on deck, two aft of the after crane, three between the
  cranes, two forward; crane columns at about 0.41 and 0.72 of the length on the port side, about 23 m over
  the deck, jibs about 27-29 m stowed forward on rests; the house 22 m long, seven decks, on a poop of about
  2.6 m reaching to its front.
