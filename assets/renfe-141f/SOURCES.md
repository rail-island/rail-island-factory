## Published figures

- **loco-info.com** (https://www.loco-info.com/view.aspx?id=17910): 1,560 mm drivers, 10.85 m engine wheelbase, 5.12 m rigid (coupled) wheelbase, 19.57 m total with the tender, 103 t in service, 166.5 t with the tender (so 63.5 t tender), 72 t adhesive, cylinders 570 x 710 mm, 15 bar, 90 km/h official limit.
- **Spanish Wikipedia, "Mikado (locomotora de vapor)"**: almost 2,000 CV (about 1,460 kW), 103 t in running order; 242 engines bought from 1953 (25 North British, the rest by Macosa, MTM, Euskalduna and Babcock & Wilcox).
- **Length over buffers** about 23.0 m: the Electrotren H0 model's 265 mm at 1:87; the pony then sits 2.0 m behind the front buffer heads.
- Not found published (estimated from the photographs): wheel diameters of the pony (1.0 m), trailing (1.06 m) and tender (0.95 m) wheels, the tender bogies' wheelbase and the boiler's heights.
