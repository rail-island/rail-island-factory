## Published figures

- **Figures** - Dutch Wikipedia, [NS 1100 (elektrische locomotief)](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS_1100_(elektrische_locomotief)):
  12,984 mm over the buffers (14,184 mm with the collision nose of 1978 - 1981), 2,988 mm wide, bogie pivots
  6,000 mm apart, 2,950 mm wheelbase, 1,250 mm wheels, the roof under the pantograph 3,750 mm, 83 t with the
  nose, 2,030 kW; English Wikipedia, [NS Class 1100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS_Class_1100): 83 t,
  2,030 kW, 135 km/h; the buffers mounted on the bogies (`generator/nl1100.ts`, `generator/nlLocos.ts`).
