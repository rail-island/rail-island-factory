## Published figures

- Alstom TFS (Wikipedia "TFS (tramway)" and the operators' published data): in service from 1987; 30.3 m long over the bodies in 3 sections, 2.3 m wide, 3.3 m to the roof (approx.); 170 passengers with standing room (approx.); 70 km/h, 550 kW, 44 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
