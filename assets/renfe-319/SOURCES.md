## Published figures

- Length over buffers 19.50 m, width 3.08 m (2.80 m for 319.201 - 220), height 4.145 m (4.034 m), wheels 1,067 mm, Co'Co' with GM D77 motors; weight 110 - 117 t; 319.2 1,870 hp, 319.3 / 319.4 2,027 hp (about 1,510 kW); 120 km/h (319.3 140 km/h): listadotren, Renfe sale annex (Via Libre 2010), via en.wikipedia "Renfe Class 319 (later versions)".
- Two 319.2 wore the brown and cream Estrella scheme in 1984 - 85 (es.wikipedia "Serie 319 de Renfe", "Diurno (Renfe)"); no photograph of them was found on Commons, so the estrella key shows the 319.3 that hauled the Estrella trains (319.310, 319.323).
