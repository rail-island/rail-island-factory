## Published and measured figures

- **Wikipedia "Pioneer Zephyr"** (infobox): train 197 ft 2 in, body 9 ft 1 1/16 in wide, 12 ft 1 3/16 in high,
  36 in driving and 30 in ride wheels, 208,061 lb, B-(2+2)-2, 72 seats; 600 hp Winton 201-A as built.
- **The Railway Age drawing** (dimensioned): 13 ft 6 in from the tip to the motor truck, 60 ft 8 1/2 in to the
  first Jacobs bogie, 57 ft 8 in between the Jacobs bogies, 53 ft 7 in to the rear truck, 11 ft 5 in beyond it;
  8 ft truck wheelbase. Its pixels (about 21 px/m along, 20 px/m up) give the rest: the nose's side profile
  (pilot tip, belt 0.38 m and window sill 0.72 m behind it, the dome rising to the headlight), the plan's flat
  front and the rounding over about 1.5 - 2 m, the power car's cowl about 0.2 - 0.4 m over the roof falling to it
  9 - 10 m behind the tip, the belt at about 1.0 - 1.4 m, the fluting to 2.0 m, the window band 2.05 - 2.85 m.
- **Running number** 9900 (the only set), on the nose; BURLINGTON on the power car, BURLINGTON ZEPHYR on the
  observation car, as in the 1934 photographs.
