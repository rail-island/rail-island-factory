## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - The Diesel Shop data sheet "EMD SD40-2": 68 ft 10 in over the couplers, hood 15 ft 1.2 in (drawn as the cabinet behind the cab; the long hood's roof at 4.5 m, 0.13 m lower on the BN 7812 photograph), cab 14 ft 8.7 in, cab 10 ft wide, hood 7 ft, HT-C wheelbase 13 ft 7 in, 40 in wheels, 368,000 lb, 3,000 hp, 65 mph, 3,957 built 1972 - 1986.
- **Truck centres** - 43 ft 6 in between the bolsters (Wikipedia "EMD SD40-2"; the Conrail Historical Society's SD40-2 technical page). The Diesel Shop gives 40 ft; the BN 7812 and NS 6155 photographs agree with 43 ft 6 in (the coupler-to-coupler length and the HT-C wheelbase both come out right at that scale, about 9 % short at 40 ft).
- **Running numbers** - the photographed units BN 7812, CR 6469 and UP 3741.
- **Paint** - each scheme's colour fields and stripes as on the photographs, the railroad's name in stroke letters, and simplified emblems: the BN herald (a white B with the green N through it), Conrail's wheel and rails, the Union Pacific shield.
