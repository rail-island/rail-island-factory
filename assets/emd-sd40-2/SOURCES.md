## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - The Diesel Shop data sheet "EMD SD40-2": 68 ft 10 in, hood 15 ft 1.2 in (its dynamic brake housing; the hood roof is drawn at 4.5 m), cab 14 ft 8.7 in, cab 10 ft wide, hood 7 ft, truck centres 40 ft, HT-C wheelbase 13 ft 7 in, 40 in wheels, 368,000 lb, 3,000 hp, 65 mph, 3,957 built 1972 - 1986.
- **Running numbers** - illustrative numbers in each railroad's SD40-2 series (BN 7800, CR 6450, UP 3800).
- **Paint** - the railroads' schemes simplified to colour fields, stripes and the
  railroad's name in plain letters; no heralds or logos are drawn.
