## Notes

- Running number shown: CRH2E-2122 (photo-crh2e-2122-at-lianyuqiao-20241001064212.jpg).
- Published figures (zh.wikipedia, CRH2 article, technical data): end car 25.7 m and intermediate car 25.0 m,
  3.38 m wide (drawn 3.27 m in the Shinkansen gauge), 3.7 m high, 2.5 m wheelbase; 8M8T as
  [Tc M M Tp] [T M M T] [T M M T] [Tp M M Tc], 300 kW motors, 9,600 kW, 250 km/h; 13 soft sleepers (WR,
  ten four-berth compartments, 40 berths), two second-class seat cars (ZE) at the ends, a dining car;
  630 passengers; in service from 21 December 2008 (CRH2-121E to 126E, Beijing - Shanghai).
- The sleeper's two sides were measured separately (the 2132 and car 15 photographs on one side, the
  2122 and 2127 photographs on the other): both show ten windows of the same size at the same 2.0 m
  pitch, within the oblique photographs' accuracy, so the model draws them alike.
