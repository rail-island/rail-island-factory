## Published figures

- Translohr STE3 (Wikipedia "Translohr" and the operators' published data): in service from 2006; 29.8 m long over the bodies in 5 sections, 2.2 m wide, 2.89 m to the roof (approx.); 127 passengers with standing room (approx.); 70 km/h, 250 kW, 23 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
