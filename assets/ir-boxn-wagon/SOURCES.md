The Rajahmundry photographs show blue BOXNHL wagons (ECR, number not legible): used for the side's structure
only (panels, ribs, ladder frames, plates); the brown paint follows the BOXN freight action and Pithapuram
photographs and the BCNA ones. The running numbers drawn (ER 82153 on the BOXN, SR 70154 on the BCNA) are
illustrative, in the older owner-code-and-serial style the BCNHL photograph shows (WCR 46304).

## Measurements

- BCNA doors: fitted projectively on `photo-bcna-freight-train-at-malkajgiri-03.jpg` (middle wagon, ends at
  x 490 and 900 px): two doors a side, each 0.14 of the length (about 2.1 m) wide, centred 0.24 of the length
  (3.6 m) from the middle, sliding outward on a top track; stanchions only between the doors (rms residual 7 px).
- BOXN side: on `photo-boxn-freight-train-at-rajahmundry-02.jpg` six panels of nearly equal pitch (1.63 m) after
  removing the perspective; ribs at about 0.36 and 0.68 of the side's height from the top plus one near the foot.

## Published figures

BOXN: 10,713 mm over couplers, 9,784 mm inside length, 3,233 mm high, bogie centres 6,524 mm.
BCNA: 15,429 mm over couplers, 14,500 mm inside length, bogie centres 10,150 mm. CASNUB-22 bogies,
1,000 mm wheels, 2,000 mm wheelbase.
