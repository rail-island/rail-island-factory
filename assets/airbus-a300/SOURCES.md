## Published figures

- Airbus A300B4 (Wikipedia "Airbus A300"): in service from 1974; length 53.62 m, wingspan 44.84 m, height 16.53 m; fuselage about 5.64 m wide (approx.); about 266 seats (typical layout, approx.); maximum take-off mass about 165 t; total thrust about 454 kN; cruising speed about 850 km/h.
- Positions of the wing, engines, tail, gear legs, doors and windows are read off the photographs and published three-view drawings (approx.); the livery colours are approximations of the carriers' schemes.
