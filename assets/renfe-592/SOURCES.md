## Figures

es.wikipedia "Serie 592 de Renfe": 70.214 m over the couplers (M-R-M), 2.86 m wide, 3.965 m high, 130.4 t, four MAN D2866 engines (840 kW), 120 km/h, 200 - 228 seats; the humps hold the air conditioning. Car lengths are not published: drawn 23.66 m (motor cars) and 22.9 m (trailer).

Running numbers: 592 015 (Regionales, photographed at Bobadilla in 1997); 592 239 (Media Distancia: motor car 239M photographed at Madrid-Atocha in 2018; the unit number is illustrative).
