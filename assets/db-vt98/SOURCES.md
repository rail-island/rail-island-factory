## Measurements

- Head-on (Koeln Stadtwald, buffer centres 1.05 m up): black band 0.58 - 0.77 m, headlamps at 1.51 m
  and +-0.87 m, aluminium strip 1.80 - 1.90 m, windows 1.95 - 2.72 m, gutter 2.75 m; big windows
  +-0.05 - 0.98 m, corner windows from +-1.09 m round the 0.42 m corners; horn grille at (-0.17, 1.36).
- Side (Kasbachtalbahn, a 1-D projective fit over 14 window edges and the doors, symmetric car, 12.65 m
  face to face): seven windows 1.285 m wide at a 1.375 m pitch from 1.557 m behind the face; the door
  0.56 - 1.435 m behind each face; the radiator louvre at the middle (798 752-2).

## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "Uerdingen railbus" / de.wikipedia "Uerdinger Schienenbus": two axles 6.0 m apart
  (lettered "6,00 m" on 798 752-2), 13.95 m over buffers, 3.28 m high, 2 x 110 kW, 90 km/h, from 1955.
- **Gauge** - standard gauge, UIC loading gauge.
