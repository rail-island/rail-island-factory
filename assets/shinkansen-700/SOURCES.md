## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "700 Series Shinkansen": end car 27.35 m, intermediate 25.0 m, 3.38 m wide, 3.65 m high, 285 km/h, 1999 - 2020; the 923 test train on the 700 (2000).
- **Width** - narrowed to 3.24 - 3.28 m (the Shinkansen gauge's 3.3 m; the E6 keeps its own).
