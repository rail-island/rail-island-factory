## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "Acela Express (trainset)": power car 69 ft 7 3/8 in (21.22 m), 10 ft 5 in wide, 14 ft 2 in high, 204,000 lb, 6,200 hp (4.6 MW), 150 mph in service, 40 in wheels, truck centres 35 ft 3 in; coach 87 ft 5 in (26.64 m), 10 ft 4.5 in wide, 13 ft 10.6 in high, 36 in wheels, truck centres 59 ft 6 in, end coach 142,000 lb, intermediate 139,000 lb, cafe 137,000 lb; two power cars, one first class (44 seats), four business class (65 seats each) and the cafe car; first revenue run 11 December 2000.
- **Paint** - the schemes simplified to colour fields, stripes and plain letters; no
  heralds or logos are drawn.
