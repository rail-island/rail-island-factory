## Figures

- Eight cars about 209 m; the end cars 27.91 m and the others 25.65 m (en Wikipedia's formation table).
  The line scan gives 25.65 m between an intermediate car's joints at the same scale as its 4.05 m
  height, and 27.55 m from the nose's tip to the first joint: the model's end car is 27.6 m to its
  coupling plane (8 cars: 209.0 m).
- Width 3,360 mm (drawn 3.27 m, the Shinkansen gauge), height 4,050 mm, floor 1,250 mm (zh Wikipedia).
- Bogie centres measured on the line scan: about 18.15 m on the end car (5.35 m behind the tip),
  17.85 m on an intermediate car.
- Formation 8 cars, 4M4T; the single-arm pantographs on the third and sixth cars.
