## Published figures

es.wikipedia "Serie 446 de Renfe": 75,993 mm over the couplers, 2,940 mm wide, 4,185 mm high, 166.6 t,
2,400 kW (four GEE 326 A2 motors a motor car), 100 km/h, M-R-M on Bo'Bo' + 2'2' + Bo'Bo', 240 seats,
3,000 V DC. The car lengths (motor 25.6 m, trailer 24.8 m) are an estimate within the published total.
