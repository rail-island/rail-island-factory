## Published figures

- **Line** - Titlis Rotair, Engelberg, Switzerland (Wikipedia "Titlis"): the first rotating aerial tramway, opened in 1992 from Stand to Klein Titlis; 80 passengers a cabin, the floor turning a full circle during the trip, about 10 m/s (approx.).
- **Cabin** - round, 6.2 m across and 3.05 m to the roof (approx., from the photographs); white, a red belt, the glazing all round.
