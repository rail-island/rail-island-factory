## Published figures

- A pit tub (British "tub", German "Hunt" / Foerderwagen) of the kind pit ponies hauled underground from the 19th century to the 1960s: a riveted or welded steel box body, about 0.6-1.0 m3, on four small cast wheels at 500-600 mm gauge, with wooden dumb buffers and a hook and chain coupling at each end.
- The model: 600 mm gauge; body 1.6 m long and 0.9 m wide at the rim, 1.46 x 0.8 m at its bottom, rim 1.08 m above the rail; 1.75 m over the hooks; wheels 0.32 m diameter on a 0.52 m wheelbase (approx., measured from the Bottrop photograph against its wheels).
- About 0.35 t empty, about 0.75 t of coal (approx.); its own limit 10 km/h (approx.).
