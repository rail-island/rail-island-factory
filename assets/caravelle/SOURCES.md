## Published figures

- Sud Aviation Caravelle (Wikipedia "Sud Aviation Caravelle"): in service from 1959; length 32.01 m, wingspan 34.3 m, height 8.72 m; fuselage about 3.2 m wide (approx.); about 80 seats (typical layout, approx.); maximum take-off mass about 52 t; total thrust about 106 kN; cruising speed about 780 km/h.
