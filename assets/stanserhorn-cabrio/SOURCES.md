## Published figures

- **Line** - CabriO Stanserhorn, Stans, Switzerland (Wikipedia "Stanserhorn"): a Garaventa aerial tramway opened in 2012, the first with an open upper deck; 60 passengers in the glazed cabin and 30 on the top deck, about 8 m/s (approx.).
- **Cabin** - hung from carriages on track ropes either side of it, so the top deck is free; 5.60 x 3.80 m in plan, 2.85 m to the deck and a 1.1 m railing (approx., from the photographs).
