- **Line** - Ngong Ping 360, Lantau, Hong Kong (Wikipedia "Ngong Ping 360"): a bicable gondola (one track rope, one haul rope) opened in 2006, 5.7 km, cabins for 17 passengers (10 seated), about 7 m/s (approx.); the Crystal cabins have a glass floor.
