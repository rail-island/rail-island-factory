## Published figures

- Wikipedia, [China Railways QJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Railways_QJ) and
  the Chinese Wikipedia article on the QJ (zh.wikipedia.org): 16,140 mm over the engine (main production;
  the works diagram's axles 1,615 + 2,870 + 4 x 1,600 + 3,050 + 2,205 mm), 29,181 mm with the six-axle tender
  (26,023 mm with the four-axle one: production engines ran with the twelve-wheel tender), width 3,375 mm,
  height 4,790 mm, wheels 920 / 1,500 / 1,120 mm, tender wheels 1,000 mm, cylinders 650 x 800 mm, engine
  133.8 t, six-axle tender 114.5 - 119.7 t, 2,200 kW official, 80 km/h, knuckle couplers.
- The model is 3.24 m wide (the 3.3 m US gauge the design is checked against) and draws a 720 mm stroke
  (the kit's largest crank that animates without flicker).
