The modelled unit is CRH6F-0475 of the Jinshan Railway (Shanghai), in its white livery with the dark window
band ending in a point on the nose, framed by a blue and an orange chevron, the thin blue line under the band
and the blue and orange swoosh low on the nose. CRH6F-0477 is a sister set in the same paint; CRH6F-0418 (Ningbo)
carries the same stripes with its name; CRH6F-0411 another livery and informs only the shape.

## Published figures

China Railway CRH6 (en.wikipedia.org) and 和谐号CRH6型电力动车组 (zh.wikipedia.org): CRH6F eight cars,
4M4T (Tc M Tp M M Tp M Tc, pantographs on cars 3 and 6), 201.4 m (25.7 m end cars, 25.0 m intermediate
cars), 3,300 mm wide, 3,860 mm high, 2,500 mm wheelbase, 860 mm wheels, 17 t axle load; 5,152 kW (322 kW
motors), 160 km/h; 1,502 passengers (1,998 crush); wide bi-parting plug doors, three a side on the
intermediate cars, equally spaced for platform screen doors. The CRH6F-A (four cars) has 240 seats. The
empty weight is not published: the model's 49 t end and 47 t intermediate cars are estimates.
