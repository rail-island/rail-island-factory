## Figures and markings

- Body 2.62 m wide and 2.88 m high, the driving motor car 16.0 m and the trailer 15.5 m over
  the couplers, 72 km/h: Wikipedia, "London Underground 1938 Stock"
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Underground_1938_Stock). Heights of the window
  heads, the shoulder and the cab front's windows, blind and lamps are scaled from the
  photographs above.
- Side number 10012: the preserved driving motor car in the side photograph. The set number
  747 on the cab door and the WATFORD blind are the heritage unit's (the Amersham
  photograph); the car letter D is the South Ealing and museum cars'.
