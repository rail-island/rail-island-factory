## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons at 1280 px wide. The model shows unit 4806 (its end car 4806-001 in
`photo-fyra-v250-den-haag.jpg`, 4806-008 in `photo-fyra-v250-4806.jpg`), the Fyra scheme of white with red,
magenta and pink fields. The test-running photos carry AnsaldoBreda lettering the model leaves out. The ETR 700
(Trenitalia's rebuild) has a new nose and is used for the body and running gear only.
