## Published figures

- **Figures** - Wikipedia, [SNCF Class CC 7100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNCF_Class_CC_7100): 18.922 m over the buffers, 2.968 m wide, 107 t, 3,490 kW, 150 km/h as delivered (140 km/h later), CC 7101 - 7158 built 1952 - 1955 (`generator/frCc7100.ts`, `generator/frLocos.ts`, `generator/openttdTable.ts`).
- The bogies (pivots 9.4 m apart, axles 2.43 m apart, 1,250 mm wheels) are the NS 1300's, the same Alsthom design.
