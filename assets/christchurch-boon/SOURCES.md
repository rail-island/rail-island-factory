## Published figures

- Christchurch Boon Tram (Wikipedia "Tramways in Christchurch" and the operators' published data): in service from 1906; 9.6 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.3 m wide, 3.2 m to the roof (approx.); 50 passengers with standing room (approx.); 35 km/h, 50 kW, 9 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
