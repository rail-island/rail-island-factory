The modelled unit is CRH6A-0407 of the first Pearl River Delta sets (Guangzhou-Foshan-Zhaoqing intercity), in
their white livery with the black window band and the orange line. The Zhengzhou sets (0425, 0427) share the
body in another paint and inform only the shape and the layout.

## Published figures

China Railway CRH6 (en.wikipedia.org) and 和谐号CRH6型电力动车组 (zh.wikipedia.org): eight cars, 4M4T
(Tc M M Tp T Mp M Tc, pantographs on cars 4 and 6), 201.4 m; a car 24,500 mm (the body: 25.0 m over the
couplers, so 25.7 m for the end cars), 3,300 mm wide, 3,860 mm high, 2,500 mm wheelbase, 860 mm wheels;
5,520 kW, 200 km/h. Two doors a side on most CRH6A intermediate cars (three on 0478, 0649 and the Jinshan
and Beijing sets).
