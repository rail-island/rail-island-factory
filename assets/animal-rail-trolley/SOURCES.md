## Published figures

- Platelayers' (permanent-way) trolleys: light four-wheel flat trolleys of timber on an iron frame, small enough for a gang to lift off the track by the handles at the ends, pushed, pumped or - on estate, plantation and sugar-cane tramways - drawn by a horse or mule, with a cross bench for the gang and a hand brake. The photographs show British examples (the W156W one a later enclosed type); the bench layout and the horse hitch are those of the estate tramway trolleys described in the brief.
- The model (approx.): standard gauge 1435 mm; deck 2.16 m long and 1.84 m wide at 0.62 m above the rail, 2.45 m over the hooks and lifting handles; wheels 0.54 m diameter on a 1.2 m wheelbase on an inside frame; two benches back to back for six (three facing each way), back to 1.54 m; the handbrake lever and its quadrant at the front right corner working blocks on the front wheels.
- About 0.45 t empty (approx.); six people; its own limit 20 km/h (approx.).
