## Published figures

- Tatra T4D (Wikipedia "Tatra T4" and the operators' published data): in service from 1968; 14 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.2 m wide, 3.06 m to the roof (approx.); 105 passengers with standing room (approx.); 55 km/h, 176 kW, 16 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
