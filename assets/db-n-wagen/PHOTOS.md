# DB n-Wagen - reference photos

One parametric model, `variant` bn, abn, bdnrf and `livery` vr, iv (vlakosimdb db_bnvr ... db_bdnrfiv).

Prototype: the DB "n-Wagen" ("Silberling") local-service coaches, built from 1959 to 1980, 26.4 m, in the 1996 DB Regio verkehrsrot livery with light grey stripes and doors (vr) and the 1986 light grey livery with the mint turquoise window band (iv), and the BDnrzf 740 driving trailer with the "Karlsruher Kopf" cab. Source: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-Wagen .

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (through `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). The page URL is the exact title.
