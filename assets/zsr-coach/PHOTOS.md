# ZSR Coach - reference photos

One parametric model, `variant` a, ab, b, bh, bds, bdmeer, wrrm, apee (vlakosimdb zsr_<variant>). See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). All licences require attribution (BY) and share-alike (SA). No free photograph of the 1990s ZSR red livery with white stripes was found: these show the same bodies in later ZSSK paint, used for the body types, window and door layouts.
