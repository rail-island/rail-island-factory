## Published figures

- Croydon CR4000 (Wikipedia "Bombardier CR4000" and the operators' published data): in service from 2000; 30.2 m long over the bodies in 3 sections, 2.65 m wide, 3.5 m to the roof (approx.); 208 passengers with standing room (approx.); 80 km/h, 480 kW, 36.3 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
