## Published figures

- Tupolev Tu-154M (Wikipedia "Tupolev Tu-154"): in service from 1972; length 47.9 m, wingspan 37.55 m, height 11.4 m; fuselage about 3.8 m wide (approx.); about 164 seats (typical layout, approx.); maximum take-off mass about 100 t; total thrust about 309 kN; cruising speed about 900 km/h.
- Positions of the wing, engines, tail, gear legs, doors and windows are read off the photographs and published three-view drawings (approx.); the livery colours are approximations of the carriers' schemes.
