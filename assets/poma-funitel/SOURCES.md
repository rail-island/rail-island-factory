## Published figures

- **System** - the funitel (Wikipedia "Funitel"): a detachable gondola on two haul ropes about 3.2 m apart, which keeps the cabin steady in wind; developed by Denis Creissels and built by Poma (the first, Val Thorens, 1990), cabins for 20 to 30 passengers, up to 7 m/s. Photographed at Palisades Tahoe (Squaw Valley) and Megeve (Rocharbois).
- **Cabin** - white with dark glazing under a black hanger frame with two grips on each rope; 2.70 x 3.20 m in plan and 2.55 m to the roof (approx., from the photographs).
