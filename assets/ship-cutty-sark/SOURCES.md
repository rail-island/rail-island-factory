## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "Cutty Sark" and the photographs: the tea clipper Cutty Sark (1869): 64.7 m on deck (85.4 m with the bowsprit), 11 m beam, 6.1 m draught, 2,100 t displacement, 3,000 m2 of sail on three masts, 17.5 knots (32 km/h) at best.
- Spars (approx., from published sail-plan figures): main yard 23.8 m, lower topsail yard 21.3 m, upper topsail 18.9 m, topgallant 14.9 m, royal 11.6 m, skysail 8.8 m; fore yards about 5 % shorter, the mizzen's crossjack 18.3 m. Rig: fore and main course, double topsails, topgallant and royal, the main a skysail too; the mizzen a bare crossjack yard under its topsails, topgallant, royal and the spanker.
- Read off the photographs (approx.): masthead heights over the deck main 39.5 m, fore 35 m, mizzen 27.5 m; lower masthead at 0.37-0.43 of the mast's height, topmast head at about 0.6; bulwarks 1.25 m; the jib-boom about 14 degrees above the horizontal, 19 m out beyond the stem head.
