## Published figures

- **Figures** - Wikipedia, [SNCF Class BB 22200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNCF_Class_BB_22200): power (4,360 kW), top speed and first year; French Wikipedia, [BB 22200](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/BB_22200) (after Defrance 1976, Redoutey 2007): 17.48 m over the buffers, 3.053 m wide, 4.311 m high, 90 t, bogie centres 9.694 m, bogie wheelbase 2.8 m, 1.25 m wheels, two AM 18 pantographs (one for each current), 160 km/h (200 with TVM). Running numbers modelled: 122342 (beton), 522266 (En Voyage), 422369 (Fret).
