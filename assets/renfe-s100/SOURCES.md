## Published figures

Renfe (grupo.renfe.com, "S-100") and the English and Spanish Wikipedia articles (Renfe Class 100, Serie 100 de Renfe):
200.150 m over couplers (M-8R-M); power cars 22.130 m and 2.814 m wide, end trailers 21.845 m, intermediate trailers
18.700 m and 2.904 m wide; 4.28 m over the lowered pantograph; bogie wheelbase 3.000 m; 392.6 t empty, 421.5 t loaded;
8,800 kW under 25 kV, 5,400 kW under 3 kV; 300 km/h; 329 seats. The power car's bogie centres (14.0 m), the 920 mm
wheels and the raised machine-room roof follow the TGV Atlantique's (generator/tgv.ts).
