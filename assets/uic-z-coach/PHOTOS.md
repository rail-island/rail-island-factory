# UIC-Z Coach - reference photos

One parametric model, `variant` firstClass, secondClass, restaurant and `operator` cd, db, obb (vlakosimdb cd_ampz, cd_bmz, cd_wrrmz, db_ic_ampz, db_ic_bmpz, db_ic_wrmz, obb_ampz, obb_bmpz, obb_wrmz). See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). All licences require attribution (BY) and share-alike (SA).
