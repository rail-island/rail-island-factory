All photographs show units of the original batch (0001-0180) in the as-delivered blue and silver
livery; the later batches (1001-, 2001-, flat sides, 9,600 kW) differ and were not used. The model
carries HXD2 0021.

## Published figures

- 2 x 19.025 m over the couplers (en.wikipedia "China Railways HXD2"; zh.wikipedia "和谐2型电力机车"
  gives 2 x 18.975 m), width 2.85 m, wheels 1.25 m, bogie wheelbase 2.6 m, 2 x 92 t (200 t ballasted
  to a 25 t axle load), 9,600 kW continuous / 10,000 kW maximum, 120 km/h.
