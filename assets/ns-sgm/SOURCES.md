## Published figures

nl.wikipedia "Stadsgewestelijk Materieel" (infobox and text): 52.2 m over the couplers for two cars,
78.7 m for three; 2.80 m wide, 3.89 m high, floor 1.23 m; swing-plug doors 1300 mm wide, three a side
on each end car, two on the original middle car and three after the 2003 - 2006 revision; Bo'Bo' end
cars, the middle car 2'2'; 106 t (two cars) and 142 t (three cars); 8 x 160 kW; 125 km/h; Scharfenberg
couplers. The original doors were grey ("de grijze deuren geel geschilderd", 1997).

The model's numbers: 2023 (yellow) and 2133 (Sprinter) - the same unit, the Spoorwegmuseum's, before
and after its renumbering at the revision.
