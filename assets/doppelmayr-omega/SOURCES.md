## Published figures

- **Cabin** - Doppelmayr (CWA) Omega IV-10: the ten-place cabin of Doppelmayr's detachable gondolas since the late 2000s (Wikipedia "Gondola lift"; Doppelmayr's product pages), seen here on Mi Teleferico's orange line in La Paz and on a ski lift at Yllas, Finland. The model is 2.20 x 2.05 m in plan and 2.55 m to the roof, the haul rope 4.45 m above the cabin's underside (approx., from the photographs against the doors).
- **Line speed** - detachable gondolas run at up to 6 m/s (22 km/h).
