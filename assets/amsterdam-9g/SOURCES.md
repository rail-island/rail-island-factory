## Published figures

- Amsterdam 9G (Wikipedia "Trams in Amsterdam" and the operators' published data): in service from 1979; 20.65 m long over the bodies in 2 sections, 2.35 m wide, 3.25 m to the roof (approx.); 140 passengers with standing room (approx.); 60 km/h, 200 kW, 21 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
