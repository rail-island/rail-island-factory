## Published figures

- Tupolev Tu-144 (Wikipedia "Tupolev Tu-144"): in service from 1977; length 65.7 m, wingspan 28.8 m, height 12.5 m; fuselage about 3.4 m wide (approx.); about 140 seats (typical layout, approx.); maximum take-off mass about 207 t; total thrust about 784 kN; cruising speed about 2300 km/h.
- Positions of the wing, engines, tail, gear legs, doors and windows are read off the photographs and published three-view drawings (approx.); the livery colours are approximations of the carriers' schemes.
