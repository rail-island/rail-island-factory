## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "ICE 3" and "DB-Baureihe 403 (2000)": end car 25.675 m, middle car 24.775 m, 2.95 m wide, 3.89 m high, 8 cars 200 m, 8,000 kW, 330 km/h, from 2000; bogie centres 17.375 m.

## Measured on the photographs

1.24 - 1.38 m across the front, 1.66 - 1.84 m on the body, meeting it 4.8 m back; the canopy's
glass 2.4 - 3.65 m, its side edge back to about 3.5 m; the lamp clusters 1.68 - 2.1 m; the lounge's
window band 2.02 - 2.73 m from 2.6 m (sill, raked) to 8.15 m; the end car's door 8.45 - 9.42 m; the
saloon's windows from 10.0 m; the leading bogie 5.0 m; the grey ICE centred 5.55 m; the
intermediate cars' door at their front end.
