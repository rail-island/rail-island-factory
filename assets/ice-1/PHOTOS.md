# ICE 1 - reference photos

One parametric model, `veh-ice-1`: `unit` powerCar (vlakosimdb ice loco, the default), coach (ice car), restaurant (ice restaurant).

Prototype: Deutsche Bahn ICE 1 (1989-1993): class 401 power cars, 20.56 m over couplers, 11.46 m bogie pivots, 3.0 m wheelbase, 1040 mm wheels; intermediate cars 26.40 m, 3.02 m wide, 3.84 m high, the class 804 restaurant car 4.295 m with its raised roof (de.wikipedia "ICE 1").

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (rendered by `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85).
