## Published figures

CRH380B (Wikipedia "China Railway CRH3"): 8 cars (4M4T: MC, TP, M, T, T, M, TP, MC; the pantographs on the
TP cars 2 and 7), 200 m like the CRH3C (end car 25.675 m, intermediate 24.775 m, 3.265 m wide, drawn 3.1 m,
the UIC gauge; 3.89 m high), 9,200 kW, 380 km/h design and 350 km/h in service, 551 seats.
