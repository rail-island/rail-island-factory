# CD 471 CityElefant - reference photos

One parametric model, `veh-cd-471`: `unit` motorCar (vlakosimdb cd_471), trailer (cd_071), drivingTrailer (cd_971); `variant` cd471 (default) or cd675 (vlakosimdb cd 675, the same body repainted).

Prototype: Skoda 1Ev "CityElefant", CD class 471 + 071 + 971, 83 three-car sets built 1997 to 2013 by CKD Vagonka / Skoda Vagonka for 3 kV DC suburban service around Prague and Ostrava; 79.2 m over the couplers, 4.635 m high, 2.82 m wide, bogie pivots 19.0 m apart, 2.6 m motor and 2.4 m trailer bogie wheelbase, 920 mm wheels (de.wikipedia "CD-Baureihe 471", cs.wikipedia "Elektricka jednotka 471").

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (rendered by `w/thumb.php`), downscaled to at most 1600 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality 85). Titles are given in ASCII transliteration; the page URL is the exact percent-encoded title. All licences require attribution (BY) and share-alike (SA).
