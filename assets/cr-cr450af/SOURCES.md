## Published figures

- en.wikipedia "China Railway CR450AF" (infobox and coach sequence): 4M4T (cars 2, 4, 5, 7 motored),
  pantographs on trailers 3 and 6, 3.36 m wide, 211 m long, 400 km/h service, 450 km/h design,
  453 km/h record, 14.5 t axle load, 10 % lighter than the CR400AF; end cars 28.55 m, the others 25.65 m.
- china-emu.cn, "CR450AF(样车)动车组" (https://www.china-emu.cn/Trains/Model/Detail-9601-101-S.html):
  211.35 m for eight cars (end cars 28.725 m, intermediate 25.65 m over the couplers), body 3.85 m
  high, 3.36 m wide, bogie centres 17.8 m, 12,000 kW installed, average axle load about 13 t,
  552 seats (30 + 85 + 90 + 80 + 63 + 90 + 80 + 34). The model follows these lengths.
