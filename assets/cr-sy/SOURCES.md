## Published figures

- **Wikipedia**, [China Railways SY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Railways_SY) (infobox after Gibbons 2016) and
  [上游型蒸汽机车](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/上游型蒸汽机车): 21,643 mm over the couplers with the tender, 3,300 mm wide,
  4,446 mm high (zh: 4,790 mm), wheels 840 / 1,370 / 1,000 mm, engine wheelbase 31 ft 4 in (9.55 m), driving
  wheelbase 14 ft 6 in (4.42 m), cylinders 530 x 710 mm, engine 84 t, tender 54.5 t, 1,100 kW (1,500 hp),
  80 km/h, knuckle couplers, built 1960 - 1999 (1,820 engines). The zh article: the tender's rear is narrowed and
  sloped so the crew can see back when reversing. The model takes 4.446 m as the chimney's top.
