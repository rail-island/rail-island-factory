## Published figures

- Bristol Lodekka: in service from 1949; 8.23 m long, 2.44 m wide, 4.09 m high; 2 axles, at 1.35, 6.23 m from the nose (approx., read off the photographs where not published); 1 doors on the modelled side; about 60 passengers (approx.); 8 t empty, 84 kW, 60 km/h (approx.); liveries or colours: Bristol Omnibus, Eastern Counties.
