## Judged dimensions

The mine head shares the coal mine's kit and figures (`assets/industry-mine/SOURCES.md`); what differs is judged from the photographs.

| figure | value | use |
|---|---|---|
| paint | a red headframe and frame columns (#bd5542), rust bunker and wagon | the headframe, the winding house's frame, the loadout |
| winding house | the 10 x 8 m house sheeted on a steel frame, an electric winder fed by a transformer and a line on two poles | in place of the coal mine's brick house and boiler chimney |
| ore | iron ore red (#8e3d24) load, a long rust-red stockpile (#9a5a3c), a grey-brown waste rock heap (#7d7166), ore-stained ground (#a3907f) | the heaps, the load and the yard |
