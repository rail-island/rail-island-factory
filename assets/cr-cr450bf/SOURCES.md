Only one CR450BF exists (the prototype CR450BF-0501, livery "Light of Technology", 科技之光), so every
photograph shows it. Commons holds no true side-on or head-on view of it: lengths come from
projective fits on the oblique views, the face from the three-quarter ones. Its two sides differ: the
left side (Jinanxi) has a crew door right behind the cab side window, the right side (Dongguantou) a
window there; the model draws the door on both sides.

## Published figures

English and Chinese Wikipedia ("China Railway CR450BF", "复兴号CR450BF型电力动车组"): CRRC Changchun,
unveiled 29 December 2024, 8 cars 4M4T (cars 2, 4, 5, 7 motored, pantographs on trailers 3 and 6),
211.4 m, width 3.36 m, 14.5 t axle load, 400 km/h service, 450 km/h design, permanent-magnet
synchronous motors; per CRRC 10 % lighter and 22 % less drag than the CR400BF, its cars lower than
the CR400BF's 4.05 m. Not published there, so estimated: the height (3.85 m, the CR400BF's less the
0.2 m quoted in the 2024 press reports), the car weights (the CR400BF's less 10 %: 47 t end car, 45 t
intermediate) and the power (about 10.4 MW: 16 motors of about 650 kW). The model: end car 28.75 m
and intermediate cars 25.65 m over the couplers (2 x 28.75 + 6 x 25.65 = 211.4 m), 17.5 m bogie
centres, 2.5 m wheelbase, 920 mm wheels, narrowed to 3.24 m for the Shinkansen gauge.
