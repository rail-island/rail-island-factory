## Published figures

- Foshan Hydrogen Tram (Wikipedia "Gaoming tram" and the operators' published data): in service from 2019; 27.7 m long over the bodies in 3 sections, 2.65 m wide, 3.4 m to the roof (approx.); 250 passengers with standing room (approx.); 70 km/h, 400 kW, 45 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
