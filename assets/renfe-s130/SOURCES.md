## Published figures

Renfe Class 130 (Talgo 250, en/es Wikipedia): two Bo'Bo' power cars of 20.0 m and eleven Talgo coaches of 13.14 m on
single axles shared at the joints; 2.96 m wide, power cars 4.0 m high (coaches 3.36 m); 4,800 kW at 25 kV
(2,400 kW a power car), 4,000 kW at 3 kV DC; 250 km/h on standard gauge (220 km/h broad gauge); variable gauge.
