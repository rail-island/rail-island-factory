# ship-harbour-tug references

**Covers** - provenance of the `ship-harbour-tug` model's references: photographs and published figures.
**Code** - see the design that names `references: "ship-harbour-tug"`
**See also** - [docs/models/world-ships.md](../../docs/models/world-ships.md)

The model is a modern azimuth stern drive (ASD) harbour tug of about 32 m, of the Damen ASD 3212 kind;
the photographs show such tugs of 28-32 m (no names, lettering or company marks are modelled).

## Published figures

- Damen ASD Tug 3212, the type modelled (approx.): 32.7 m long over all, 12.8 m beam, 5.35 m depth, about 5.5 m draught over the thrusters (about 4 m hull), two azimuth thrusters of about 2,500 kW (5,000 kW), about 80 t bollard pull, 13 knots (24 km/h); a towing winch on the forecastle, a towing hook aft.
- The model: 32 m x 12.8 m, depth 5.4 m, hull draught 3.6 m, a forecastle 17 m long and 1 m high.
