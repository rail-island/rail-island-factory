## Published figures

- 27.05 m long over the couplers, 2.78 m wide, 3.45 m high, bogie centres 18.35 m, bogie wheelbase 3.0 m,
  57 t, 8 + 65 seats, 120 km/h (nl.wikipedia "Blauwe Engel"; Spoorwegmuseum, "NS41 Blauwe Engel").
- No photograph of the rear end's face was found; it is drawn as a plain end wall with the gangway.
