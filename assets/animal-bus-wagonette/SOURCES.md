## Published figures

- Wagonette and charabanc (Wikipedia "Wagonette", "Charabanc"): open excursion wagons from the mid 19th century (the wagonette from about 1845, the charabanc's rising cross benches from the 1850s-1860s); 12-20 passengers on forward-facing cross benches, a canopy on posts against sun and rain, entry by a door and step at the rear; a pair of horses (approx.).
- Read off the photographs: the break wagonette's rear wheels about 1.3 m, front about 0.9 m; the bed about 1 m above the road, the driver's box raised at the front; the charabanc's benches each a step higher toward the rear (approx.).
- Estimates (approx.): the model is 4.4 m long over the step and the dash, 1.95 m wide over the hubs, 2.9 m high to the canopy; five benches of three and one beside the driver (16 seats); about 0.9 t empty; its own top speed 18 km/h.
