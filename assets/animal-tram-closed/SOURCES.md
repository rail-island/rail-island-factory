## Published figures

- Closed single-deck European horsecar of the 1870s-1890s (Prague horse tramway, opened 1875; Wikipedia "Horsecar", "Trams in Prague"; the Doebeln museum car no. 1): in service from 1875; a saloon about 4 m long with an open platform at each end, about 6 m over the dashes, 2.0 m wide over the panels, about 2.8 m to the clerestory (approx.); 16-20 seated on two longitudinal benches plus standing on the platforms, 24 in all (approx.); 8-10 km/h behind one or two horses, the car about 2.5-2.6 t empty (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Read off the photographs (approx.): the saloon's height to length about 0.55, the floor about 0.9 m up (a third of the car's height), wheels about 0.75 m on a single truck of about 1.7 m wheelbase, the dash waist-high above the platform; the arched roof running on over both platforms with a lantern clerestory over the saloon (Prague 1876 engraving), the claret lower panels with gold lining under cream window pillars (Doebeln). The model has five windows a side (Prague and Doebeln show four large bays; five is the common count of the Vienna and Berlin cars).
- Estimates (approx.): section lengths, window, sill and roof heights, the colours; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
