## Published figures

- **Line** - Sugarloaf Cable Car (Bondinho do Pao de Acucar), Rio de Janeiro (Wikipedia "Sugarloaf Cable Car"): opened in 1912; its current glass cabins (2008, approx.) carry 65 passengers, about 10 m/s (approx.).
- **Cabin** - glazed nearly all round, dark grey under a pale roof; 5.4 x 3.3 m in plan, 3.0 m to the roof (approx., from the photographs). The list gave 1972, the date of an earlier generation (approx.); the model follows the photographed current cabins.
