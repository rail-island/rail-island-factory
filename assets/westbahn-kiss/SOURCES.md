## Published figures

Wikipedia (de) "Stadler KISS", table "Technische Daten der Varianten", Version Westbahn: 150,000 mm over
the couplers as six cars and 100,360 mm as four (so end cars 25.36 m tip to coupling plane, middle cars
24.82 m), 2,800 mm wide, 4,595 mm high, wheels 920 mm, bogie wheelbase 2,500 mm, Bo'Bo' + 2'2' + ... +
Bo'Bo', 6,000 kW, 200 km/h, 296 t as six cars, 501 seats (KISS 2: 523). The running number 4010 008 is
the Melk photograph's set.
