## Published figures

- **Line** - Peak 2 Peak Gondola, Whistler Blackcomb, British Columbia (Wikipedia "Peak 2 Peak Gondola"): a Doppelmayr 3S ropeway opened on 12 December 2008, 4.4 km long, 28 cabins for 28 passengers each, 7.5 m/s; two of the cabins have a glass floor and are painted silver, the others red.
- **Cabin** - no dimensions are published; the model is 4.10 x 2.60 m in plan and 2.65 m to the roof, sized from the photographs against the passengers (approx.). Two track ropes 0.8 m apart (approx.), the haul rope under the carriage.
