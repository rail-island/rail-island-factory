## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - ja.wikipedia "近鉄80000系電車" (after Tetsudo Fan 2020-04): length over couplers 21,600 mm (end car) and 20,500 mm (intermediate), bodies 21,350 / 20,000 mm, body width 2,790 mm, roof 3,740 mm, floor 1,140 mm and the end car's raised floor 1,860 mm (720 mm higher, the premium car's high-decker), 130 km/h, from 2020.
- **Paint** - the same article: metallic red #ad2320 under clear coat, premium gold #c29d3b band and logo, semi-transparent black round the windows; the LED headlights two rows of twenty; doors 900 mm clear.
- **Equipment** - single-arm pantographs PT-7126A, one on each motor car (the intermediate cars); bolsterless bogies KD-323 / KD-323A.
- **Gauge** - the prototype runs on Kintetsu's 1,435 mm Osaka and Nagoya lines, so its standard-gauge wheelsets are real.
