## Published figures

- Tokyu 300 (Wikipedia "Tokyu 300 series" and the operators' published data): in service from 1999; 24.9 m long over the bodies in 2 sections, 2.5 m wide, 3.7 m to the roof (approx.); 120 passengers with standing room (approx.); 40 km/h, 240 kW, 32 t (approx.); track gauge 1372 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
