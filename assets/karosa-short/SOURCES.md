## Dimensions

From the Czech Wikipedia infoboxes (all two-axle, high floor, engine lying behind the rear
axle in the rear overhang, driving the rear axle; all doors on the right side). Width
2500 mm and height 3165 mm for every type.

| Type | Length (mm) | Wheelbase (mm) | Front / rear overhang (mm) | Doors | Built | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B 731 | 11 055 (11 347 with the long rear) | 5 600 | 2 290 / 3 085 | 3 (2-2-2, front one narrower), double-leaf outward-swinging | 1981/82-1996, 4 909 | city, automatic gearbox; .1667/.1669 from 1994 have the longer 900-series rear |
| B 732 | 11 055 / 11 347 | 5 600 | 2 290 / 3 085 | 3 (2-2-2, front narrower) | 1983-1997, 4 495 | as B 731 but manual gearbox |
| C 734 | 11 055 | 5 600 | 2 290 / 3 085 | 2 (2-2-0): front ahead of the front axle, one in the middle | 1975-1997 (series from 1981), 18 079 | suburban, 45 seats |
| LC 735 | 11 055 | 5 600 | 2 290 / 3 085 | 2: double-leaf ahead of the front axle, single-leaf behind the rear axle (2-0-1) | 1977-1991 (series 1983-1991), 6 519 | coach, 44 seats, about 5 m3 luggage hold between the axles |
| B 931 | 11 345 | 5 600 | 2 355 / 3 390 | 3, double-leaf outward-swinging | 1995 (prototype B19), series 1996-1998 | 900-series front and longer rear; windows in rubber |
| B 931 E | 11 345 | 5 600 | 2 355 / 3 390 | 3 | 1999-2002 (338 B 931 + B 931 E) | floor lowered by 100 mm in the front part, Skoda rigid front axle with disc brakes |
| B 951 / B 951 E | 11 320 | 5 600 | 2 355 / 3 365 | 3 (front narrower) | 2001/02-2007, 182 | Iveco Cursor engine behind the rear axle; E (from Oct 2003): slightly modified front, bonded glazing |

Notes: front + wheelbase + rear overhang gives 10 975 mm for the 700 series, 80 mm short of the
quoted 11 055 mm (bumpers are probably not in the overhangs); the 900 series adds up exactly
(11 345 / 11 320 mm). Wheel and tyre sizes are not given by Wikipedia.

## Native vlakosimdb files

`native/bus_kar_b731/`, `native/bus_kar_b732/`, `native/bus_kar_b931/`, `native/bus_kar_b931e/`, `native/bus_kar_b951e/`, `native/bus_kar_c734_1/`, `native/bus_kar_lc735_1/` - vlakosimdb `db/motocar` at commit `b170acf` (`#` in a folder name becomes `_`):
`index.xml`, the model XML as `model.xml` and the original BMPs (10 pixels a metre;
`x_inc` the front, `x_dec` the rear, `z_inc` / `z_dec` the right and left sides,
`y_dec` the underside). The XML gives the shell's outlines; the BMPs the doors,
windows and paint; the photographs and dimensions above everything the textures
cannot resolve (docs/models/buses.md).
