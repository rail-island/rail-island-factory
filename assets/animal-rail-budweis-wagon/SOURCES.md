## Published figures

- Budweis-Linz horse railway (opened 1832, 1106 mm gauge): its main traffic was salt from Gmunden northward and timber, carried in open four-wheel wagons of timber on large wooden spoked wheels with iron tyres, two or more coupled behind one horse; the replica salt wagon at Kerschbaum (photographs) carries salt barrels under a sheet.
- The model (approx., read off the Kerschbaum photographs against the gauge): body 3.24 m long and 1.68 m wide over the stanchions, floor 1.12 m and the side tops 1.6 m above the rail, 3.8 m over the draw hooks; wheels 0.9 m diameter with 12 wooden spokes on a 1.6 m wheelbase; plank sides and ends on outside stanchions, iron top bands and bolts, a lever brake.
- About 1.0 t empty and about 3 t of salt or timber (approx.); its own limit 12 km/h (approx.). The model is drawn empty.
