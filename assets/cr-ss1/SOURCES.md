The modelled unit is SS1 008 (plates "韶山1 008"), preserved at the China Railway Museum, in the green
and yellow livery of the photographs of 2015 and 2017. The model's plates show the digits only ("1008").

## Published figures

From the Chinese Wikipedia article "韶山1型电力机车" (infobox and text) and the English "China Railways SS1":

- length 20,368 mm over the couplers, 19,400 mm over the frame; width 3,106 mm; roof 4,140 mm above the rail;
- Co'Co', two identical three-axle bogies of 4,600 mm wheelbase, 1,250 mm wheels (new);
- 138 t, 23 t axle load; 3,780 kW continuous; 90 km/h maximum (the English article says 95 km/h);
- a pantograph on the roof over each bogie, the main circuit breaker in the middle, braking resistors at both
  ends of the roof; carbon-strip single-arm pantographs from unit 0007 on, replacing the first units' diamonds.

The bogie centres are not published; 10.9 m is read from the side photograph.
