## Photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (`pnpm refs:fetch`), at most 1280 px on the long side.
Running numbers: the `capitole` livery shows 6530 (first series, long horizontal louvres), `maurienne`
6558 (second series, seven plastic panels, as delivered in 1972 with its third-rail shoes, which went in
1976; the photographs show it after its 2000 repaint, without them), `beton` 6568 (third series, seven
stainless panels).

The `beton` livery (only 6512 and 6568 wore it) has no photograph on Commons; it is drawn after the
BB 22200's beton livery, `assets/sncf-bb22200/photo-bb22394-antibes.jpg` (a sister class, used only for
the orange band's course and the dark grey cab sides).

## Published figures

- **Figures** - French Wikipedia, [CC 6500](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/CC_6500) (after Defrance, Redoutey,
  Zalkind and Hérubel): 20.19 m over the buffers, 3.002 m (first series) and 3.066 m wide, 4.30 m high,
  bogie centres 11.99 m, bogie wheelbase 3.216 m, wheels 1,140 mm, 115 - 119 t, 5,900 kW, 200 km/h;
  the side grilles by series; the liveries (Arzens's "coup de soleil" metallic grey with the Capitole red,
  later on beton grey 804; the Maurienne units' dark blue-green 312 with white bands; the beton livery of
  6512 and 6568) and the Maurienne units' third-rail shoes.
