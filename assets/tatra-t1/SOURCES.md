## Published figures

- Tatra T1 (Wikipedia "Tatra T1" and the operators' published data): in service from 1952; 14.5 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.4 m wide, 3.06 m to the roof (approx.); 95 passengers with standing room (approx.); 60 km/h, 160 kW, 15.8 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
