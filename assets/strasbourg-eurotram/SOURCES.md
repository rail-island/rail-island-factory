## Published figures

- Strasbourg Eurotram (Wikipedia "Eurotram" and the operators' published data): in service from 1994; 36.5 m long over the bodies in 7 sections, 2.4 m wide, 3.4 m to the roof (approx.); 210 passengers with standing room (approx.); 70 km/h, 600 kW, 40 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
