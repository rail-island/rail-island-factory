## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "Paddle steamer" and the photographs: a Swiss lake paddle steamer of the Stadt Luzern type (1928) (approx.): 62.5 m long, 14.6 m over the paddle boxes (7.5 m hull), 1.5 m draught, 1,200 passengers, 900 kW steam engine, 30 km/h.
- From German Wikipedia "Stadt Luzern (Schiff, 1928)": built by Gebrueder Sachsenberg, Rosslau; 63.5 m long, 7.8 m beam (15.2 m over the paddle boxes), 1.6 m draught, 415 t displacement, 1,600 PS, 1,200 passengers. The model takes these figures.
- Measured off `photo-dampfschiff-stadt-luzern-1928.jpg` (starboard side, bow right, about 21 px/m, approx.): the shaft and the arched fanlight at 0.41 L from the stern (u 26 m), the funnel over it; upper saloon u 12-23.5 m under a flat roof reaching aft to the stern; bridge-deck house u 27-34.5 m with the wheelhouse u 30-34 m on it; promenade deck forward to u 54.5 m; mainmast at u 13 m and foremast at u 39 m, both raked aft; main deck about 2.4 m, promenade deck 5.0 m, wheelhouse roof 11 m and funnel top 15 m above the water.
- From `photo-ds-stadt-luzern02.jpg`: the paddle boxes are closed in the sponson houses, flush with the hull's white side, with a black rubbing strake at main-deck height; the funnel is white with a black top and red-mouthed cowls; the boot-topping is red-brown, not green.
