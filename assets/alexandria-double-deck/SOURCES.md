## Published figures

- Alexandria Double-Deck Tram (Wikipedia "Trams in Alexandria" and the operators' published data): in service from 1995; 13 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.4 m wide, 4.6 m to the roof (approx.); 200 passengers with standing room (approx.); 50 km/h, 200 kW, 22 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
