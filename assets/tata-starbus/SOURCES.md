## Published figures

- Tata Starbus: in service from 2007; 11 m long, 2.6 m wide, 3.2 m high; 2 axles, at 2.2, 7.8 m from the nose (approx., read off the photographs where not published); 2 doors on the modelled side; about 70 passengers (approx.); 10.5 t empty, 132 kW, 80 km/h (approx.); liveries or colours: BMTC, BEST.
