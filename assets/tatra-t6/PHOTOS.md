One model (vlakosimdb tram_ckd_t6). The photograph of 8745 whose number the model carries could not be downloaded (Commons rate limit); its page is https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tatra_T6A5,_-8745,_DP_Praha_(28134245817).jpg .
