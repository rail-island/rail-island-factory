## Figures

- Car length 23 m over the couplers, width 3.24 m, height about 4.0 m (ICF; en.wikipedia "Vande Bharat Express").
- Wheels 952 mm; bogie centres 14.9 m (LHB-derived bogies); the end car's leading bogie under the crew door.
- Pantographs on the intermediate trailer cars that carry the transformers (DTC-MC-TC-MC units), never on
  the driving cars: in the 8-car train the third and the sixth car.
- Running numbers: train numbers 22436 (Varanasi - New Delhi, white and blue) and 20901 (saffron), unchanged.

## Notes

- No orthographic side photograph of the end car was found: the nose's profile comes from the Lonavala
  photo (taken at a slight angle; lengths about 10 % uncertain), checked against the three-quarter views.
