## Published figures

- Compagnie generale des omnibus (CGO), Paris (French Wikipedia "Compagnie generale des omnibus", "Omnibus hippomobile"): the company from 1855; the three-horse omnibus a imperiale of 40 places (14 inside, the rest on the platform and the imperiale) from the 1870s until the last horse bus in 1913; about 2.6 t empty (approx.); the imperiale reached by a spiral stair from the rear platform, the driver on a high seat at the front.
- Read off the Atget photograph (place Saint-Sulpice, about 1898) and the CGO card: rear wheels about 1.5 m with 16 spokes, front wheels about 1.0 m, saloon of six windows a side about 3.3 m long, rear platform about 1 m, saloon roof about 2.5 m, the imperiale's railing about 3.4 m with the route boards over it (approx.).
- Estimates (approx.): the model is 5.35 m long over the platform and the dash, 2.0 m wide, 3.6 m high to the route boards; chocolate body with yellow panels and cream letterboards.
