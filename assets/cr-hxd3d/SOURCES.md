## Published figures

From the Chinese and English Wikipedia articles (China Railways HXD3D, 和谐3D型电力机车), citing CRRC Dalian:
22,989 mm over the couplers, 3,100 mm wide, 4,100 mm high, 126 t, 21 t axle load, Co'Co', bogie
wheelbase 2,350 + 2,000 mm, 1,250 mm new wheels, 7,200 kW, 160 km/h. No published bogie-centre distance
was found; 11.4 m is measured on the 0631 photograph. The livery ("red orangutan", designed by Tricon
Design AG) is the production scheme shown by every photograph from 2015 to 2024.
