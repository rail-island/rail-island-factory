## Published figures

- Length over buffers 20.38 m, width 3.0 m, 90 t (dual-voltage), 200 km/h in service and 220 km/h approved, 75 built 1991 - 1993 (es.wikipedia, Serie 252 de Renfe).
- Bogie centres 10.5 m, bogie wheelbase 3.0 m (de.wikipedia, CP-Baureihe 5600, the sister class); wheels 1.25 m (Eurosprinter, as the DB 120).
- Running numbers shown: 252-003-9 (AVE) and 252-024-5 (Renfe Mercancias), both photographed; check digits verified.
