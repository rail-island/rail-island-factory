## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "San Francisco cable car system": Powell cars 27 ft 6 in long, 8 ft wide, 10 ft 3 in high, about 15,500 lb, 60 passengers, 9.5 mph (the cable's speed); service since 1873, the Powell lines since 1888. California Street cars 30 ft 3 in long, double-ended.
