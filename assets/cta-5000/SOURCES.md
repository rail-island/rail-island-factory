## Published figures

- **Dimensions** - Wikipedia "5000-series (CTA)": 48 ft long, 9 ft 4 in wide, 12 ft high, married pairs,
  2 x 2 doors per car, stainless steel with fibreglass end bonnets, 600 V DC third rail with contact shoes,
  built 2009 - 2015.

## Measurements

- **Face** (Grand, 211 px/m from the 12 ft roof and the end door's sill at 1.12 m): face 2.6 m wide (the
  bonnet inset from the 2.84 m body); windscreens 2.0 - 3.12 m high, z 0.57 - 1.19 and -0.45 - -1.19 (the
  operator's one wider); end door 0.68 m wide, its window 2.25 - 3.0 m; LED sign 3.29 - 3.5 m, 0.56 m wide;
  four marker lamps a side at 3.36 m, z 0.64 - 1.09 (white, blue, amber, red outward); head and tail lamps
  at 1.68 m, z 0.72 and 1.0.
- **Side** (projective fits over the doors' and the car's ends on the Fullerton and Randolph photographs,
  d from the cab end of the 14.13 m body): cab window 0.24 - 0.86; left side a wide window 1.26 - 2.36,
  right side a narrow dark one 1.25 - 1.92 and a slot; doors centred 3.15 m either side of the middle,
  1.27 m wide; three 1.04 m windows at 1.18 m between them; at the plain end a 0.98 m window 1.62 - 2.6 m
  and a narrow one 0.68 - 1.29 m from the end. Windows 1.95 - 3.0 m, doors 1.1 - 3.05 m, the eave 3.2 m.
- **Trucks** - the front axle about 0.9 m behind the face on the Fullerton photograph: truck centres
  33 ft 6 in (estimated), 7 ft wheelbase, 28 in wheels.
