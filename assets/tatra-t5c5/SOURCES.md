## Published figures

- Tatra T5C5 (Wikipedia "Tatra T5C5" and the operators' published data): in service from 1979; 14.7 m long over the bodies in 1 section, 2.5 m wide, 3.15 m to the roof (approx.); 116 passengers with standing room (approx.); 65 km/h, 176 kW, 18 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
- Estimates (approx.): the section lengths, door and truck positions, window and floor heights and the colours are read off the photographs; the model rides on standard-gauge wheelsets (docs/models/loading-gauge.md).
