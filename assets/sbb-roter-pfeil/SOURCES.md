The model is the RCe 2/4 of 1947 - 1956: the CLe 2/4 body of the drawing with the buffers and the hook added from 1944
(22.4 m over the buffers). Its running number, RCe 2/4 604 (the CLe 2/4 204), is the unit of the SBB Historic photograph
F 112 00049 040 (Commons, "Rce 2 4 604 (Roter Pfeil) Unfall in Cadenazzo"); the side shows it as "RCe 604", the font having no slash.
