## Published figures

- From Wikipedia "Sleipnir (ship)" and the photographs: Sleipnir (2019), the largest semi-submersible crane vessel: 220 m long, 102 m beam, 49.5 m depth, 12 m transit draught (32 m operating), two 10,000 t cranes, diesel-electric 96,000 kW, 10 knots (19 km/h); displacement about 273,000 t at operating draught (approx.).
- Read off the photographs (approx.): deck box 13 m deep (36.5-49.5 m above the keel), the pontoons' top 1 m above the transit waterline, the open gap 23.5 m; crane houses about 38 x 30 x 18 m on their slewing tubs, A-frame cap about 90 m above the deck, booms 144 m; the funnel casings about 31 m above the deck; the accommodation 7 decks (about 22 m) with its radar mast to about 38 m.
