## Published figures

- CS Cable Innovator (IMO 9101132, built 1995): 145.5 m over all, 24 m beam; the hull 133 m to the transom, the three stern sheaves in their fairings reaching about 12.5 m beyond it (photo, approx.); freeboard to the weather deck about 14.5 m at a 7.5 m draught (photos, approx.).
- The game figures (displacement, power, speed) are kept from the first entry: a typical 2015 cable-laying ship (approx.): 140 m long, 30 m beam, 7 m draught, a 7,000 t cable carousel, an A-frame and stern sheaves, diesel-electric 12,000 kW, 13 knots (24 km/h).
