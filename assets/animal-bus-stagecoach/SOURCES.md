## Published figures

- Abbot-Downing Concord coach (Wikipedia "Concord coach", the Wells Fargo museum's coach No. 251): built in Concord, New Hampshire, from 1827; nine passengers inside on three benches and up to a dozen on the roof; about 2,500 lb (1.1 t) empty; the body slung on two leather thoroughbraces between iron jacks; pulled by four or six horses on mail routes, a pair on short stages; about 8 ft 6 in (2.6 m) high, about 5 ft (1.5 m) wide over the body (approx.).
- Read off the photographs: rear wheels about 1.55 m (61 in), front about 1.24 m, 14 and 12 spokes; the body about 2.45 m long, its bottom about 1 m above the road; front and rear leather-covered boots; red body with gold lining and lettering, yellow running gear (approx.).
- Estimates (approx.): the model is 4.2 m long over the boot and the footboard, 1.8 m wide over the hubs, 2.6 m high to the roof rail (2.8 m to the luggage); its own top speed 20 km/h.
