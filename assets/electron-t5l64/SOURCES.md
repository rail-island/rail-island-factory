- Electron T5L64 (Wikipedia "Electron T5L64" and the operators' published data): in service from 2014; 26.4 m long over the bodies in 5 sections, 2.5 m wide, 3.5 m to the roof (approx.); 200 passengers with standing room (approx.); 65 km/h, 360 kW, 30 t (approx.); track gauge 1435 mm.
